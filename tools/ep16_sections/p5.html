20. Case study: reading one child's chart {{t:01:42:26}}

On request the speaker takes a sample chart for about ten minutes. It is a teaching example of how he reads a chart with several planets in one house, not a real person's forecast. He warns that it comes out khichdi-like because the lagna sits at 9 degrees and more than one lagna is "working". {{t:01:42:26}}

Neighbour lagnas

A lagna of 9 to 13 degrees can have the previous and next sign working too. If the degree is 15 to 16 or more, the next sign (2) also works. If it is within 7 or 8 degrees of the start, the previous one works. {{t:01:43:28}}

Birth time

He calls it foolish to insist on the exact minute. Mother says "ten minutes to go", father says "ten minutes past". The previous and next lagna will both work. A baby is not born in one second, and a nurse may note 11:35 where it was 11:32. {{t:01:45:03}}
