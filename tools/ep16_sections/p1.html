1. Ask big, do not beg: the Sun is self-respect {{t:00:00:33}}

This is the second class on the Sun. It was prompted by a cartoon reel the speaker saw: a small girl, tired of her parents' lectures, tells God "give me bigger problems": where to park a private plane, which country to shop in. His point: if you ask the Sun for something, ask big. Even the beggar should be a top one. {{t:00:00:33}}

The Sun is life

All plants grow from sunlight, so we are really eating sunlight. It is our energy and our vitamin D. If you must pray, pray to the Sun. {{t:00:01:03}}

Begging

A person with a weak Sun begs: for love, for money. He says this is a Saturn-afflicted Sun, since Saturn makes a slave and a beggar. {{t:00:02:06}}

Remedy

Give yourself respect. Be an equal. Ram called Hanuman brother and Krishna called Arjuna brother. Only an equal is taken into the lap. {{t:00:02:06}}

  • Lagna is "I myself". The 1st house is the self, and the Sun is exalted in the lagna in his reading. To make the Sun strong wherever it sits in the chart, understand that power is life. When the soul leaves a dying person, the Sun has left the body. {{t:00:02:38}}
  • Technical terms: atmakaraka, amatyakaraka, bhratrikaraka. In the Jaimini Sutras, if your Sun leaves the body you are dead. In Latin the Sun is SOL, and with "U" added it becomes SOUL. {{t:00:03:09}}
  • If you never gave yourself respect, your lagna becomes weak. The life energy leaks out, you feel dead and burnt out, with no wish to do anything. The Sun and Moon go together: the Moon (mind, play, light) takes its light from the Sun. If the soul goes, Pluto (Yamaraj) takes it. {{t:00:03:40}}
  • A person who touches others' feet and begs weakens his Sun. Life energy drops and old age comes sooner. Anti-ageing and growing wealth come when you respect yourself. You respect your car and yourself. People curse the car and kick the door. {{t:00:04:11}}

The Latin SOL/SOUL point and the speaker's wordplay are his own illustration.

2. A broken Sun kills Venus first {{t:00:04:11}}

The Sun is a giving father

The Sun is the father who leaves something for his child and takes nothing back. A king. When sons give, they play Saturn, and Saturn gives in strange ways. {{t:00:04:11}}

Cause

You did not respect yourself, you went down, the Sun is corrupted. A corrupted Sun first hits Venus: the fun, comfort and love in life. {{t:00:04:42}}

Why Venus

Venus sits next to Earth and is the hottest planet (about 471 degrees C). It traps the Sun's heat. The Sun resents the energy Venus took. {{t:00:05:13}}

  • Venus's guru Shukracharya had the mrit sanjivani, the art of reviving the dead. Venus and Varuna live near the heart. Doctors restart a heart with a current: fire (Mars) plus the Sun switch on, and the person lives. To live, Venus must keep running. {{t:00:05:13}}
  • Venus holds all splendour, fun, servants, money and name. Venus is the feminine form and the thing of enjoyment. When Venus is damaged: tension with the wife, fights with a girlfriend over a phone, a daughter who is upset, a house help who is troubled. All of it, he says, is a Sun fault. {{t:00:05:46}}
  • If the maid is a problem, your Sun is down. You have been cursing something. Your splendour, your personal life, gynaecological problems begin. A woman who curses her life and says "I will live like this or that" has her Venus start to fall apart. {{t:00:06:17}}
  • He says the daughter of such a mother, or the son of a father who says "I am worth nothing", may go so far as cutting his or her wrists, because Venus does not come. It is for love. Madness for love is Venus's fault, and madness for sex is Indra's. {{t:00:06:49}}
  • Venus is the only planet that shines in the sky for its own reason: the hottest star, and it gives life as well. Once Venus is spoiled it begins to cut life. {{t:00:07:22}}
  • All household fights between husband and wife come from the Jupiter-Venus fault. Jupiter is the Sun's guru but was born of the Sun. Arjuna fights while Krishna is the grace. Ram is doing it and Hanuman actually acts. A house like that loses its blessing, romance and fun end, people grumble, there is theft of money and love, and the physical life ends. {{t:00:07:22}}
  • People who curse themselves pass the deep impact on their children. A person who does not respect himself has a wasted life. {{t:00:07:54}}

The remark on a child cutting wrists is a heavy claim and is the speaker's only. Self-harm has many causes and is not caused by a parent's astrology. If someone you know is at risk, reach out to a doctor or a crisis helpline. The notes do not endorse the cause he gives.

Ask big, as servant or master {{t:00:08:25}}

  • Do not beg God. If you ask, ask strongly, and think "I am the servant, You are the master" or "You are the servant, I am the master". God does not mind. We say "God, give me a car, give me money". He says that is like speaking to your father's servant. {{t:00:07:54}}
  • Discounted Lakshmi. We buy a small Lakshmi statue for 500 rupees, then ask for lakhs from it. A diluted Lakshmi gives diluted money. We beg a vegetable seller for a discount and do not give a rickshaw puller his due. {{t:00:08:25}}
  • You are angering Saturn, and Saturn is the Sun's son and devours the Sun. Respect yourself above all. The lagna must not be weak. The people you give respect to sit higher than you, and then ruin you. {{t:00:08:57}}
  • A Sun placed well makes a king and a giver, one who feeds even strangers. So stop living a discounted life. Say it once or twice and your Sun stays strong. Then all your relations run as you wish, whether they love or hate you, since the Sun drags everything through space. People follow you and you are in your own play. You are not a beggar. That is "Aham Brahmasmi". {{t:00:09:30}}

The remark on Hindu statues and on discount shopping is the speaker's joke and personal view. The notes do not endorse it.

3. The Sun in the 2nd house: wrong charity {{t:00:10:01}}

The sign

The 2nd house is wealth. Sign 2 is Taurus, Venus's sign, so the Sun there is dangerous. A person with it has a huge ego and will not listen to anyone. {{t:00:10:01}}

Problem

A rich man is suddenly hit: a tax raid, a seizure, jail. "We gave for the temple, we opened a school." He says it was the wrong charity. {{t:00:10:32}}

Remedy

Do not give in ignorance. Get knowledge, or a guru, before giving. {{t:00:13:09}}

  • Karna's charity. The Sun in this house is as wealthy and generous as Karna. Karna gave away his earrings and armour and died. Wrong giving wrecks your life: "the axe of Parashurama falls on rich men out of the blue". {{t:00:10:32}}
  • Even Sita thought it necessary to give alms to Ravana, and was abducted. Wrong charity exists. You cannot throw everything away as donation. {{t:00:11:04}}
  • When money piles up and you cannot manage it, you say "let me do some good for the world". He asks: when you had nothing, did you do good? Then you are not the emperor of that lagna Sun. You are broke and cannot hold it. A real rich man comes in a rickshaw and pays the rickshaw man generously. He is rich at heart. {{t:00:11:34}}
  • The 2nd house Sun prompts wrong charity, breaks the family and cuts it. Wrong charity feeds ego. Ego leaving gives joy to the soul, like a thorn leaving the body. The joyful sheath (anandamaya kosha) is not touched until ego ends. Anger shows ego, and anyone who is angry is egoistic. {{t:00:12:04}}
  • Q: Someone who gives a little every day? A little may bring small faults. {{t:00:12:38}}
  • Jupiter in the 4th or 10th. With such a position, if you build a temple or donate to one, you may land on the street and take beatings in court. This is where knowledge and the guru come in. {{t:00:13:09}}

The speaker's comments on charity, tax raids and religious giving are his opinion and are not tax or legal advice.

The beggar and the golden bowl. A beggar went around a railway station rattling a dirty black bowl. People dropped coins. Two months later a passer-by said "give me your bowl and I will give you 10 lakh". The beggar thought him mad. "What will I earn without it?" The man raised it to 1,000, then 2,000 rupees. The beggar took the 2,000 and swore never to come back, thinking of drink and a party. The man, an old goldsmith, put the bowl in his bag. On the train he felt its weight: half a kilo of gold. He had paid almost nothing. {{t:00:13:40}}

  • This is our body (kaya): an invaluable bowl that we use for cowries. We build a house, make four fixed deposits. What should be filled? Knowledge. This is all gold, the Sun, all knowledge. A knowledgeable one has the fun and the rest sit with sour faces. Ego ruined even a warrior like Karna. {{t:00:15:15}}
  • Knowledge makes God take care of you. It is like a parent feeding a child before himself: the parents become servants and we become the masters. "Aham Brahmasmi" means I am nothing in front of you. Then "Tat tvam asi": you are that which I am. He asks if this is double-speak. {{t:00:16:20}}

Bhrigu, Lakshmi and who is a Brahmin {{t:00:20:59}}

  • Shukra is Bhrigu's son. Bhrigu kicked Vishnu's chest and Vishnu asked if his foot was hurt. Lakshmi was angry, and Bhrigu said "you will not come to a Brahmin's house, and I will write the Bhrigu Samhita so that you come and fall at the feet". That is why Lakshmi comes to the feet of the knowing. {{t:00:20:59}}
  • Wise men and babas often have a store of Lakshmi, until they get greedy. Then they are not Brahmins. A Brahmin is one who knows the Brahman. It is a varna (type) and not a caste (jati). The four types are Brahmin, Kshatriya, Vaishya and Shudra. {{t:00:21:33}}
