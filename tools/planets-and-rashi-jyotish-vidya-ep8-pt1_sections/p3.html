8. Mercury, Jupiter and Venus: quick notes and their remedies {{t:01:50:37}}

Mercury's one weak spot

Mercury is called a neutral ("napunsak") planet, good in almost every sign, and is said to be debilitated only in Pisces (12), where it turns into a poisonous cloud, like carbon monoxide from a lit stove: speech turns unclear or harsh. If Ketu and Jupiter (which the speaker says both work on behalf of the 12th house) are otherwise fine in the chart, even this debilitated Mercury can still bring wealth, often through shady or indirect means such as MLM schemes or being the real power behind someone else's public position. {{t:01:51:09}}

Mercury's remedy

Never disrespect a sister, aunt, daughter or sister-in-law (behan, bua, beti, sali): the speaker says they can bring a false court case on you without your noticing how it happened, and this is explicitly pointed at the pattern of false cases filed against men. Also avoid hard bargaining with a doctor or guru over money, and keep your speech calm and silent rather than gossiping or cursing. {{t:01:54:17}}

Serve the "hijde" community

Regular, ongoing giving to the hijda (third-gender) community is named as a direct Mercury remedy, alongside protecting your case in court. {{t:01:55:21}}

Jupiter and the liver

Jupiter is linked to the liver, which the speaker calls the seat of the soul's fire; a disturbed Jupiter shows up as liver trouble (he names fatty liver), and he advises treating the liver before the heart when cholesterol is the real root cause. {{t:00:29:53}}

The guru and the bitter gourd

His teaching story: disciples ask their guru's blessing for a pilgrimage to gain wisdom. The guru sends a bitter gourd (karela) along with them and asks for it back afterward. They return transformed by the trip, but the gourd is still bitter. The guru's point: a thing's nature does not change just by being carried to a holy place; only real inner change, which is Jupiter's gift, actually works. {{t:00:33:35}}

Saturn's yellow oil

Saturn is said to obstruct Jupiter's wisdom. The remedy given is offering yellow mustard oil to Saturn, reasoned through a chain of symbols: Jupiter's colour is yellow, and Mars (fire) purifies Saturn (metal), so burning oil (Mars plus oil) is used to clear the obstruction Saturn places on Jupiter. {{t:00:36:15}}

When Lakshmi "keeps her veil on"

Answering why money will not come despite effort: the speaker says Venus (Lakshmi) is a woman in this image, and a veiled Lakshmi means the household is treating the actual women in it with too much control and conflict for her to want to stay. {{t:02:04:54}}

What triggers it

Constant fighting, giving orders instead of speaking sweetly, an unclean environment (dusty mirrors, a dirty neighbourhood) and not giving a woman at home the freedom to make her own small choices. {{t:02:05:56}}

The fix

Speak sweetly, let her choose things for herself, keep household mirrors and the surrounding area clean, and serve the cow (which the speaker calls a complete form of Venus). {{t:02:06:28}}
