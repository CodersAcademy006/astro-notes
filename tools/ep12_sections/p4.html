9. Do not try to please; set Saturn with a guru {{t:00:51:33}}

Problem

Trouble with the house help, your sons and your workers. You check them, and you try to please them. {{t:00:52:03}}

Cause

In his reading, checking and pleasing a worker in Saturn's area makes them turn against you. A parent who tells a child "we did all this for you" has the child turn against them. He calls this "a law of the cosmos". {{t:00:52:03}}

Remedy

Leave the work to them. Keep a guru, since Saturn lives under the guru. Duty and service (seva) are two different things. Seva means from the heart. {{t:00:52:35}}

  • Do not try to please the maid: you gave her a sari and then forget it. Do not keep saying "I did so much for the company", or your promotion will not come. And if it comes by accident, you will cry blood over it. {{t:00:52:35}}
  • Whatever worker you really leave things to will clean the room and keep your house well. We do not leave. We follow behind: "what are you doing?" {{t:00:53:06}}
  • The mother-in-law and daughter-in-law quarrel is Rahu's fault, with Saturn behind it. The in-laws' side is Rahu, he says, so take note. {{t:00:53:06}}
  • Saturn and Jupiter. They are not enemy planets. Saturn is a "cruel" planet (krur) and has no dealings of enmity. When Saturn gives, it gives like a rocket, even through cruel work. Sharp businessmen who sell goods that cannot be returned rise like rockets because they hold to their rules. {{t:00:53:38}}
  • Duryodhana is firm about himself. He has one-pointed attention and no "if and but", no reverse psychology, and so he gets heaven. The speaker calls God's rules strange. The Pandavas took the good path, and he says people who walk the "good path" dance in ida and pingala and do not activate sushumna. {{t:00:54:09}}

The remarks on Duryodhana and heaven are the speaker's own provocation in class. They are not a claim from a text.
