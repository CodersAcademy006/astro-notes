20. Q&A: stones, planet strength and routine {{t:02:14:11}}

Q: Pandits say strengthen Jupiter, but then more trouble comes.

The question is wrong, he says. A stone does not make a planet strong or weak. It corrects, like glasses: your vision neither grows nor shrinks, it is corrected. {{t:02:14:11}}

His analogy

A thermometer shows 98.4 and does not give you strength. A stone filters the planet's rays in your body to an optimal line. There is over-correction and under-correction. Too much vitamin D harms bones and teeth. {{t:02:14:43}}

Planets

Planets are neither weak nor strong. They are out of proportion: one place weak, another strong. Planets are full. Your receptors take too little or too much, and that is what you fix. Like salt: a pinch or a fistful. {{t:02:16:20}}
