8. What Saturn does to the body {{t:00:42:01}}

Problem

Nerve and bone trouble: weak nerves, paralysis, bad bones, hip bone and every complaint below it, spinal injury and spinal cord problems. {{t:00:42:01}}

Cause

Saturn owns the nerves and the skeleton. Where it sits or looks, that body part is in its dark area. {{t:00:42:01}}

Remedy (speaker's)

Keep Saturn happy. Saturn is the Sun's son, so look after your own sons and workers. Do not drink your sons' blood. {{t:00:42:33}}

  • Eyes. Saturn weakens the optic nerve. He says that if Saturn looks at the 2nd house, the person will have eye trouble, and that a person with one eye affected and not the other is a sign. He names glaucoma as an example, "because Saturn has to black it out". {{t:00:42:01}}
  • How to keep Saturn pleased. Give attention and love to your children and take peace. Do not expect them to serve you. Saturn's existence is because of the father, so a child who does not respect his father harms his own Sun. {{t:00:42:33}}
  • A Sun that is badly placed hits the part of the body it sits in. He lists the Sun's areas: kidney stone, heart attack, bones, eyes. The day's topic is still Saturn. {{t:00:43:05}}

These are the speaker's health claims from chart reading. They are not medical advice, and a doctor should be consulted for any of these conditions.

Saturn linked to the 7th house {{t:00:43:37}}

  • For a boy or girl with Saturn linked to the 7th (sitting there or aspecting it), the typical complaint is "my husband (or wife) does not listen to me". {{t:00:43:37}}
  • His dialogue for the wife: when you say "do the Gita path", he says "I will drink beer". When you say "fine, I will also drink beer", he says "stop this nonsense". Whatever you want, this one wants the opposite. {{t:00:44:08}}
  • Remedy: learn to want the opposite. This is reverse psychology. "How to want" is next week's class. The partner will want what you want. {{t:00:44:08}}
  • Observe a child or a colleague who always goes against you. Do not let "Munna" know how much money is in the pocket (his pet word, and the captions do not make clear who he means). Do not discuss and do not plan too much. Keep him confused, and then he says "papa, let me do it". {{t:00:44:40}}
  • Whatever area you looked into where Saturn sits will fail. Other planets work too, since this is a universal rule that depends on combinations. {{t:00:45:13}}

Why prayer for a job fails in a Saturn area {{t:00:45:13}}

  • You go to an interview saying "O God, give me this job". If Saturn has even a small influence on the 9th house, the job will not come. {{t:00:45:13}}
  • The moment you earn and put the money into a temple or mosque, your days go down. People say "every time I come back from the Mother's court, bad days start". The speaker says the visit is not the problem. They went and asked, "give this, give that", and what you voice, to an idol or to yourself, does not happen. {{t:00:45:44}}
  • The shopkeeper story. You ask Ansari for black dal and he says it comes tomorrow. You go back every day and get into the habit of going. Meanwhile Gupta ji next door got it the first time and never visited the shop again. Those who got it in one ask do not return. The ones who keep going are caught in a hypnosis of asking. {{t:00:46:16}}
  • So why do some never visit a temple and get everything? They are too busy and have no time. The speaker's point is that asking more than once is the problem and not the temple. {{t:00:47:18}}

The speaker mixes religion and chart reading here. His claim that visiting a place of worship leads to bad days if you ask for things is his opinion, not a rule of any faith.
