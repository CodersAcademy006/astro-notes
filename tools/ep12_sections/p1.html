1. Saturn in the body: the rope-pull behind life {{t:00:01:07}}

This class is on Saturn (Shani). The speaker warns that Saturn has a strange nature and that nobody can cover it fully. Next week's class is on how to wish and manifest, and today is the side of luck (naseeb). His starting claim: life is not run by our choices alone. You either have knowledge, or a wise person's grace, or good luck. {{t:00:00:36}}

Where Saturn sits

Pelvic girdle and hip bone, the base (muladhara) chakra, the root. The spine stands on it. Knees and thighs also come under Saturn. {{t:00:02:43}}

What it does

Saturn is the one who gets work done through others. It is the lord of the "devil body" and of darkness. {{t:00:06:55}}

First practical hint

Keep the legs moving. Ageing climbs upward from the feet, so work the feet to stay young. {{t:00:07:56}}

  • Body map. The Sun is at the centre of the solar system and, in the body, at the heart. Mercury and Venus sit near it, Mars at the navel, and Jupiter just below the navel above the water chakra. Below that are the intestines and then the pelvic girdle, which is Saturn's area. {{t:00:02:09}}
  • Behind Saturn stand Indra, Varuna and Yama. These are the next three in his scheme (Uranus, Neptune, Pluto in the speaker's terms, which orbit in about 80, 160 and 250 years). {{t:00:03:15}}
  • Jupiter relates to the gut: when the stomach is upset, it pulls things down and throws them out through the bowels. {{t:00:04:16}}

The tug of war: Sun, Jupiter, Saturn {{t:00:03:45}}

  • The speaker says the Sun is a huge magnet pulling every planet along. Jupiter is the only planet pulling the Sun backward. It is too small to win, but it stops Earth from being dragged into the Sun. That is his reading of "gravitation" as guru-akarshan, the pull of the guru. {{t:00:03:45}}
  • Jupiter also guards the asteroid belt. He links the dinosaur extinction to an asteroid that fell and says Jupiter pulls such things away, as the gut throws waste out. {{t:00:04:16}}
  • Jupiter orbits in 12 years, which is the Kumbh cycle. A kumbh is a pot, and in the body the pot (kund) holds the dormant energy (kundalini). In animals the energy stays pointed down, so they only eat, excrete, mate and breed. In humans it rises. {{t:00:04:47}}
  • Saturn holds Jupiter from behind with its agents Indra, Varuna and Yama, like a game of tug of war: the Sun pulls one way, Saturn the other, and life is the rope in the middle. {{t:00:05:51}}

The astronomy and astrophysics here (gravity as Jupiter's grace, the asteroid belt, the Sun turning into a red ball in lakhs of years) are the speaker's own symbolic reading, not mainstream science. {{t:00:06:23}}

Why Saturn is the god of darkness {{t:00:06:55}}

  • Jupiter is a big planet, and Saturn is small and sits behind it, so the Sun's rays do not reach it. Someone small standing behind someone big is in the dark. That is why Saturn is called the lord of darkness. {{t:00:06:55}}
  • You have a spirit body and also a devil body. One is plus and one is minus, and both are there for your benefit. Asuras and rakshasas are useful too, and the speaker says the devil body is what keeps us running. Saturn is its head. {{t:00:07:26}}
  • The body runs on the hip bone, so the speaker tells you to keep the legs going. Doctors say the same: keep walking. {{t:00:07:26}}
  • Ageing. Even on the deathbed the skull (Sun) keeps working. The feet go first, then the knees, then the hip, then the stomach. Old age climbs upward, so working the feet slows it. {{t:00:07:56}}

2. Saturn works through agents: Rahu, Ketu and the unknown {{t:00:08:29}}

The speaker's picture: the king is the Sun and the queen the Moon. When the king wants something done for the guru, Saturn is the minister who passes the order to its agents, Indra, Varuna, Yama, Rahu and Ketu. Saturn does not do the work. It gets it done. {{t:00:09:00}}

The Ram-Hanuman model

Ram does nothing yet it is Ram's work, because Hanuman acts. Hanuman acts only when Ram is there. In the same way Rahu and Ketu are the ones who do the work for Saturn. {{t:00:09:31}}

The power of the agents

Rahu is the state when the Sun is eclipsed and Ketu when the Moon is eclipsed. So the agents can block even the spirit body's light. {{t:00:10:03}}

The sign of Saturn

You say "I just do not understand what is happening": home fights, a business that does not run, marriage that does not happen. That area is working but your Saturn is not set. {{t:00:10:37}}

  • Saturn is the god of the sudden. A lottery, a sudden illness, a job lost or gained: you were in the dark about it. Whenever you find yourself saying "I cannot understand why", it is the Saturn area. {{t:00:10:37}}
  • Jupiter commands Saturn. When the guru arrives, all servants stand at attention, and even Saturn is quiet in front of Jupiter. Saturn hides behind Jupiter, so the setting of Saturn depends on Jupiter. {{t:00:11:09}}
  • The account of past karma. Our life is half day, half dark. We know a few things and most we do not. The ledger of karma from earlier births is all held by Saturn. The speaker's example is Bhishma, who suffered arrows for a snake he had thrown on thorns a hundred births earlier and did not remember. {{t:00:11:42}}
  • The earth element sits in the muladhara, and he says all the secrets of a birth chart are stuck in the root chakra. {{t:00:12:12}}

How a thought becomes a fate {{t:00:12:45}}

  • A thought comes from above like a spark, from the crown (sahasrara), from a portal we do not know. It drops down and touches Saturn, and a seed is planted. A tree grows from it. {{t:00:13:15}}
  • All stored karma is the sanchit karma, the unopened account. The karma that comes true is whichever "button" you pressed. Sometimes you do not know the good button exists, and a guru presses it: "you have this skill too". {{t:00:13:15}}
  • A marathon coach is the example. The guru sits above Saturn, so Saturn needs him most. Arjuna did not know where to go, and when Krishna took over, the Sun's light switched on. {{t:00:13:46}}

The speaker calls the portal and the seed process his own model of karma. He does not tie it to a named scripture here. {{t:00:13:15}}

3. Choice is the Sun, no-choice is Saturn {{t:00:14:16}}

Problem

Most of us have no real choice in life, and still spend our days trying to understand it. {{t:00:14:16}}

Cause

Where there is choice, the Sun works. Where there is none, Saturn works. When we poke at what Saturn is already doing, it turns against us. {{t:00:14:16}}

Remedy

Let go ("chhodo, chhodo"). The speaker says this advice in his class is to let Saturn become active. Do not drive nails into it. {{t:00:14:48}}

  • Luck is Saturn's. A driver who escapes an accident by chance was saved by Saturn. It works when you do not know. Hand the work to your household worker and then follow behind checking "did you sweep it right?" and he will take revenge, "100 percent", because you will not let him work. {{t:00:15:18}}
  • The ball story. A teacher stood two fighting boys on opposite sides of a ball. One said white, the other said black. They swapped sides and now each said the opposite. The ball was half white and half black. Both were right and both were fighting because their information was different. The speaker says husband and wife, and we and our job, do the same. {{t:00:15:49}}
  • Our car or body does not want to go somewhere, and we force it. Going to a relative's house without wanting to means going "without mind". The body then takes the hit, with a high chance of injury. He says this is because we did not leave it to Saturn. {{t:00:16:52}}
  • The Arjuna image again: when the mind says "this way", Krishna says "quiet, son". {{t:00:16:52}}
  • The lucky ones. You see people who become rich while fools by your standard. A village girl lives like a queen, cannot speak English, but has activated Saturn. A man became a king because he activated Saturn. They have simply left it to Krishna. {{t:00:17:24}}

The speaker says this tongue-in-cheek and mixes luck, karma and character. Treat it as his teaching style and not as a claim that effort has no value. {{t:00:17:24}}

Saturn is the lord of things {{t:00:17:56}}

  • Saturn is earth, the root of the earth, the 12 by 12 plot, destiny that does not change. The earth changes form but returns to earth, and Lakshmi comes out of the earth. Everyone who wants things, wealth and success is looking at Saturn. {{t:00:17:56}}
  • Saturn is the lord of cheezein, things: a car, a bungalow, a house, all of it from land. Knowledge and other givings are other planets. {{t:00:18:27}}
  • Those who do not build a building and live in a hut should just build a house. "Dream big" is next week's class. First understand that you have a planet that can make you the emperor of things while you sit at home. {{t:00:18:27}}
  • An egoist says "I made it". A man of muddy nature may say "He gave it and He took it", and Saturn is very kind to him. A man who says the second also gets Jupiter and Varuna on his side. {{t:00:18:59}}

Ignorance is a blessing {{t:00:19:30}}

  • In the chart Saturn is the blind planet. Some children pass without studying. Some become rich without work. Some girls get a proposal served on a plate, with the job and house also arriving at home. {{t:00:19:30}}
  • It happens to those who have recognised their Saturn, knowingly, unknowingly or through a guru's grace. "Ignorance is a bliss." {{t:00:20:01}}
  • You work in a top company at a crore a year, and the one paying you is less educated. "Allah is kind to him, so a donkey becomes a wrestler." {{t:00:20:01}}
  • A person who gives everyone advice is not a donkey, the speaker says. People who come to him become kings and he stays where he is. A king needs a minister, but the minister is the clever one and the king the less clever. {{t:00:20:31}}
  • Ego is the trigger. The moment ego comes in, Jupiter digs your grave and sends you down. No one can speak back in front of Jupiter, because it is survival. {{t:00:20:31}}

4. Saturn's pace, sight and U-turns {{t:00:21:03}}

Pace

Saturn is "Shani-char", the slow walker. It takes about 30 years around the 12 signs, 2.5 years per sign. {{t:00:21:03}}

Rule of signs

Main lord of sign 10 (Capricorn) and the weaker lord of sign 11 (Aquarius, the pot). {{t:00:21:33}}

Aspect

Saturn aspects the 3rd, 7th and 10th from where it sits, with a crooked, indirect sight. {{t:00:23:36}}

  • Sign 10 is Makar Sankranti. The Sun turns upward there, and "Saturn's time is gone". The god-sleep months end, the Pausha month passes, ancestors have been honoured. Bhishma waited for the northern course (uttarayana) to leave, since he did not want to go in Saturn's time, when Yama's world would take him. {{t:00:21:33}}
  • The outer planets in his scheme take about 80, 160 and 250 years (Uranus, Neptune, Pluto). Saturn at 30 years is the one visible in a lifetime. {{t:00:22:04}}
  • Everything slow is Saturn's. Court cases, illness, house hunting, waiting to become rich. Things drag. {{t:00:22:35}}
  • But Saturn is also the emperor of luck. When it gives, it gives by chance and surprise: "oh, we never thought of this". Otherwise it drags and rubs like rust on iron. The illness you are desperate to cure may grow instead. {{t:00:23:06}}
  • Prarabdha. Your fate is stored with Saturn, who is the Sun's son. It is powerful, but Jupiter stands in front of it, so it looks crookedly. This is why Saturn's sight is called "crooked". It always works from four places in the chart, even if you do nothing: where it sits and the places it aspects. {{t:00:23:36}}
  • Retrograde. Saturn runs backward (vakri) several times in a 2.5 year stretch. He says it cannot be 20 times, it is four or five times. So good days can turn upside down suddenly, and love can be snatched away as suddenly as it came. {{t:00:24:11}}
  • He calls Saturn the lord of darkness and of justice, brother of Yamaraj. {{t:00:24:42}}
