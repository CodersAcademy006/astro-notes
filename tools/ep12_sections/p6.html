15. Serve the blind, give in secret, let go {{t:01:27:03}}

Problem

Blindness in life: you cannot see what to do, where to go, what not to do. {{t:01:27:03}}

Cause

Saturn's darkness is on that area. {{t:01:27:03}}

Remedy

Serve blind people. Feed a blind person. Go to a school for the blind and give food, the simplest thing. {{t:01:27:03}}

  • Match the item to the sign. A blind school is the 5th house. If you give an item opposite to the sign sitting there, it harms. If it goes with the sign, it is very good. Without knowing this, the person who does a lot of charity is the unhappy one. {{t:01:27:34}}
  • The happy one does nothing: gives nothing in temples or to the poor, picks no fights, leaves the planet alone, lives by plain consciousness. That is a wonderful life. Or someone who gave a lot and never found out where or who: no fault attaches. {{t:01:28:05}}
  • If you say "we fed 200 people at the temple today", it all becomes known. If your planet is fine, good. If not, it will wreck you. So do anything, stay quiet, keep it dark. Only gain follows. {{t:01:28:05}}
  • Mixed small habits he lists. Do not worship Saturn out of doubt, keep your hair and beard tidy, do not litter, do not throw nail clippings (Rahu), keep your head covered, hold your tongue (Ketu). He says nothing then goes wrong. {{t:01:28:35}}
  • The Ramdev camp example. For a month people follow the strict routine and are cured, then see samosas and kachoris and fall ill. Who wants to be cured by a free remedy? He says we want to eat everything, party, and take medicine. {{t:01:29:07}}
  • Who is blind? Not only those without eyes. Those who do not know are blind. People come to the guru saying "show me the way". Saturn follows the guru in space. A "blind" person who tries to teach the guru gets thrown far. {{t:01:29:39}}
  • Help people without teaching them: cross a small child over the road without caring whether he learns. That means your Saturn is set. {{t:01:30:40}}
  • He says blind people rarely have accidents and walk with a stick, and puts it down to a sixth sense and Saturn's grace. Blind boys and girls live well. {{t:01:31:11}}

The statement that blind people do not suffer accidents is his anecdotal remark. It is not a fact the notes endorse.

West, the giver of things {{t:01:31:11}}

  • The west is Saturn's direction. In the house it is the zone of Pushpadanta and Varuna, where the dining and kitchen waste goes in his example. At every temple or place of worship the Lord's place is this one, and bowing there is enough. {{t:01:31:11}}
  • Saturn is the giver of everything: house, car, bungalow. If you cannot rise above a one-room home, are in poverty, cannot pay rent, and live on loans, your Saturn is angry. {{t:01:31:42}}
  • Saturn turns straight when Jupiter commands "do good work, son". Saturn gives full justice when the guru is with him, and the guru is with him when Ketu stops the foolish talk. So give up pointless advice, stories, boasting and ego. {{t:01:32:13}}
  • Saturn is the buffalo, the black colour, the black idol and the iron figure people carry around. {{t:01:32:13}}

Tula daan and "blind donation" {{t:01:32:46}}

  • Tula daan on Saturday (donation by weight). For any physical problem or an illness no one can diagnose, the speaker says tula daan sets Saturn. {{t:01:32:46}}
  • Blind donation (andha daan). If you see no path ahead, give with your own hand to someone, and do not know who got it. Saturn makes you rich in return, he says. {{t:01:32:46}}
  • Hold the line: do not light a torch for others and teach all your life. Wife teaches husband, father teaches son, mother teaches sister. You are destroying your own Saturn. Say "roll or do not roll, it is your choice". {{t:01:33:18}}
  • The attitude he asks for is beparwah, carefree: leave it to God as a blind man walks with a stick. {{t:01:33:48}}
  • Mustard oil on Saturn's idol. It works with no particular feeling, he says. In a gurudwara too the lamp works. It works best when you go to Baba Nanak and say "I have left it to you". {{t:01:33:48}}

The claims about donation and oil are traditional folk remedies as he states them. The notes do not endorse them, and nothing here replaces medical care.

Memory, forgiveness and the goal {{t:01:34:19}}

  • Saturn is the god of memory. All past-life karma is in its memory. The old fire flares if someone seems like an enemy, and when you cannot forgive, Varuna gets angry. {{t:01:34:19}}
  • That gives calf trouble, leg-vein problems, and Varuna's place is the heart. He adds that Saturn is exalted in sign 7, at Varuna's place in the womb of the sea, where the whole earth is hidden and Lakshmi comes out from under the earth. {{t:01:34:50}}
  • Set a goal, then leave it to Krishna. "Krishna" is anyone you can hand it to. The word lakshya (goal) and Lakshmi are linked in his telling. Make the goal once. {{t:01:35:22}}
  • On how many affirmations: say it once and leave it, and activate Saturn. Saturn is the main worker with plenty of labour, skilled and unskilled. Tell him "the guru is coming, keep the palace set", and do not follow him with a stick. {{t:01:35:22}}
  • If a client or a call does not come, "it will come, we will see". If you start asking when, the call will not come. {{t:01:35:54}}

16. Marriage, children and the seed {{t:01:35:54}}

Marriage

You cannot hold a choice for your spouse. Girls and boys who have looked at a photo, said "yes, this seems fine", and still no marriage happens. {{t:01:35:54}}

Cause

You pray "give me that one". Saturn does not give choice. Even sinners get what they want, he says, because they do not harass God. {{t:01:36:57}}

Remedy

Do not set a choice. Do not make demands. Think of it as digging into the earth: hidden, you do not know what comes out. {{t:01:37:28}}

  • If you insist on your choice you may sit at home until you are 40 or 50. {{t:01:36:26}}
  • God is the society of the five elements: earth, sky, air, fire, water. Earth is the first element and is Saturn. {{t:01:36:57}}
  • Children. Do not pray and beg for children. People ask for 10 years, tire, and say "I leave it to you". Then it happens. Reports are fine, the 5th house is fine, the egg and sperm count are fine, they are trying, and it does not happen. Why? Because they announced it ten times. {{t:01:37:28}}
  • The son craze. Parents want a son and get a row of daughters. He claims the unwanted daughters' lives are also spoiled, the ancestors stay displeased, and the son later beats you. {{t:01:37:59}}
  • If you want a child through greed (Indra's greed), he says the child will become an Indra, asking for apsaras. {{t:01:38:31}}
  • The seed. Your starting consciousness is the seed: mango, banana or bitter gourd. A house, spouse and in-laws come like that seed. Seed is hidden in the womb of the earth. Keep wealth, love, peace and mind hidden. {{t:01:38:31}}
  • First signs of Saturn gone off: a hidden pain, stretching pain in nerves, back and below-waist trouble. Keep yourself healthy. {{t:01:39:02}}
  • Black dog. People keep a black dog, and he says some who could not get children have conceived after keeping one. He calls it a strange tota (folk trick), a Ketu thing, and black brings in Saturn. {{t:01:39:34}}
  • Home check for Saturn. No rusty metal goods. No spilled oil anywhere. No corner of iron, wood, rubbish or junk. All storage that is badly kept belongs to Saturn. {{t:01:39:34}}
  • Do not beg God for admission to a particular school or college. It will not happen in your chosen one. So learn to let go: this is Saturn's power. {{t:01:40:06}}

The son preference, the claim that daughters are neglected, the black dog totka and the fertility remarks are the speaker's views or folk beliefs. The notes record them and do not endorse them. Parents who need help conceiving should see a doctor.
