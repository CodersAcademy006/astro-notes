17. Sade sati and dhaiya {{t:01:40:06}}

A viewer asks about sade sati, Saturn's seven-and-a-half year period. The speaker's answer: it is counted from the Moon sign of your birth, not from the Sun sign, and it is much feared and over-hyped. He says the chart has to be worked out first, and that this class only gives the idea. {{t:01:40:06}}

How it runs

Saturn spends 2.5 years in each sign. Sade sati starts when it enters the sign before your Moon sign, goes through your Moon sign and then the next one. Each stretch is a "dhaiya" of 2.5 years. {{t:01:42:45}}

What it does

Saturn is dark oil poured on the Moon, which is water. It spreads darkness over the mind. {{t:01:42:12}}

Remedy

Have a guru. Jupiter is exalted in the Moon's sign (Cancer, sign 4), so a guru calms it. Go to a gurudwara if you have no guru, or hang a guru's photo. {{t:01:42:12}}

  • Name sign. He says the sign of your name's first syllable is another thing to check. In his example, a child with Moon in sign 4 was named from a syllable that activated the wrong sign, and he advises changing it. He compares the first letters (for example for signs 1, 2, 3 and 5) and does not give a complete table. {{t:01:40:38}}
  • Saturn moving through the signs: the first dhaiya begins when it reaches the sign before the Moon sign. The second dhaiya is when it sits on the Moon sign (his example sign 4). The third is the "going" dhaiya when it reaches the next sign. When Saturn is retrograde on the Moon, it hits hard. {{t:01:41:41}}
  • If a person's Moon is placed in kind signs, the three signs may be fine (good, friendly, a little spoiled), and only the "going" stretch troubles. {{t:01:43:17}}
  • Same sade sati, opposite result. Rama and Ravana had the dhaiya together. Krishna and Kansa too. The results were opposite. So it does not work from Saturn's sign alone but from the birth chart: whether the Moon has moved to another sign, which sign is activated, and so on. "It is a big khichdi." {{t:01:43:49}}
  • It is bad press. Saturn entering the 12th sign does not mean everyone in Pisces or Aquarius cries. Millions have it at once. {{t:01:44:52}}

Name-syllable corrections and the Rama/Ravana comparison are the speaker's way of explaining. He does not give a full method for name change, and these notes do not either.

A trained astrologer's boundaries {{t:01:44:52}}

  • People "drink the blood" of the knowledgeable pandit, who is busy and has many followers. He refunded a fee to a client who demanded he explain why the chart showed ancestor trouble (pitru dosh). His line: if you want a class, join a class. The ancestor class would take two and a half to three hours. {{t:01:45:22}}
  • Whatever the issue, Saturn can only be turned on when Jupiter is on. Saturn unleashes its cruelty and bad results only in front of Jupiter. So make anyone your guru: father, mother, anyone. You just need one. {{t:01:45:53}}
  • During a dhaiya, take the Sun with you: confidence and brightness. Then Saturn does not spoil anything. It keeps giving: "I am serving, sir". If you trouble Saturn, it brings out Rahu's results. {{t:01:46:25}}

Secrecy, with a story {{t:01:46:57}}

  • If you tell, you get a quarrel. If you keep the relationship dark, you get partnership without needing to talk. Even if you made a mistake or an improper relationship, keep it hidden. {{t:01:46:57}}
  • A young woman came to him about ten years back saying she would marry only a man she could tell about her past illness. He said: why tell, you will ruin yourself. She came again recently. Seven years married, and the husband, now preparing for divorce, says "you were ill before". His line to her: you wanted to be truthful and put up a poster. Forget the hidden past. {{t:01:47:28}}

This is the speaker's advice on keeping personal history private in a marriage. It is his opinion, and honesty about health in a marriage is a matter for the people involved and for professional advice.

When Saturn beats you {{t:01:47:58}}

  • The common confusion: Saturn, the god of justice, begins correcting. Love goes, the relationship goes dark, and you do not know what will happen. If you worry, it breaks bones and makes you poor. {{t:01:47:58}}
  • His answer: no tension. Next time do not celebrate, do not hope, do not tell. Peace and no joy-making. Then Saturn stays set and gives a lot. {{t:01:48:30}}
  • Saturn's trade-off. It gives money but not enjoyment. It gives sex but not love, or love but not sex. It gives property but not enjoyment, or enjoyment but not property. If you want all, stop trying to find out. Do not brood or fuss. Do not say again and again "I want this". {{t:01:48:30}}
  • If you still burn with desire, wear a strong stone. But if you offer Saturn oil every day and Jupiter is not set, the oil blocks and spoils Saturn. You can stay poor despite worship. Worship done right fixes a person. People do it wrong because they copy: one did it, so all do. {{t:01:49:01}}

18. Worked example: reading one chart {{t:01:49:32}}

The speaker works through one sample chart (his "Munna Bhai" chart) on the board for about fifteen minutes. He first jokes that he does not know whether he is joking or serious, and says there is a lot of homework behind it. It is a demonstration of method. The numbers are sign numbers, 1 to 12. {{t:01:49:32}}

  • Setup. The lagna (ascendant) is sign 2 at 24 degrees. Because of the degree, the next signs 3 to 10 also get "activated" in different ways. Sun and Mercury share sign 2, which at that degree is "not great", yet with the sign 3 activation they form a Budha-Aditya combination, "wonderful". He says this comes by experience. {{t:01:50:02}}
  • In the chalit chart, no planet has shifted, so it is the same as the lagna chart. The KP chart shifts several: Rahu and Neptune come to one sign, Uranus moves, and Sun, Ketu and Mercury move. {{t:01:51:35}}
  • Speech and authority. Mercury in sign 3 gives powerful speech, and the Sun with it gives authority, whether the person succeeds or fails. {{t:01:52:06}}
  • Wife. Venus sits with Saturn, so the wife is fully with him, but Venus is disturbed, so there will be constant quarrelling. Saturn still helps fully. {{t:01:52:36}}
  • Pluto in sign 6 (Mercury's sign) is superb and acts like an exalted placement. The person will run a "dictatorship" like Dharmaraj: this is right, this is wrong, do it this way. It is justified since the 5th house is strong and the ancestors are kind. Multiple successes and a sharp "eagle eye" for distance. {{t:01:53:08}}
  • Any planet well placed gives that kind of success and intuition. Any planet debilitated means ancestor dosha: he says this is guaranteed. {{t:01:53:39}}
  • Moon and Indra (Uranus) in the 7th. The Moon is well placed but Uranus is badly placed, so he hates indulgence and turns everything into enmity, even toward himself. The mind is irritable, with doubt and harsh speech. {{t:01:54:12}}
  • Back. With the 8th sign activated for the Moon, there can be a gap in the spine (he names lumbar levels). With Mars and Jupiter helping, he will not be bedridden. He walks about even in pain. He makes many enemies and also parties with them. {{t:01:54:44}}
  • Marriage tension. Rahu and Neptune near the partner house, so the household has storm. Rahu says "I will end it in divorce". Neptune says "I will never let it happen". Jupiter and they fight forever. The Ketu in the debilitated place is steadied by Mercury. Headaches or stomach trouble in him or his wife. {{t:01:55:14}}
  • The two houses of Saturn here are secrecy and the in-laws: the more he stays secret with his in-laws and about fortune, the more positive Saturn is. The more he interferes, the worse. The 8th is the house of secrecy. If he never counts his money, blind money comes. {{t:01:56:49}}
  • Property. Saturn looking at the 5th gives property he may never use, and some that gives comfort. If he keeps asking and planning, nothing gets built. {{t:01:57:19}}
  • The 11th house is Jupiter's friendly sign, as good as rented out to Ketu, so gains will be large. {{t:01:58:23}}
  • KP refinement. With Mercury strong in sign 2 (Venus's sign) and Neptune and Rahu moved away, divorce is out of the picture. Jupiter's aspect adds "I will not let you go, and we love only after fighting". {{t:01:58:53}}
  • Remedy summary for this chart: a pearl for the Moon. For Rahu, keep the ancestors' grace and keep a guru. Sitting silently destroys enemies and Ketu (speech). The two Suns, one exalted and one debilitated, cancel each other. {{t:01:59:25}}

Which thread colour {{t:02:00:31}}

  • Using the navamsa, he decides the thread colour. Black: no, it will harm. Yellow: no, never. Red: yes, red is fine in both. Red protects Mars and Jupiter. {{t:02:00:31}}
  • To refine it, dilute the red with a bit of blue, since blue suits Rahu and settles Mars, Moon and Neptune. But blue alone would destroy Jupiter, so make it purple: more red, less blue. {{t:02:01:03}}
  • Warning on Rahu with Jupiter. If Rahu is placed on Jupiter by a wrong colour or thread, a head-only figure appears. He says avoid head-only idols and busts at home: horse heads, Ganapati heads, Buddha heads sold as decorations. Remove them. He calls this highly negative, because Jupiter goes down and nothing works, even Saturn, without Jupiter. {{t:02:01:35}}
  • Without Jupiter, Saturn, Mars and Rahu cannot do anything. So have a guru in some form: father, brother, friend. If you cannot find one, make a small box that you call "you are my guru", or make the cosmos your guru and leave it to it. {{t:02:02:06}}

The thread colours, the claim that every debilitated planet means ancestor dosha, and the advice to remove head-only statues are the speaker's own rules. The notes record them and do not endorse them. The sample chart is a teaching example and not a person.
