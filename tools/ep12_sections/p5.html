14. Blue sapphire: when to wear it and when not to {{t:01:20:17}}

The speaker's rules on the Saturn stone. He treats it as the main gem remedy, calls Saturn a "badly named" planet because the stone is widely worn wrongly, and says the shade and strength matter more than carat weight. This section records his rules and is not an endorsement of wearing any stone. {{t:01:20:49}}

The idea

If an area of life sits in Saturn's darkness, set Saturn. Blue sapphire for permanent need, like glasses. {{t:01:20:17}}

Do not wear

He marks three placements where pure blue sapphire is forbidden, one of them the 8th house, the house of death. He says this comes from experience. The captions do not make the other two clear. {{t:01:21:20}}

Instead

For those cases he suggests a supporting stone: a white or yellow topaz with a blue shade, "a pinch" of sapphire. How much blue depends on how Jupiter, Venus and the Sun sit with Saturn. {{t:01:22:21}}

  • How blue and how clean depends on Saturn's degrees and its presence in the chart. Not yellow-blue, not white-blue, not light blue. This only comes with experience and is a long matter. {{t:01:20:49}}
  • A retrograde Saturn can be given a sapphire. A combust one may be helped, but a bad planet that is combust is already "good, do not touch it". {{t:01:20:49}}
  • Sign 10 is taken as Saturn's. Sign 11 is also his but belongs to Yama, so it follows Pluto and comes in a later class. {{t:01:21:20}}
  • Strong stones: people come with a sapphire so powerful it is like putting 10 kg of flour in a 5 kg bag. It helps and also hurts. Always use another planet's support. Vastu does the same: each zone is supported by the zone beside it. {{t:01:22:52}}
  • Size. Five or seven ratti is enough. People say "weigh 10 kg wear 10 ratti, 100 kg wear 100". It does not work like that. Quality and power of the stone matter. Price differs for that reason. {{t:01:26:01}}
  • Cost. He says remedies are expensive the way life-saving medicine and operations are, or going abroad, a party, a child's wedding or education. Trade money for time. People who skimp by nature have their 12th house disturbed, and he says it is not their fault. {{t:01:26:01}}
  • The cheap-sapphire and car-buying contrast: people pay extra for car features and house paint but haggle for a sapphire. {{t:01:13:25}}
  • "Wear it if you understand its importance. If you do not, you die." He also says miracles happen to only some people and the rest keep begging. Doctors loot, "but we sacrifice for the cure anyway". {{t:01:14:26}}
  • People go to astrologers for 20 years and doctors for 50 visits with a fat file. They will keep looking until they find the right person, and they cannot leave. {{t:01:14:59}}
  • Remedies work even more strongly inside the Saturn mahadasha. {{t:01:17:40}}

The cost, size and stone-quality claims are the speaker's own. Stones are a sensitive area with a lot of fraud. Check any stone and any seller independently, and do not buy on the strength of these notes.
