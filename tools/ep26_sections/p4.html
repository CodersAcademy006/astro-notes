16. Rahu in the houses 1 to 6 {{t:01:24:48}}

He lays out the natural chart (Kalpurush): sign 1 is the face, 2 and 12 the eyes, 3 and 11 the shoulders, 4 and 10 the chest, 5 and 9 the knees, 6 and 8 the feet, and 7 the private parts and stomach area. Signs are fixed to places: 1 Mars, 2 Venus, 3 Mercury, 4 Moon, 5 Sun, 6 Rahu, 7 Neptune, 8 Uranus (Indra), 9 Jupiter, 10 Saturn, 11 Pluto, 12 Ketu. {{t:01:25:21}}

1st house {{t:01:26:24}}

Problem

The Sun is eclipsed. The person argues back at any talk of wisdom, talks nonsense, and is ready to fight. He calls this the "good kidnapper": he would even kidnap you for a good cause. {{t:01:26:24}}

Logic

The 1st is Mars's place, the monkey (Hanuman), and Rahu is the elephant. A monkey that makes the elephant dance and never lets it rest. A bad one becomes an extremist, a good one a wrestler-friend. {{t:01:27:27}}

Notes

Trouble with government. Strong Rahu in the 1st is clever enough to steal and not get caught, he says. {{t:01:27:59}}

2nd house {{t:01:27:59}}

  • Venus's house and Venus is the guru of the demons. Rahu is welcome there. Rich, with an expensive watch, a bracelet and a costly car, fond of parties and swearing. He tells the family "you are below me". {{t:01:28:29}}
  • Gives, and takes. If negative it brings a tax raid and agents of the enforcement office. {{t:01:28:59}}
  • Rahu's point is repentance. He gives and then takes. If you do not set Rahu and become proud, trouble follows. Hanuman is humble and eager to work for Ram, and ego cannot enter him. {{t:01:29:32}}
  • The cow (Venus) and the elephant do not fight. The elephant fights nobody unless provoked, and breaks a tree to pass if blocked. {{t:01:30:04}}

3rd house, Gemini {{t:01:30:35}}

  • Rahu is exalted in Gemini (the 3rd sign, Mercury's), the throat chakra. A great communicator and healer who can heal by talk. Success in social media, the stock market, and as a king of random guesses in gambling. Without Mars's grace, he says, lottery gains do not come. {{t:01:30:35}}
  • He says such a person can "cut a diamond" in debate, and you cannot cut his argument. If bad, they fight, kill, and go mad in fits. {{t:01:31:07}}
  • The goat and the elephant do not fight. {{t:01:31:37}}

Rahu is a magnet {{t:01:31:37}}

  • A magnet is used for Rahu's faults. It pulls out the old buried pain of the heart, which he calls the "heart wall". A belt with a magnet for a bad back (Saturn) is, he says, repaired by Rahu. He asks to be reminded in the next class to say more. {{t:01:32:09}}
  • Rahu pulls the strength of whatever it sits with and gives it its own. Magnetism is attraction: a woman pulled to a handsome young man, a man pulled to a beautiful woman, and they lose clarity (Sun and Mercury). A "prince on a white horse". {{t:01:32:41}}
  • He says girls are tricked, kidnapped, ruined, and films are made of them, because Rahu made the illusion. {{t:01:33:12}}
  • Break the magnet. If something entices you, hold witness mind and say "God gave it, whose luck it is". The magnet ends. The car may even come to you. A famed teacher, he says, drew cars to himself that way, and never had a child, yet left a legacy. {{t:01:33:44}}
  • With a magnet, a 5 kg one pulls 4 kg of iron and a 100 g one is pulled by all iron. {{t:01:35:18}}

The remark about girls and the example of a named teacher are his. The advice to stay clear-headed about attraction is general, and any real case of coercion or abuse needs help from the police and support groups.

4th house {{t:01:35:18}}

Where

Rahu in the 4th, with Ketu in the 10th. The 4th is the Moon's umbrella. It is debilitated there, yet can give good results. {{t:01:35:18}}

Logic

Many billionaires have a debilitated Rahu here. The secret: they had the mother's love. If a mother's love is missing, the person is ruined. {{t:01:35:48}}

Picture

The "tied elephant": royal comfort, many vehicles (the elephant is a car, the motorbike a horse). Pulls in mother's love. {{t:01:36:20}}

  • It brings deceit from friends and in property. As soon as the roof goes on the house, another deal sours. Such a person should not trust anyone and must decide with their own eyes. {{t:01:36:50}}
  • Never go to the share market or crypto on someone's say-so. Social media work comes from the 3rd house. If he invests money there it stops coming, and if he does not, it starts. {{t:01:37:22}}
  • This person becomes an owner who keeps living bodies working for him, and uses only the head. If he starts using the body he will not enjoy. A strange condition: to enjoy luxury, do not do labour yourself. He thinks, makes ideas, has others do the work. He gifts cars, not toffees. {{t:01:37:54}}

The advice to avoid the share market and crypto is his reading of one house and not financial advice.

5th house {{t:01:38:26}}

  • It is the Sun's house, a dangerous one: Rahu eclipses the Sun. Intuition, free gifts, lottery gains, habits, parties. It plants habits of vice and extravagance, and a need to give advice to everyone. {{t:01:38:26}}
  • Children are blocked. He says it can lower a man's sperm count and block a woman's fallopian tubes, and cause gynaecological trouble that is hard to locate, as Ketu's help is absent. {{t:01:38:57}}
  • A child may have mental or bodily difficulty, such as ADHD. Ketu stops bodily growth and Rahu stops growth of intellect. {{t:01:39:29}}
  • If Jupiter or Mars aspects, Rahu is checked. Then fame, song, a YouTube channel, and a famous decision-maker or accountant. {{t:01:40:00}}
  • Lions and elephants: both say "I am the king". The elephant beats the lion by intellect. Study and foreign travel also come here. {{t:01:45:45}}

His claims on fertility and a child's conditions are his reading of a house. They are not medical statements. Ask a doctor about fertility or a child's development.

6th house {{t:01:40:32}}

The three

Disease, debt and enemies. He calls it the "killer Rahu". It also reads your luck in work, since the 6th is the 10th's luck house. {{t:01:40:32}}

Pattern

It makes enemies and clears them. Such a person cannot be jailed and may become his own doctor. But he gets disease, debt and enemies together. {{t:01:41:03}}

What he suggests

Keep one "false enemy", go to hospital for illness, and pay interest to someone. Rahu is then satisfied. {{t:01:41:35}}

  • A competitor next door: put a fan factory beside a fan factory. Work alongside the enemy and you rise. He calls this a strange charm (totka). {{t:01:41:03}}
  • When you visit a hospital, drink their tea and use their urinal, and do the same at a police station, as the remedy goes. {{t:01:42:07}}
  • Fear of jail. Eat jail-made roti for 43 days. If that cannot be done, build a four-walled enclosure of iron rods, make a stove inside, bake a roti there, mix that flour into your own and eat it. He says this ends the outer jail. {{t:01:42:07}}
  • Bank loans. He says never repay the bank loan and you will have fun, naming industrialists as examples. If bad intent comes, Rahu brings jail. {{t:01:42:39}}
  • If you do wrong, such as harassing someone's wife or taking a sibling's money, Rahu sends you to hospital. It is Saturn's justice. It hits until you repent. The sooner you accept, the sooner police sticks stop. {{t:01:43:10}}
  • You may get exactly the money your instalments need and no more. Make it a bigger instalment, and the income rises to match, and so do needs. To grow wealth you must also fix Mercury, Venus and Neptune. {{t:01:44:11}}
  • Rahu is in its own house here and is an emperor in any sign. It gives damage only if you did injustice. {{t:01:45:13}}

The advice not to repay a bank loan, the jail-roti charm and the hospital and police-station charms are the speaker's own folk remedies. We do not endorse them. Not repaying a loan has real legal and financial consequences.
