1. Why Rahu exists: the Sun, Saturn and the shadow {{t:00:00:33}}

This class is about the planet Rahu. The speaker opens with the verse "in joy everyone forgets, in sorrow everyone remembers" and builds a reason for Rahu from it: joy makes us forget God, so sorrow is arranged to make us remember. He sets up a chain of Sun, then Saturn, then Rahu as Saturn's agent. {{t:00:00:33}}

The channel opens with a standard notice that it does not promote drugs, alcohol or intoxicants, and that the content is for information and education only. {{t:00:00:00}}

Problem

When life is pleasant we forget God and others. Only trouble makes us remember. He says our real enemy is comfort, because in comfort the "list of strangers" grows. {{t:00:01:04}}

Cause and logic

The Sun is the supreme soul (paramatma). It gives life and light, so we feel good and forget. The Sun then appoints a justice system: his son Saturn, the dark side, to correct it. {{t:00:01:36}}

Remedy direction

Accept the pain at once and remember God without begging. The rest of the class explains how Rahu works and what to do. {{t:00:08:57}}

  • The Sun's gift. Sunflowers open, vegetation switches on, we say "come, let us earn", the morning air feels fresh. The Sun is the "god" in this picture. {{t:00:02:06}}
  • Saturn as the dark side. The Sun is light, Saturn is darkness. In the evening bacteria come. He says Jain study went so far as to note infrared and ultraviolet light after sunset, so food after dark brings insects. {{t:00:02:37}}
  • Justice is harsh. Anyone who goes for justice should expect cruelty, because justice for one means injustice for the other. Saturn records your plus and minus karma and presents the stored karma (sanchita) in the next birth as your destiny (prarabdha). {{t:00:03:40}}
  • The agent. Light and darkness cannot coexist, so Saturn appoints an agent, a shadow planet that does not exist. In the story, when the Sun met Chhaya (shadow), Saturn was born. Chhaya is never actually there. Rahu follows Saturn. {{t:00:04:12}}
  • Saturn tells Rahu to go and hit the person so that he remembers God. Man begs for a job, gets it, then says "party, drinks", and rings a bell for two minutes. Even God says "you deserve Rahu". {{t:00:05:15}}
  • Rahu is the agent who beats you for the Sun's sake, to purify you and make you remember God. He makes you cry, and the speaker says Rahu is busy making you a sadhu and a saint. {{t:00:05:47}}
  • Rahu punishes even someone innocent in this life. Yet "you must have some fault", he says, and the beating brings the remembering. {{t:00:06:19}}
  • So Rahu is the god of self-observation. It beats a human into a sage, a siddha, even an astrologer. {{t:00:06:19}}

This is the speaker's frame: suffering is for a purpose. It is a belief about karma, not a reason to stay in harm or to blame a person for what happened to them.

Dasha lengths as he lists them {{t:00:06:50}}

  • In the 120-year Vimshottari cycle: Venus 20, Saturn 19, Rahu 18, Jupiter 16. The Sun is the smallest at 6, Mars 7, Moon 10. The captions mix up the order of the Moon and Mars, so these follow the classical table. {{t:00:06:50}}
  • Rahu is the shadow of the demons' teacher, and the shadow's number sits right beside the gurus'. So for 18 years it hits and hits, and in the end hammers out gold jewellery, silver jewellery and iron tools: everything useful. {{t:00:07:21}}

Mischief is part of growth {{t:00:07:51}}

  • Whenever a hit is happening in life, it is Rahu doing some justice. The inner child (the "munna") first does mischief. A small child at an uncle's house breaks things and the parents are helpless. He says mischief is needed by children and also by adults. {{t:00:07:51}}
  • When the inner child is quiet it says "fine, take a toffee". So accept every delay and block at once. Say "yes, Rahu, you must beat me, I accept." {{t:00:08:22}}
  • Remembering is not begging. "O God, save me, save me" will get Rahu saying "chew, chew". Real remembering is gratitude: always keeping the inner mind in the infinite. {{t:00:08:57}}
  • Krishna says "you never remembered me because you never forgot me". When God comes to mind as a living presence, Rahu cools and trouble declines. If you turn beggar again, you get hit again. {{t:00:09:28}}

2. The eclipse: how Rahu is "made" {{t:00:10:00}}

What happens

Rahu swallows the Sun for 18 years. When it comes there is no clarity in life. He says doubts and suspicion arrive: "his character is bad", "the maid stole", "someone is behind me", "did I leave the latch open". {{t:00:13:38}}

The mechanism

Earth moves round the Sun, the Moon moves in the middle. At a special distance the Moon covers the Sun fully. The word for eclipse (grahan) is "to take", as a priest says "I will take, and give the fee on time". {{t:00:11:02}}

Reading

When someone comes between you and your life energy, your life stops, your head jams. That is how Rahu works in a life. {{t:00:12:04}}

  • The Sun is a thousand times bigger than Earth. The Moon (the "Chanda mama" where the ancestors are remembered) sits in the middle, and the Sun disappears for some places for some minutes. {{t:00:10:32}}
  • Light takes 8 minutes to reach Earth, and the eclipse lasts as long as the Moon stays there. {{t:00:11:34}}
  • Rahu Kaal is daily. It is the time when something comes in the middle. {{t:00:12:04}}
  • The skull jams, and the body cannot go on without it. So a planet was developed that jams, a new ray, and it is called Rahu. {{t:00:12:35}}
  • Why 18 years. The Moon's elliptical orbit pattern repeats every 18 years, which he calls the Rahu phase. Like a bone with a 45-day plaster, there is no 2.5-day scheme. Rahu runs 18 years. {{t:00:12:35}}
  • With the Sun eaten, life is dark for a time: you cannot see and have no clarity. The stories show Rahu swallowing the Sun, and these are signs. {{t:00:13:06}}
  • Small nuisances: constant scratching, a horn in the ear. You grow irritated, and then cry "God, silence it". Then God comes, and the work happens. {{t:00:13:38}}

3. The story of Durvasa, Indra and the churning of the ocean {{t:00:14:08}}

The speaker calls these stories signs and indicators. He retells them along the body's chakras. {{t:00:14:08}}

Durvasa's garland {{t:00:14:08}}

  • Indra rides his elephant Airavata. The sage Durvasa, the god of anger, comes by with a flower garland an apsara gave him, and tosses it to Indra, who is drunk with joy. Indra puts it on the elephant's head. {{t:00:14:08}}
  • The scent draws bees. The elephant shakes his head and the garland falls. Durvasa says "you insulted what I gave." {{t:00:15:10}}
  • Whenever we insult or are insulted, Durvasa switches on in us and we curse in bad language. He says anger lives in the liver and acts through the sacral centre. If we throw it outward, we wreck another's liver. If we keep it in, we wreck our own. {{t:00:15:41}}
  • The liver belongs to Jupiter, near the sacral centre. It regenerates, because Jupiter is the guru and revives. The heart also revives, hence the "revival herb" (mrit sanjivani) there. {{t:00:15:41}}
  • Indra begs forgiveness and Durvasa curses him again: heaven, apsaras and wealth will leave you. {{t:00:16:13}}

The mapping of anger to the liver and sacral centre is his scheme. It is not a medical statement.

Vishnu and the churning {{t:00:16:13}}

  • Indra runs to Vishnu in the ocean of milk. The ocean of milk is the infinite sea at the sacral (water) centre, where Vishnu sits. Brahma is lowest, with four faces, at the four-petalled base centre (muladhara) where Saturn is. Rahu begins from there. {{t:00:16:43}}
  • Vishnu says: heaven will be recreated for you, since demons are about to win. We will churn the ocean and bring out nectar so this does not happen again. {{t:00:17:16}}
  • Meaning of the churn. The serpent Vasuki is wound round Mount Mandara and spun, with demons on one side and gods on the other. Vishnu as a tortoise holds the mountain. The serpent is our kundalini. On one side runs ida, on the other pingala, and friction between them makes the snake rise. {{t:00:17:47}}
  • Many people use the kundalini for eating, children and sleep all their lives, and wisdom never arrives. Wisdom comes by sorrow, sorrow by Rahu. Rahu eats the Sun so that you suffer and create God. {{t:00:18:18}}
  • That is why when people are in trouble the body sits still and only the head moves: "what do I do, right side, left side". Then something comes to mind. This is the churning. {{t:00:18:50}}

The poison comes first {{t:00:18:50}}

What comes out first

Of the 14 treasures, the first to come was poison (halahal). In any inner churning, first comes abuse: "that fellow, that situation, my grandfather, my boss did this". {{t:00:19:20}}

Why it matters

If you hurl it out at a boss or uncle you spoil the churn. You cannot say it to a wife, mother-in-law or others in a triangle. So the poison must not go outward. {{t:00:19:51}}

The fix

Shiva is called. He holds the poison in the throat, turning it blue (Neelkantha). In practice you need a "Shiva": a mother or friend who listens to your poison and says "fine, forget it". Throw it into space, give it to the cosmos. {{t:00:22:28}}

  • He says the body's lower organs (spleen, pancreas) are where the poison would rot you if it went down. It is held at the throat, where nothing stays, an empty zone of space (ether), so it neither goes up nor down. {{t:00:20:21}}
  • Blue matters. Most thread remedies use blue, and after black, blue is the colour in tattoo remedies. {{t:00:20:52}}
  • After the poison: the wish-tree (kalpavriksha), then the horse (he says "a motorbike, a one-bedroom flat"), the elephant ("a four-wheeler"), Lakshmi, Rambha and so on. Finally Dhanvantari the healer, with the pot of nectar. {{t:00:21:56}}
  • After the wish-tree, wishes are granted but "we are greedy". Each prize is a modern comfort. {{t:00:22:28}}

His modern reading of the mythic treasures and his joke about Rambha are his own colour. Nothing here is a claim about the actual story.
