10. The three remedies: daan, dua and dava {{t:01:36:44}}

Daan (charity)

Give the troubling planet's own item away with your own hand. The speaker's claim is that the planet's hold leaves through the hand that gives. {{t:01:36:44}}

Dua (prayer)

Blessings from anyone count: parents, elders, even pets and household staff. A neutral, grateful approach draws a better return than demanding one. {{t:01:36:44}}

Dava (medicine)

Care for the body itself. The speaker places this alongside, not instead of, the other two. {{t:01:36:44}}

Giving water to the Sun (surya ko jal) {{t:01:48:53}}

  • Only offer it when the morning sun is visibly red. Once it has turned yellow the speaker says it has already become "Mangal," a fireball, and offering water at that point is said to backfire. {{t:01:48:53}}
  • The sun must be physically visible to you; picturing it in your mind does not count. {{t:01:49:56}}
  • The water must land on soil or a plant, never on stone, because the earth needs to absorb it; many people now do this into a potted plant. {{t:01:49:56}}
  • Best done before about 6 to 6:30am. {{t:01:49:56}}

Saturn's charity during dhaiya or sade-sati {{t:01:37:49}}

The speaker's own rule here: if Saturn already sits well in your chart, giving Saturn's items away during this phase can do harm; if Saturn sits badly, the same charity helps. He frames this as something to check with a knowledgeable person rather than do blindly. {{t:01:37:49}}

11. Ancestors, your DNA and their blessings {{t:01:52:02}}

  • DNA as a record. The speaker's claim is that DNA carries encoded feeling and thought across lifetimes, what he calls akashic records, and that this is why some things feel instantly familiar or unfamiliar without any obvious reason. {{t:01:53:03}}
  • Why the father's line is checked. In his framework the mother's identity is never in question, so paternity is what gets verified, since the father's DNA is what carries forward. {{t:01:52:33}}
  • Gaya shraddh timing. Wait at least three to four years after a death before performing shraddh at Gaya. Ancestors beyond three generations are said to move to a different realm, where remembering them with feeling still helps, even without the formal ritual. {{t:01:56:15}}
  • Monthly practice. On amavasya (new moon), feed a cow, a dog, a crow and a guest with real feeling behind it, a practice the speaker recommends especially to couples who are not conceiving or whose children are not thriving. {{t:01:54:38}}

The speaker adds that women connect to their ancestors' blessings through the men in their life (father, husband or any willing male relative), since in his framework the lineage itself runs through the father's side. This is his own traditional view, recorded here but not endorsed. {{t:01:55:42}}

He also claims that children who lose their mother very early often rise unusually high in life, citing several historical and mythological figures as examples. This is offered without evidence and these notes do not endorse or verify it; it is recorded only because it was said. {{t:01:41:37}}

12. Faith and surrender: the Nirmal Baba example {{t:01:57:17}}

The speaker describes Nirmal Baba's method as finding whoever in a crowd is "neutral," meaning free of fixed belief, and giving that person a small, almost trivial task, such as feeding a dog a biscuit. His claim is that the belief itself creates the shift, not the biscuit: the person decides "my days will improve now," and that decided feeling is what moves things. He estimates roughly half of any such crowd will see a change this way, and calls this a form of mass surrender. {{t:01:58:18}}

He offers his own household as a smaller example: his wife began silently naming her guru while cooking, after hearing a lecture suggesting food made while remembering god becomes "prasad," and found that doing so changed how the food tasted to her. {{t:02:00:55}}

Both examples are the speaker's personal illustrations of belief and surrender. These notes record them as his stories, not as instructions to follow any particular guru or practice, and do not endorse either Nirmal Baba's methods or any guarantee of results. {{t:01:59:21}}
