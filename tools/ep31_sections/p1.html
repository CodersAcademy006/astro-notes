1. The cow and the lion: why you need a guru {{t:00:00:33}}

The class on Jupiter (Guru, Brihaspati) opens with a story. A cow is grazing in a forest and a lion decides to eat her. Both end up stuck in a marsh, and only the cow gets out, because she has a guru and owner who comes looking for her. The speaker uses this to say: a person with no guru, no friend and no guide dies badly in their own marsh.

  • The planets in the cow's life. The cow eats grass. The speaker says grass holds the most Sun element (he says a single blade takes in a million photons) and needs the most water. The cow is Venus, the grass is Mercury, the photons are the Sun and the water is the Moon. Cow dung is used as manure. {{t:00:00:33}} {{t:00:01:05}}
  • The cow is worshipped and is Venus, the guru of the demons, here in her giving form. {{t:00:01:05}}
  • The lion is the wrathful form of Jupiter. When Jupiter turns wrathful it is counted as negative, and that goes with the negative form of Venus. In the positive form of Venus we keep a cow. {{t:00:01:35}} {{t:00:02:06}}
  • The cow runs towards a marsh, thinking death there is better than being torn apart. She sinks. The lion jumps after her and sinks in another corner. {{t:00:02:06}} {{t:00:02:38}}
  • The lion says he would tear her apart if he could only get out. He is full of ego. He has no friend and no guru: "I am the king of the jungle, I listen to no one". {{t:00:03:09}}
  • The cow says her master will come looking for her. He comes, throws a rope noose around her neck, she pulls too, and she comes out. {{t:00:03:40}}
  • The cow laughs and tells the lion how necessary a guru and a friend are. The lion dies slowly in the marsh. {{t:00:04:11}}

Problem

People who end up alone have a painful, bad death. The speaker points to ICU patients begging for death, calling them "nigure", people who have no guru to guide them out of the marsh. {{t:00:04:11}} {{t:00:04:45}}

Why

A marsh forms in our own mind and makes life hellish. Without a guide there is no one to pull you out. {{t:00:04:45}}

Fix

Have a guru or true friend. The speaker's meaning of the word: "gu" is darkness and "ru" is light, so the guru takes you from darkness to light. {{t:00:04:45}}
