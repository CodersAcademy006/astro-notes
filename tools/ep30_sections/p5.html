19. Questions from the class {{t:01:54:55}}

Can you wear several stones, even from enemy planets? {{t:01:54:55}}

  • Most charts need correction in 4 or 5 planets. The speaker usually corrects only one or two master planets and sees how much relief comes. If the main relief arrives, you become strong enough to fight the rest. {{t:01:54:55}}
  • Do not stop when you feel content. "If Arjuna said I have fought, I am happy, he would never reach his goal." Keep following Krishna. Some cases took years and the work still got done. {{t:01:56:28}}
  • After a stone: a small remedy of two rupees now and then is enough. A stone lasts 100 years. When you die, the next person can wear it. A stone or clothes taken off by one person can be used by another. The speaker calls the fear of a dead mother's clothes "a delusion of the head": people take the property but not the clothes. {{t:01:56:59}}

Sun and Mercury together, and the "double Ketu" {{t:01:58:01}}

  • When the Sun and Mercury sit together, a Ketu-like effect forms. If Ketu is also there, it is a double Ketu and works with very high power. In sign 4 it can ruin even the grandmother and mother. {{t:01:58:01}}
  • Mars, Mercury and Jupiter together form the "Ketu of the three worlds" (trilokya ka Ketu). In a negative sign (for example sign 4, where Mars is debilitated) it harms the mother, but makes the person ruler of the three worlds. {{t:01:58:32}}

Does Ketu move clockwise or anticlockwise? {{t:01:59:02}}

  • It moves backwards in the zodiac like Rahu, from sign 4 to 3 to 2. Both are always retrograde. {{t:01:59:33}}
  • Its aspects are not its own. They follow the planet it is joined with. If Jupiter sits in some house and Ketu sits in another, Ketu takes Jupiter's head through the 4th, 5th and 8th aspects, and can make a raj yoga and an interest in astrology. {{t:01:59:33}}
  • Ketu is a dangerous planet because it lends its body to any planet it touches: "this morning with one, this evening with another". The speaker's example: a gang that wants a man killed and a man who wants him saved both phone the same agent. {{t:02:00:36}}

Mercury as a remedy when Ketu is in the 12th {{t:02:01:09}}

  • In sign 3, use green (Mercury). In sign 12, do not use green. Yellow comes there, and Mercury in the 12th would cut twice. Do not give cash to the 12th-house groups (strangers, religion) if the sign is 12. {{t:02:01:09}}
  • Mercury debilitated or exalted in the 12th both work out well. Venus too gains in both. A debilitated Saturn makes a king (neech bhanga raj yoga). {{t:02:02:11}}

Blue thread for the Sun or Venus, then Rahu's 7th aspect {{t:02:02:43}}

If the Sun or Venus is in a negative sign and you fix it with a blue thread (Rahu binds them), Rahu's 7th aspect then affects the job. That needs a separate remedy, which is why the right and left foot threads are different colours. "We are only stopping the negative, not creating positive." {{t:02:03:13}}

Is mantra chanting also Ketu? {{t:02:03:43}}

  • Yes, in a subtle way. The Moon takes about 27 days to go round, and there are 30 tithis of 12 degrees each. There are 27 nakshatras, each with 4 padas, giving 108. {{t:02:03:43}}
  • The speaker explains that each nakshatra has its own syllables, for example Ashwini with "chu, che, cho, la". When you say a syllable, you send a vibration to that region of space, and it rings on the "portal" inside you. He calls it quantum physics. {{t:02:04:46}}
  • Gentle nakshatras are loving. On such a day, if you talk to your spouse or partner to apologise, forgiveness comes quickly. {{t:02:06:19}}
  • Start a house in a movable (char) nakshatra, marry in a fixed one. Each nakshatra has a deity. Saying Maha Mrityunjaya and similar mantras rings the matching nakshatra. {{t:02:06:50}}

The speaker's own explanation, not a classical derivation. He plans a separate nakshatra class.

Ketu debilitated in the ascendant {{t:02:07:52}}

Green thread, green belt or green cap, but you cannot wear them always, so use them as long as you can. A fashionable eyebrow cut or threading also counts as a Ketu cut. A steel piece in the ear for a debilitated Ketu. Surgical tools are steel, not copper or brass. {{t:02:07:52}}

Speech power (vak siddhi) {{t:02:08:56}}

It needs Mercury's strength and a settled Jupiter. The speaker warns that people who learn stambhan mostly use it on themselves, because we speak about ourselves all day, and so our own talk harms us. What we say is heard, and we are manifesting for 24 hours. {{t:02:08:56}}

Ketu in the 6th house {{t:02:09:59}}

  • It is slack in Rahu's house, a "lazy" Ketu that still makes a bhogi-yogi. It takes the head of Rahu. What happens depends on where Rahu sits and where that sign's lord is. {{t:02:09:59}}
  • If Rahu is weak, the 6th is weak. If the Sun is weak, the 5th is weak. If the Moon is weak, the 4th is weak. If Rahu does not support Ketu, Ketu cuts itself. They always work as a pair. {{t:02:11:33}}

Mass belief versus what is written (prarabdha) {{t:02:12:37}}

If the planets say a war should happen and mass consciousness pre-lives it, does it not happen? The speaker says it turns into a proxy war. People who already mentally lived a win react badly to the loss. Fans break TVs after a match, and students who "pre-lived" a pass cry and tear books after an exam. Their reaction shows how fully they had lived the result in the head. {{t:02:12:37}}

Do I need to sweat the small things to become a king? {{t:02:13:41}}

No. A king thinks big and treats the small as a by-product. Perfectionism increases duality. The one who lives in singularity becomes the king, because he accepts everyone, those who vote for him and those who do not. "King size will not work with small thinking." {{t:02:14:11}}

Can you fix a planet by consciousness, with no remedy? {{t:02:15:13}}

  • Yes. If your Jupiter or Venus is flawed and you have decided that your partner may not be with you, and you live happily without them, you need no topaz. Human consciousness is already set. {{t:02:15:13}}
  • If Saturn is retrograde or afflicted and you must wash your own clothes and vessels, but you are not unhappy about it, there is no need for a blue sapphire. Wear stones and give charity only if you suffer. {{t:02:15:43}}
  • If your Venus or Neptune is flawed, do not buy perfume or alcohol. Live easily. {{t:02:16:14}}
  • His example: an Iranian man who is said to have died at 96 after not bathing for 60 years, smoking all day. "What will Rahu or Ketu do to him?" The speaker says the stones and remedies are needed because of attachment and greed. {{t:02:16:14}}

The speaker's own illustration. He does not give a source. Do not use it as a health guide.

Is a temple at home advised? {{t:02:17:44}}

The question was what to do if a home shrine has been there for a long time. The speaker's answer is a long political and social aside. His core claims: nakshatras are deities, and their qualities are in the Puranas. He says turning a deity into a statue business, moving prayer into private homes and splitting people by caste and religion has weakened shared learning, and that Sanskrit is the shortest language a computer understands. {{t:02:18:16}}

This passage is the speaker's personal and political opinion, not astrology. These notes record only that it was said, and do not endorse it.

He closes the class by promising the next class on Jupiter. {{t:02:21:22}}
