1. What Ketu is: the fish, the sword, the flag and the shield {{t:00:00:33}}

Ketu is a shadow planet with a body and no head. The speaker pictures it as a headless figure shaped like a fish, holding a flag, a sword and a shield. Wherever Ketu sits in your chart, those four things show up in that area of life.

SymbolWhat it means in life
Fish bodyYou float along, bound to a current, with great speed. {{t:00:02:38}}
SwordA cut will happen there: in the body, in a relationship, in speech. Ketu is the cutter. {{t:00:03:08}}
Flag"It is only me, my flag will fly." Ego and stubbornness. {{t:00:03:08}}
ShieldThe urge to defend and block, with no brain behind it. {{t:00:03:08}}
No headNobody inside is thinking. Someone else does the thinking: mother, father, brother, sister, society. {{t:00:03:08}}

Both Rahu and Ketu are described as constructs: shadow points made by the Sun and Moon positions, which stand for the energy body that the planets act upon. {{t:00:02:07}}

The tail rule {{t:00:03:40}}

Ketu is bad

Harm comes from tailed creatures: fish, dog, cow. They do not suit you. People do not follow you, nobody listens to you or obeys you. {{t:00:03:40}}

Ketu is good

Tailed animals bring you benefit and gain. People follow you: a child behind its mother, a spouse supporting a spouse, a helper behind you like a tail. {{t:00:03:40}}

Quick test

Nobody listens to you: Ketu is bad. You listen to nobody, not even yourself: Rahu is bad. Trouble in the lower body points to Ketu. Trouble in the upper body or head points to Rahu. {{t:00:04:11}}

Ketu is touch, intuition and fulfilment {{t:00:06:47}}

  • Rahu has the head, so it sees, hears and speaks. Ketu has hands and feet, so it feels its way by touch, like a blind child reading Braille. With no eyes or ears it must run on intuition. {{t:00:07:49}}
  • Ketu rules the boneless parts: the skin, the organs of pleasure. All enjoyment and satisfaction that arrive through the body is Ketu. {{t:00:08:21}}
  • Ketu is called the giver of moksha, but the speaker calls it the god of sex first. After a sweet or after real fulfilment, you say "enough, I do not want more". That satisfied state is his meaning of moksha: it comes after you have really enjoyed, not before. {{t:00:06:47}}
  • So Ketu is both "bhogi and yogi". It first gives enjoyment, then detachment. He cites Osho's "Sambhog se Samadhi": people got stuck at the first word and never rose above it. The people who criticise it loudest are the ones who never enjoyed. {{t:00:08:21}}
  • The relationship with Rahu. Rahu is the demon that deludes. Ketu follows. Whoever's head is fixed onto Ketu, Ketu behaves like that head. That is why the word guru matters so much. {{t:00:07:18}}

The speaker also says Ketu's "moksha" is not the enlightened kind. It is the kind that comes from being beaten and made wise. {{t:00:09:54}}

Why to study planets as behaviour {{t:00:01:03}}

At the start the speaker says astrology is more than reading planets. Intuition is what makes predictions true, and Neptune is the strongest planet of intuition, Saturn of guesses, the Sun of the soul, Jupiter of knowledge, Venus of enjoying life. When you live the way a planet behaves, that planet gets activated in your life. {{t:00:01:03}}

3. Ketu in Saturn's sign: the ears, the cuts, and instant karma {{t:00:14:35}}

  • Saturn the snake. Saturn judges, hisses and threatens. Rahu is its head and Ketu its body. The body grips and decides whatever Saturn wants. Ketu here follows Saturn's nature: whatever Saturn says, Ketu does. {{t:00:14:35}}
  • Ketu is the ears. The ears are the route of space (akash). They stay open sleeping or awake. A person who says "I will not listen" ruins their hearing. Childhood ear piercing sets Ketu. The urinary and sexual tracts are linked to the same point. The Brahmin's sacred thread over the ear presses a nerve and keeps the pancreas in order. {{t:00:15:07}}
  • Diabetes link (speaker's claim). If sugar appears in the urine, Ketu is bad. The reason he gives: you see with the face, hear with the ears and cut with the tongue, so cutting speech damages the pancreas. Ear piercing is the remedy, because it pushes the Ketu sword in and makes a hole. {{t:00:15:39}}

Problem

Many cuts. Cut scars in many places. Women cut fingers while working in the kitchen. {{t:00:16:12}}

Cause

The mind is so angry and rotten that the body prepares to cut. It could not cut anyone else, so it cuts itself. Rahu fights, Ketu suffers. {{t:00:16:12}}

The warning

The speaker's examples: a husband wishes harm on his wife and ends up injured on his bike. A lover says "I am crazy for you" and then cuts himself. A mind that wanted to cut someone else turns the blade on its owner. {{t:00:16:42}}

Instant karma {{t:00:17:42}}

  • In the chart, Ketu looks at Saturn about 70 to 80 percent of the time. When you wish harm on someone, it comes back on you quickly, "instant karma". {{t:00:17:42}}
  • We forget what we wished. "I never harmed anyone" is usually wrong, because the harm was in the mind. {{t:00:18:13}}
  • Whatever you ask for your own body, the cosmos portal opens and returns the same. If someone else does not receive it, Ketu delivers it to your body under Saturn's leadership. {{t:00:18:13}}
  • Remedy: forgive others. The more you forgive, the more you are forgiven. {{t:00:18:44}}

4. Ketu in the 11th and 12th houses, and death awareness {{t:00:18:44}}

The 11th is Pluto (Yamraj), the 12th is Ketu's own house.

  • Knowing your own death. Ketu's influence on the 8th house or the 8th sign, even by aspect, gives a hunch of your own death. Many people say near the end "I will not survive". The energy body senses it, not the physical body. {{t:00:19:16}}
  • A good death. If the lord of the 11th (Pluto) sits well, death is graceful. {{t:00:19:16}}
  • A bad death. Dying in pipes and needles, because you rotted the well of your life by cursing relatives, neighbours and strangers. Ketu then cuts the body. The sharpest cut is cancer, where your own cells eat your body. Boils, pimples and tumours are milder forms of the same cutting. If a growth cuts off and comes out, the body is cutting and freeing itself, which is Ketu healing by cutting. {{t:00:19:47}}
  • Ketu in the 11th: very wealthy, good friends but they may all be drinkers and revellers. Gifts come back in the same amount. Sometimes a bad combination makes you penniless, starved of bread. {{t:00:20:49}}
  • Ketu in its own sign, the 12th: sword, fish and shield all strong, no head. A saint-like person, unmoved by others' wealth, wanting no change, wanting no happiness from children. The crowd is the family. Gets a sense of death a year ahead and can say when others will die. Can become a predictor. {{t:00:21:20}}
  • If other planets support, he becomes a protector, like a lion or wolf. If not, he becomes a street dog: fed if someone throws a roti, otherwise lost. {{t:00:22:22}}

Do not use hands and feet for wrong action {{t:00:22:22}}

  • The speaker defines wrong action as whatever hurts another person against their will, even if it feels good to you. Helping a man who does not want to be picked up is wrong. Touching someone who objects is wrong. {{t:00:22:22}}
  • Force makes a pauper or a fakir. Without force Ketu gives pleasure, comfort and enough enjoyment to lead to fulfilment. {{t:00:22:54}}
