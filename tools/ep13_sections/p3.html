15. Mangalik, marriage and Hanuman worship {{t:00:54:24}}

Problem

Mars in certain houses (the captions read like 1, 4, 7, 8 and 12) makes a person Mangalik and throws trouble into marriage, since Mars is celibate. {{t:00:54:24}}

His reading

Strong Mars, and a Hanuman devotion that is not set, turns off the soft side of a person. Romance dies and strife can follow. {{t:00:55:27}}

His rules

A Mangalik should marry a Mangalik. Without setting Mars, do not do the fire rounds, otherwise a pot marriage is needed. {{t:00:58:30}}

  • A request to girls. His words: not stubbornness. Stubbornness with women is sin, since nobody can out-stubborn them. He jokes about a husband bringing a necklace. {{t:00:54:55}}
  • Hanuman for women. He says worshipping Hanuman is not good for women's household life. It can give strength, rules, quickness at work, but the male element becomes too strong. The softness and the feminine element go. Romance ends and it makes a devotee out of the husband. If both agree, no problem. {{t:00:55:27}}
  • A "Hanuman devotee" man, with Mars bad, who comes out of the temple and eyes the girls, is ruining his Mars. It is not easy to be a Hanuman bhakt. {{t:00:55:57}}
  • Tuesday and meat. "Today is Tuesday, no non-veg." The fear is inside us, because the brain is trained. Hanuman is so powerful a god that he works even in the skull. {{t:00:56:28}}
  • Take no credit. A person who takes credit is a fool, he says. Thank God that you were the instrument (nimitta). "I did nobody's work. God did your work through me." This is the opposite pole: do not let Mars turn bad. You are only a vessel. {{t:00:56:58}}
  • Will power is the strong navel. The south-east area of the house gives energy and the south gives confidence, and the whole area belongs to Mars. {{t:00:58:00}}
  • From years of experience, he says a Mangalik who marries a non-Mangalik will be separated sooner or later: ten years, twenty years, by surgery, or by loss of interest in each other. {{t:00:58:30}}
  • Court marriage. A court registration is a marriage of Mercury and Saturn, a contract. The planet says "you have gone to court" and will not let you divorce, but quarrels continue. {{t:00:59:31}}

The statements that a Mangalik who marries a non-Mangalik will be separated are the speaker's own claim from his practice. They are not shown to be true and the notes do not endorse them. He also says (at 00:59:01) that a pregnancy of a certain sex can separate a couple. The notes leave that out because it is not clear and not supported.

16. Flowers, thorns and sweets {{t:01:07:18}}

Problem

We look at the thorns in our life and the flowers in other people's. {{t:01:07:18}}

Cause

What you see, you evoke. Saying "how much money that rich person has" feeds energy to the rich and leaves you saying "my life is a mess". Both come true. {{t:01:07:49}}

Remedy

See only the flowers in your own life. Negative talk comes from a disturbed navel, which is Mars. {{t:01:08:19}}

  • Other people's unhappiness is hidden. We see only their notes, and so we make copies of what we see, and so the energy goes to them. {{t:01:07:18}}
  • Why children love sweets. A small child with a chocolate: sweet is Mars. Our milk teeth fall after about eight and new teeth come. The body recycles. The new teeth will be ruined by worms, and so the child eats sweets, since the navel is free and there is no hatred. {{t:01:08:19}}
  • "Take a toffee, uncle, take one too": pure Mars, a child who gives and shares. Give a child toffee, sweets, halwa, any sweet. This is the easiest remedy. {{t:01:08:51}}
  • If a grown-up eats a lot of sweets, he says to check the kidney and pancreas. He adds a claim that it can mean sexual unfulfilment or a good sexual energy, and that old people like it since the tongue is the one pleasure left. {{t:01:09:22}}
  • Sweets are fine if the body digests them. Sexual energy is what turns into other energies: creativity, painting, kundalini rising. So eating sweets is very good, he says. {{t:01:09:54}}
  • Share sweets with children. If there are no children, take any elderly person and make him a child first. Mars works in small children's schools. A child lives in all of us and we have pressed it down. That is why will power is small. {{t:01:10:24}}
  • He says the child should be set free: a person who says "to hell with it" and dances anywhere has will power. He names two famous public figures as examples of this. {{t:01:10:55}}
  • The third eye looks at the navel from the forehead, since there is fire at both places. {{t:01:11:28}}
