1. Mars is effort: trust yourself, not others {{t:00:01:09}}

This class is on Mars (Mangal). The speaker opens with a joke about a husband who never got flowers until his funeral, and uses it to say that a man's place is to act, not to be loved for being sweet. Mars is the planet of purushartha, the effort that gives a man meaning. {{t:00:00:36}}

What Mars is

Effort, action, fire, the navel, friendship, blood, the commander (senapati) and Hanuman. {{t:00:02:11}}

The common mistake

We work, then pray for the result, and expect things from others: "my son should give me happiness, my brother should". {{t:00:02:44}}

The rule

Expect from yourself, not from the result and not from others. Hope, reassurance and celebration are to be dropped. {{t:00:02:44}}

  • Man without action. A "sweet" boy who is only a gentle donkey has no value, he says. He has to be able to protect. Even a barking dog is rated by whether it does something. Without purushartha a man's life is wasted. {{t:00:01:40}}
  • Arjuna. When Arjuna gave up effort, Krishna told him to act. Arjuna wins because he trusts his own arrows and Krishna is given to him. Duryodhana trusts others: "I have Karna, Bhishma, Drona". Finished. {{t:00:02:11}}
  • Sun and Moon. The Sun glows by its own energy and always gives light and respect. The Moon borrows from the Sun and shines and fades, honoured on new moon and full moon. A person who relies fully on himself and sends others "to hell" has a mighty Mars. {{t:00:03:15}}

The remarks on how a man must be, and on women and men below, are the speaker's views in a light and teasing tone. They are not astrological rules and are not endorsed here.

2. Mars is the navel {{t:00:04:16}}

Birth

At birth we are the navel: the cord and placenta join us to the mother. From that centre we rise upward and settle downward. {{t:00:04:16}}

Two energies

Spirit energy above, devil energy below. Both work from the navel. A strong navel is the "core strength" trainers speak of. {{t:00:04:48}}

Feeling

On a roller coaster the navel goes in with fear. When we are happy it comes out. {{t:00:04:48}}

  • Ayurveda works through the navel. The five airs are set from it: prana above it, apana below, udana and vyana through the body. {{t:00:05:18}}
  • Mars as the cosmic navel. In the speaker's planet order, Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Earth, then Mars, then Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune and Pluto. Mars sits at the middle as the navel of the solar system. It is called the twin brother of Earth. {{t:00:05:51}}
  • Fire without water. Mars is a ball of fire and has no water or Moon. So Mars is debilitated in sign 4, the Moon's sign: you cannot put fire in water or water in fire. {{t:00:06:22}}
  • Home. The south-east is the fire corner. The north-east is where water converts from space. A kitchen in the wrong place can give many ailments. {{t:00:06:53}}
  • "Mangal bhavan, amangal hari." If the stomach is wrong, every disease follows. A ruined Mars in the chart means a hell of stomach trouble, since the navel has shifted. The doctor says fast and let the body heal itself. {{t:00:06:53}}
  • Mars heals. It makes the doctor, and it owns the pharmacy. He says the pharma industry is the second biggest after war. {{t:00:07:24}}
  • If Mars is good you are active. If it is weak you lie around, cannot sit or get up, go to the temple and beg "O God, give me". {{t:00:07:55}}

The size of the pharma industry and the doctor's advice to fast are the speaker's own remarks.

3. Mars, the navel and friendship {{t:00:08:28}}

Your navel is your friend

It stays with you until the last. The soul works from it. When you cry it quivers, and when you laugh it shakes. {{t:00:08:28}}

Problem

You make no friends, you quarrel, you get angry, you carry old words. That means Mars is spoiled. {{t:00:08:59}}

Remedy

Make friends. Forgive. Say "forget it, leave it". The navel's job is to digest, and that includes old insults. {{t:00:11:35}}

  • Digestive fire. The belly holds the jatharagni, which burns even iron and soil, and stomach acid strong enough to burn a blade. The owner of this fire is Hanuman. His consciousness runs from the navel. He is a celibate, with no gender in this reading, and Mars is a male planet. {{t:00:08:59}}
  • The navel does not care what you eat. We make a fuss about veg, non-veg and wine, but it says "I will burn it all". The speaker says this is why "I cannot digest what you said" means more than food. {{t:00:09:32}}
  • If Mars is good you say "forget it, nothing in this". You are free. If Mars is bad you say "you tore my heart", you hold anger, and you do not forgive. Friends do not form, and comfort from the navel downward does not come. {{t:00:10:02}}
  • Pancreas, liver and intestine sit at the navel. Below it are the legs, knees, hip and waist. Old age climbs up from the feet. Even on the deathbed the tongue works, since the fire keeps it going. Mars promises breath to the end. {{t:00:10:33}}
  • Lottery. He says that if your Mars is good, a lottery can hit. Mars is good when you have friends, and friends mean you let things go. Friends call each other donkey, dog and scoundrel, and nobody takes offence. If Mars is bad, you take offence quickly and never forget. {{t:00:11:03}}
  • Making Mars powerful means: wherever it sits in the chart, it gives that place's good results. {{t:00:11:35}}
  • In the palm, Mars has two mounts and Venus one. Hanuman is celibate and his ida and pingala are burnt away, so whatever goes into the belly is turned to ash. If Mars is set right you will lack nothing. {{t:00:12:05}}

The lottery claim is the speaker's. A lottery is a game of chance and the notes do not endorse it.

The body of Mars {{t:00:12:37}}

  • Athletes are made by Mars. A gym-goer has Mars in the chart: a steady, strong, tight body with strong bones. A body that spreads with age is a Jupiter fault. A body that stays tight has strong Mercury and Mars. {{t:00:12:37}}
  • Mars is the fire in a young person. If you cannot speak, have no confidence and feel your life leave when you talk, Mars is down. His fix: make friends, forgive, leave it. Otherwise you make your own life a hell. {{t:00:12:37}}
  • That is why people get Mars treated through an astrologer or guru. Then anger settles, cursing others stops, friends come and love comes. {{t:00:13:09}}

Mars the driver, between husband and wife {{t:00:13:09}}

  • Mars is the driver of our life. For a man, Venus is the wife. Jupiter is the husband ("pati parmeshwar", husband as guru). Mars comes between. {{t:00:13:09}}
  • When the friendship between the two is gone, there are divorces. The couple sit on a bed and fight over ludo. Children are born "in stubbornness", some crooked or disabled. There is slapping, anger, abuse. No friendship means Mars is bad. {{t:00:13:41}}
  • Mars sets whatever house it sits in into friendship. In the house of enemies, the enemy becomes a friend. In the house of fortune, fortune becomes your friend. {{t:00:14:13}}
  • Ram's Arjuna is Hanuman, and Krishna's Hanuman is Arjuna. The friend drives the car. If Arjuna says "Krishna, I am with you", Krishna drives. "Ram's work is done by Hanuman." Ram gets the name. {{t:00:14:13}}
  • Leaders are the ones with many followers, as friends die and get cut for them. That is the navel. To fix Mars wherever it sits, you must understand the navel and friendship. This holds for either gender. {{t:00:14:46}}

The remarks on disabled children are the speaker's rough rhetoric and are not a medical or astrological finding. The notes do not endorse them.
