18. Debt, coral and gambling {{t:01:17:13}}

Problem

Debt that does not go away. Mars aspecting the debt house (6th), in the main, chalit, nirayana-bhava or KP chart or navamsa, "means you will have debt". {{t:01:17:45}}

Cause

A Mars link to the 12th house or the debt area. It is the speaker's reading that debt, itself, is Mars's matter. {{t:01:18:16}}

Remedy

Do not fear the debt. Installments will come, he says. Treat Mars with a coral. {{t:01:18:16}}

  • "God has never let bread run short and never let us rise above bread." So expand your work, and you may end up in a second-hand car and rented flats, paying lakhs of salary every month to staff. {{t:01:18:47}}
  • Coral as he describes it. The best treatment is a coral on the ring finger on Tuesday morning, 5, 7 or 8 ratti, up to 10 depending on the person. Not 15, since that is an overdose: do not put 10 kg of flour in a 5 kg bag. Start small, then take more. {{t:01:19:19}}
  • Not very costly. Italian coral is the best, and any good stone will do. No cracks, lines or marks, no black marks, not crooked. Price depends only on these things. {{t:01:19:19}}
  • Money lent. If Mars is bad in two particular houses, money you give will be taken away. Do not lend a second time. You learn from two or three times that sugar tastes sweet, so do not do it ten times. Decide that from today you will not give money away for nothing. {{t:01:19:52}}
  • Greed. "There is a great deal coming, I will take a loan and do it." The navel is going in as you say it, so do not. Money will be lost. {{t:01:20:22}}
  • Online rummy. He warns against rummy circles played with loans, selling a kidney and house papers as stake. People lose because the navel is not strong and a scared navel is playing. "Just once and my loans will be gone", then 10 lakh becomes 15. Do not. {{t:01:20:22}}
  • For Mars, do not let your character traits slip, and do not avoid the coral. {{t:01:20:53}}

The speaker's debt advice is not financial advice. Online gambling is a serious risk. Take professional help for debt. The coral dose is the speaker's and the notes do not endorse buying or wearing a stone.
