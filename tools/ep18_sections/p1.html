1. The 50-50 rule before the class {{t:00:00:33}}

The class opens with a rule that carries through the whole Venus teaching: do not go above 50. When a person becomes over-careful about something, he pushes his energy above 50. Venus is the planet of two, and the speaker says the whole subject of relationships comes down to this balance. {{t:00:00:33}}

  • The Arjuna picture. Arjun sits in Krishna's chariot calmly, keeping his own balance and shooting arrows. Krishna drives and does not disturb him. Arjun does his 50 and the chariot runs. If Arjun thought "Krishna, you drive and also keep my balance too", it would not work. {{t:00:00:33}}
  • So "I do something, the rest is for the universe". This is a special problem in husband-wife, mother-son and brother-sister relationships: wherever there are two, balance is the main thing. If both hold 50-50 it is complete. {{t:00:01:05}}
  • Do not take yourself as completely right when you lecture a wife, boyfriend, girlfriend or child. Do not go above 50. {{t:00:01:36}}
  • What happens above 50. Rudrata (the fierce form) comes: Shiva's destroyer form, the cause of death. You hurt yourself or the other. How far the other person goes is not your business. A team is not made without two people cooperating. {{t:00:01:36}}

2. Venus, stories as signposts, and Bhrigu and Lakshmi {{t:00:02:08}}

Venus is the second planet from the Sun. The English name is for the goddess Venus, the Hindi name is Shukra. The speaker asks: did anyone see Jupiter or Venus, was there a Shukracharya, was there a Ram? Even the tortoise-and-hare story is a story. He says the stories are signposts to guide our lives, and whether Ram and Ravan were real or imagined is left to the listener. {{t:00:02:41}}

  • He suggests the ancient writers may have been great astronomers who were also astrologers, with a light given to know cosmic science and what we now call the occult. {{t:00:03:12}}
  • Brahma made creation, then there were mind-born sons (manas putra), who appeared from the mind, like Narad, who teleports. The speaker compares this to inventions: a sofa or a TV is made by the mind with no act of procreation, and we also call it a creation. {{t:00:03:44}}
  • In this setting, the sage Angiras had a son Brihaspati (Jupiter), a great scientist, scholar and astrologer. The sage Bhrigu had a son, Shukra. {{t:00:04:16}}

Bhrigu kicks Vishnu {{t:00:04:48}}

The story

Bhrigu kicked Vishnu on the chest. The mark is still on the Laddu Gopal idol. He was angry that Vishnu did not come out to receive him and lay on the Shesh Nag's bed instead. {{t:00:04:48}}

Lakshmi's reaction

Lakshmi, as the speaker says in a "woman's nature" remark, was angry: "my husband was kicked and he is told to come out". She cursed that she will not enter a Brahmin's house. {{t:00:05:20}}

The result

Bhrigu said he would write a book that brings her to his feet. After many curses and births (16,000 queens in Krishna's form, eight births), Lakshmi folded her hands and said she will come to the feet of the wise. {{t:00:05:51}}

  • The speaker's reading: knowledge can be very egoistic. This is Jupiter: "he says my welcome has to be done." People who say "I am very humble" get a blanket beating, so you must have the strength to protect your own field. {{t:00:05:20}}
  • That is why baba figures have Lakshmi at their feet. The world today has one obsession, money, notes, a rocket life. When Lakshmi comes into the pocket by effort it is one thing. If she comes onto the head, from fathers and grandfathers earning, it is another. For her to come to your feet you must become like Bhrigu. {{t:00:06:23}}
  • If she comes uncalled she comes grudgingly to the rich: they close the door at once "let us keep it in sacks". Then Lakshmi says she will come tearing through the roof and bring so much that the roof gets repaired too. {{t:00:06:23}}
  • Bhrigu's son is Shukra, the planet of this class, called Shukracharya. {{t:00:06:55}}

The remark that Lakshmi came to the feet of the wise and that babas have wealth at their feet is the speaker's reading of the story. It is not a claim to endorse any teacher.

3. Shukra turns rival, and Mrit Sanjivani {{t:00:06:55}}

The grievance

Angiras taught both Jupiter and Shukra. Shukra felt the teacher favoured his own son, and that Jupiter was made guru of the gods. Shukra's ego was hurt, so he turned against them and chose to be guru of the daityas. {{t:00:07:56}}

Gods and daityas

The gods are constructive and the daityas destructive. Kashyap's wives Diti and Aditi gave daityas and gods. The speaker says the Caspian Sea is named for the sage Kashyap. {{t:00:07:56}}

Shiva's help

The gods always won because Vishnu supported them. So Shukra worshipped Shiva (the Moon). Shiva has the Moon in his hair, Rohini nakshatra is in Taurus and the bull, Nandi, is his ride. Details come in the Moon class. {{t:00:08:27}}

  • The speaker's aside on mothers-in-law: if a daughter-in-law is clever enough to make her mother-in-law more than a mother, she gives her everything including the nine-lakh necklace. Women being sensible and a little clever is the foundation of love. A home where the woman is not upset and does not make trouble is called heaven. {{t:00:09:30}}
  • He says the mother-in-law (Rahu) can eat the daughter-in-law (Venus), but Venus can eat Rahu too, so the bride is not always the victim. Son-in-law and father-in-law have no such quarrel. {{t:00:07:25}}

The mother-in-law, daughter-in-law and "clever wife" remarks are the speaker's jokes and opinion about family life, not astrology.

The Mrit Sanjivani mantra {{t:00:10:01}}

  • Pleased with Shiva, Shukra took the Mrit Sanjivani mantra, which gives life back to the dead. The speaker says it is the Maha Mrityunjaya mantra used in a different way: Maha Mrityunjaya gives a peaceful death, or no death, or a longer life. Mrit Sanjivani revives one who has already died. {{t:00:10:32}}
  • The speaker says the Mahadev stories hold full detail. He gives the Maha Mrityunjaya opening line "Om Tryambakam yajamahe" and says the sealed (keelit) mantra is opened by prefixes and suffixes. With that use you can revive a dead thing or save one who is about to die. {{t:00:14:11}}
  • Shiva is called the destroyer (generator, operator, destroyer, with neutron, proton, electron), but the speaker says he is also the resurrector, as in the mummy films. {{t:00:11:02}}
  • In the Ramayana, the sanjivani herb revived Ram and Lakshman bound in the snake noose. {{t:00:11:02}}

The mantra story is a faith claim, and the speaker's comparisons (zombie films, the coccyx bone kept in Islam, cremation to stop reuse of the body, the claim that those traditions have no soul and a single birth) are his own descriptions. They are not verified here, and nothing in these notes endorses a claim about reviving the dead.

Shukra's philosophy: keep the dead alive {{t:00:12:05}}

  • The speaker says Hinduism, as one branch of Sanatan (ever-prevailing), burns the body so it cannot be reused, which is why in the old films the mummy or reviver is burnt. Christianity and Islam also say there is one birth, so no next life. {{t:00:12:05}}
  • Shukracharya's philosophy: whatever the situation, keep the corpses alive. The daityas die again and again, and he revives them. {{t:00:12:37}}
  • In the body. The speaker says if a kidney has failed, the liver (Jupiter) has the power to revive. The starfish regrows a lost limb and a lizard regrows its tail. Shukra and Saturn go with the lizard, and the tail is Ketu. {{t:00:13:07}}
  • The catch: Shukra cannot destroy the daityas, since then they would not protect him. He needs the daityas so that they die and he can revive them. {{t:00:13:40}}

What Venus revives in your life {{t:00:14:11}}

What can be dead

Your business died. A relationship died: love, spouse, brother, child. It is on the brink. Who revives? Venus in your chart. {{t:00:14:11}}

Example

People ruined by war, wealthy traders who came across at the Partition, started over, and stood up again. That is Mrit Sanjivani, the power of Venus. {{t:00:14:41}}

Even without Jupiter

If you are not wise and Jupiter is not working, Venus' placement in the chart can still revive the lost wealth or love through knowledge. {{t:00:15:12}}

The Partition-era example is the speaker's own illustration.

4. Venus and Jupiter, the two Brahmin planets {{t:00:15:12}}

  • Two planets count as Brahmin: Venus and Jupiter. They are rivals, and the speaker calls them evenly matched. The saffron flag is Jupiter. When Venus comes the two stay enemies. {{t:00:15:12}}
  • Colours of the chakras. The speaker maps heart-area vibrations: violet, indigo, blue, then green (the colour used in Islam, he says), then yellow, then orange (the swadhisthana chakra, and saffron is Jupiter's colour). Jupiter is of the gods and Venus of the daityas, two opposed categories. {{t:00:15:46}}
  • Spin. Jupiter spins round in about 11 hours (a day). Venus takes about 243 days, and is the only planet in the solar system that spins against the rest. So its way of thinking must be quite different. {{t:00:16:20}}
  • The speaker says the three Abrahamic religions have Shukracharya as their main source. He says there are no photographs of Allah as this is a different line of thought, with one birth only, "finish it in this life". Hindu belief is of rebirth: the Gita says the soul does not die and takes a new garment. Here the body is kept and revived. {{t:00:16:51}}
  • He says reviving the dead is not a small thing, even Jupiter cannot do it. Venus is very strong. When Jupiter is debilitated he produces a bad "Sanatani", when Venus is exalted she does better, and the other way round for debilitated. {{t:00:17:22}}

The speaker's statements that Hinduism and Islam can never be together, that Abrahamic religions come from Shukracharya, and the colour-to-religion mapping are his own religious opinions and are not endorsed here. These notes do not treat any faith as better or worse than another.

Jupiter

The symbol of the male (purush), stable. {{t:00:18:27}}

Venus

Nature (prakriti), active energy. It spins clockwise, as a clock turns. All other planets spin the other way. Uranus is a bit different because of its different spin axis. {{t:00:17:54}}

5. The slowest planet {{t:00:19:28}}

The speaker's claim: Venus is the slowest planet, and many of the differences in how women and men act come from this. He insists there is no "fight about speed" for men and women to have. {{t:00:19:28}}

  • A woman's hair stays black all her life and never goes white, he says, as Venus spins the other way. "No man can do it." He adds a joke that she would kill you with her eyes, then says shooting is wrong. {{t:00:19:28}}
  • The party example. A woman is told "come to the party", has said it for 15 days, is still getting ready at 6, and after getting ready says "my saree and lipstick do not match". He ties it to Venus being slow, 6 km an hour on the surface against the earth. {{t:00:19:59}}
  • He says women think more over a thing, while men decide quickly and say "finish it". Some women develop male qualities and reach high posts. Some men become so slow and lazy that the mother says "will he ever get out of bed, or will God lift him". {{t:00:20:30}}
  • Venus is slow, runs backwards, works gently and goes into fine detail. These are feminine qualities, which can be in men too. Jupiter is fast, big, and rotates in 11 hours: men are bigger. Venus is a little smaller than earth and is called Earth's twin sister. {{t:00:21:02}}

All of the gender remarks (hair never going white, women taking long to get ready, men being quick, women thinking more) are the speaker's own generalisations and jokes. They are not facts and are not astrology.

6. The shining planet: morning star, evening star and acid {{t:00:21:34}}

Why a female planet

The speaker says Venus traps everything inside it. It is the only planet seen at night as well as in the morning, the morning star and evening star. So a woman shines morning and evening and "wants to shine". {{t:00:21:34}}

Gas versus shine

Jupiter is a gas giant, so there is a lot of hot air, and big talk. Venus alone shines. Its temperature is about 450 C, because of the greenhouse effect and clouds of sulphuric acid. {{t:00:22:05}}

The body link

Sulphuric acid dissolves lead. The stomach makes such acid and would dissolve even a blade. Someone with Venus disturbed has no power in the stomach to digest anything. {{t:00:22:35}}

  • The speaker's line: a man hits with hands, eyes or abuse. A woman kills with eyes and tongue. Many officials come to him with wives who say "you came to him" and he says the question does not arise: you decide, and then the husband's name changes. {{t:00:22:35}}
  • If you say it once or twenty times or two hundred thousand times, the nagging will run. God gave a woman a tongue of great power. With wisdom it pours sugar syrup and puts everyone in her pocket. Without it, it creates enemies. So a woman has to be clever, smart and quick. {{t:00:23:07}}
  • When Jupiter's knowledge comes into Venus all is well. Without it the beauty and attraction fade. She is the brightest star, so she is always bothered "am I shining or not?" She goes to the gym with straightened hair and make-up, though she knows she will sweat. It is a feminine trait. {{t:00:23:38}}
  • "Is everyone attracted to me? Is everyone looking at me?" When this goes to extremes it passes 50 and Jupiter may be destroyed. If Jupiter wakes Venus and Venus wakes Jupiter, the inner vision and inner creation run superbly. Only two Brahmin planets sit in your chart and both are strong. {{t:00:24:08}}
  • Venus absorbs all the Sun's power. Mercury is nearest to the Sun but swings from 425 C by day to minus 150 C at night as it traps nothing, so your intellect does not hold things, and is confused: in the morning praise, in the evening abuse. A woman says "I have decided, I will turn doubt into certainty". Men who hold on firmly act the same way. {{t:00:24:41}}
