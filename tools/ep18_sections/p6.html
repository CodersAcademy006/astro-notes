The speaker's statements about autism, birth defects, muscular atrophy and the causes (couples' chart matching, marrying against family wishes, sex done with low "energy", rituals) have no scientific basis and are not endorsed here. His claim that "there is no remedy" refers to astrological remedies only and should not discourage seeking evidence-based help. The advice to avoid wedding fire rounds and to cut family ties is his own and is not endorsed.

About these notes

Source video: Jyotish Vidya Ep. 18, Planet Venus, Brajesh Gautam. Notes are written from the full transcript, section by section, and timestamps link to the exact moment in the video. The captions are automatic Hindi and garble some house and sign numbers, so doubtful details are left out. This class contains many remarks on women, marriage, sexuality, religion, health, fertility and disability. These are the speaker's own claims and opinions. They are recorded as his, not endorsed, and are not medical, legal or relationship advice. Remedies involving cow urine, stones and ritual are given as stated and carry no endorsement. No medicine amounts are given in the class and none are recorded here. A few passages (on harm done to a person, and on religious sites) were left out because they were unsupported or could blame people who were harmed.
