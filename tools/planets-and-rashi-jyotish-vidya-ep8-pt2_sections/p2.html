"Neech bhang raj yog": when a bad placement flips to a strong one {{t:02:18:12}}

A debilitated (nich) planet sitting in a naturally difficult house (the 6th, 8th or 12th are called the most dangerous, the 3rd less so) can flip from a weak result to a powerful, often protective one. His worked example: Saturn debilitated in the 1st house destroys that house outright, but the very same debilitated Saturn placed in the 8th house (death) does not bring death, it blocks it, because a malefic planet undoing a malefic house cancels out as strength. {{t:02:18:12}}

This is presented as a general rule in the talk, but the speaker is clear it needs a real chart to apply correctly, and not every debilitated placement works this way.
