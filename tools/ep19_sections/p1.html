2. How Venus behaves: slow, single-track, upside down {{t:00:03:39}}

Single track

Whatever lands in the head stays: a doubt, a suspicion, a stubborn idea, "I will die" or "I will kill". There is no second view. {{t:00:03:39}}

Very slow

Its rotation takes 243 days and it spins backwards. The speaker's example: a woman says "ready in two minutes", and in that time her husband watches a three-hour football match. {{t:00:03:39}}

Repeats and re-checks

Checks things 20 times, explains one point 40 times, readies and re-readies. Gets up at night to check that the door is locked. The speaker says the Moon's fault joins in here. {{t:00:04:09}}

  • Lazy but rich. A man chews betel, does two hours of work, then parties, yet has a Mercedes and a bungalow. The speaker says he has "descended" the Venus nature in his life, and that is why Venus's pleasure gives him luxury. Venus can also ruin you. {{t:00:04:39}}
  • It moves backwards. Only Venus and Uranus spin this way, so there are no seasons. A thought arrives and stays. {{t:00:05:10}}
  • Venus has one eye and Saturn gives the other. If Saturn is angry, the person goes blind in the mind. The speaker calls depression a Saturn matter. {{t:00:05:10}}
  • Upside-down talk that turns out right. A child or woman says something illogical, and it proves true. The speaker says such women use the left brain very little. The right brain is the divine brain. So respect for women is essential, or things go upside down, and quarrels between husband and wife follow. {{t:00:05:41}}

Venus is like a small artificial Sun. It is so hot that it keeps the Sun's heat. The Sun is life and Pluto is death. When Venus goes into Pluto's house, even Yamraj says "welcome boss", and gives a fine death, with a rally in the street. {{t:00:07:47}}
