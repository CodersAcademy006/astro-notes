Why Venus runs the big religions, as the speaker puts it {{t:01:54:51}}

He says a person with this chart pattern will research other religions and will not follow anyone blindly, and that Venus (Shukracharya) drives the Abrahamic religions because they say "all is okay, we are okay". He contrasts it with the Sanatan way where "all can exist together", and says Parshuram (cut it down) and Kalki (a mixture of all consciousness levels) are both needed. {{t:01:55:22}}

This is the speaker's personal and religious opinion. These notes record it as said and do not endorse it.

Same-sex orientation remedy {{t:01:57:29}}

Do celibacy or semen retention improve Venus? {{t:01:58:01}}

The speaker's own view on celibacy and desire. It is not a medical or scriptural consensus.

Which planets do gems serve? {{t:02:01:11}}

Question

Do we wear gems for strong planets or for weak ones? {{t:02:01:11}}

Speaker's logic

For the house that needs waking. If Saturn sits badly in the 8th but rules the 9th, the 9th (fortune) house must be woken. The stone works like a torch on that house. {{t:02:01:41}}

Example

Blue sapphire for Saturn, even if Saturn is debilitated, because it lights the 9th and the 10th. Saturn works on the person through effort. Nothing comes without work. {{t:02:02:13}}

Facial hair, periods, testosterone in women {{t:02:02:45}}

Should I avoid celebrating my birthday? {{t:02:10:02}}

Claim

Some say a birthday party brings trouble because of certain planetary positions. {{t:02:09:31}}

Speaker's rule

Venus gives children. If Saturn hits the 9th, or the 5th and 7th houses are flawed, a party does not suit. If the 4th is flawed, parties on a property or house-warming bring disputes. {{t:02:10:02}}

What to do

  • With a weak Moon, no party for a child up to age 5. The Sun takes over at about 10, Jupiter at about 16. {{t:02:11:37}}
  • With a flawed 4th house, skip the housewarming puja and the party. Go without a muhurat. {{t:02:12:09}}
  • With a flawed 7th house, no party wedding. With a flawed Jupiter, no party business. {{t:02:11:04}}

Doing "number two" work: what to decide {{t:02:12:41}}
