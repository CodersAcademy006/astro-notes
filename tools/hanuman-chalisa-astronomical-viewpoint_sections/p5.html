8. Ketu, Rahu and the body's "extra" parts {{t:00:51:29}}

He reads Hanuman's tail as Ketu, and treats anything that sticks out from the body as a Ketu signifier: a tail, an ear, external cartilage, even the sex organs and breasts in his account. {{t:00:59:56}}

Ear piercing, his claim

He says piercing a girl's ears calms the Mercury-linked chart placement tied to anger, and separately claims it can reduce asthma, heart or chest problems and urinary issues, and improve pancreatic function around sugar digestion. {{t:00:52:32}}

Rahu and Ketu as hair and beard

In his scheme, Rahu is the hair on the head and the moustache; Ketu is the beard and the lower body. Keeping both long is said to keep both grahas "checked," which he uses to explain Sikh practices of uncut hair and a covered head or beard. {{t:00:59:56}}

His general claim about control

Binding or covering hair and beard is, in his account, how a tradition "controls" Rahu and Ketu so they cannot damage Jupiter (wisdom) or push someone into sexually exploitative behaviour. {{t:01:02:32}}

The specific medical claims in this section (that ear piercing reduces asthma, heart, chest or urinary problems, or regulates blood sugar) are the speaker's own traditional claims. These notes record them because he said them, do not verify or endorse them, give no medical advice, and recommend seeing a qualified doctor for any of these conditions. The broader claim connecting uncut hair or beards to preventing sexual violence is also his own and is not endorsed here. {{t:01:02:32}}
