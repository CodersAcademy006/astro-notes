Why give water to the sun?Only while it is still visibly the sun, roughly before 10 or 11am; once it has "become a fireball" later in the day, he says offering water at that point damages Mars instead of helping. {{t:02:04:07}}
Why do career problems and strained relationships with relatives seem to show up together?He turns the question back on the person asking: an inability to accept the career you have, or to tolerate relatives, is itself the issue, not two separate problems with two separate causes. {{t:02:04:38}}
What causes a "displaced navel" (nabhi khisakna) or umbilical-cord-area trouble?He attributes it to uneven left-right energy supply through the spine, tied to the sympathetic and parasympathetic nervous systems, and says a correctly tied black thread around the toes can help realign it. {{t:02:05:41}}
Is tying a black thread on the legs for Mars or the navel useful?Yes in his account; black is said to absorb the most negative energy, though any colour can be used if matched to the specific need. {{t:02:08:48}}
What should women specifically do to keep the navel strong?Avoid doubt and suspicion above all; he calls this the one habit he says is most damaging to a woman's navel energy, more than any specific ritual. {{t:02:09:53}}
