8. A "trapped" Moon: overthinking, cancer risk, and the rain-water remedy {{t:01:17:05}}

This section includes the speaker's claims connecting internal emotional conflict to cancer. These are his personal belief, not medical fact. Nothing here should be used to explain, predict or treat any real illness; please rely on qualified medical care for any health concern.

The claim

An 11th-house Moon aspected by Saturn, Mars or Pluto, in his reading, can tip into the person "fighting with themselves," which he calls a risk factor for cancer. {{t:01:17:36}}

His mechanism

Fighting with yourself mentally is said to eventually show up as the body's own cells turning on each other; he cites austere saints who denied themselves everything as his examples of people who, in his telling, fell ill from this kind of internal conflict despite (or because of) their discipline. {{t:01:17:36}}

His recommendation

Favour the "middle path": neither full indulgence nor full self-denial. He states that some cancers, in his belief, can also resolve, whether through past-life karma clearing or a shift in planetary influence. {{t:01:18:38}}

  • He separates cancer's cause by planet in passing: Mars is linked to leukaemia specifically, with other planets linked to other cancer types, though he does not expand on this in detail here. {{t:01:19:10}} He adds that a person who stays easy-going and "flowing" rather than emotionally stuck is, in his claim, unlikely to develop it at all. {{t:01:18:38}}
  • A 6th-house Moon linked into the chart (he adds the caveat that this must appear in the main birth chart, not only the navamsa, unless the reading concerns a marriage) produces compulsive overthinking, over-talking or over-deliberating, with the exact flavour depending on which other planet is also involved: Mercury for over-talking, Jupiter for excessive deliberation, Saturn for sullen brooding. {{t:01:19:40}} A 12th-house link on top of this, he adds, brings constant suspicion and a feeling of being denied due credit. {{t:01:20:12}}
  • The remedy for a 12th-house (Ketu-trapped) Moon. Keep rainwater collected at home, and give generously to strangers rather than people you know, since anonymous giving is treated as the active ingredient. Liquids without the Moon's own water element, such as carbonated soft drinks, are offered as one category of anonymous gift; he is clear that this is not an endorsement of the drinks themselves, only of what can be given away this way. {{t:01:20:12}}
  • Other versions of this water remedy, depending on which house or sign is involved: water collected from a cremation ground's borewell for the most dangerous placements, well or pond water where that is more practical, or water from any open ground such as a park where the placement is milder. He adds that cremation-ground water can be kept for one to two years before changing it, while well water should be refreshed as it dries up. {{t:01:21:13}}
  • He repeats a broader principle for fixing a mother relationship under strain: avoid open conflict and crying matches with your mother; if the relationship is genuinely unworkable, he suggests symbolically "adopting" an aunt or other mother-figure instead and mentally releasing the birth connection, which he calls "cord cutting." {{t:01:21:45}}
  • A practical note on home repairs: a damp patch does not have to be fixed instantly if a real repair is already scheduled, since intention to repair is treated as enough to pause the negative effect; simply covering the damp patch with plastic will not substitute for an actual repair, though which exact corner of the house is affected (he singles out the south and south-west as the most damaging zones for this) changes how serious it is. {{t:01:27:27}}
  • Anonymous charitable giving generally, in his framework, is described as a way of pre-emptively "spending" the roughly 12 percent of resources he says is otherwise destined to be lost to misfortune, doctors' bills or deception; giving it away deliberately and quietly is framed as cheaper than having it taken by force later. {{t:01:23:48}}

The claim linking internal emotional conflict, austerity or specific planets to cancer and other serious illness is the speaker's personal belief and is not medical advice. These notes do not endorse it as an explanation for any real diagnosis.

9. Hypnosis, the Blue Whale game, and the double-slit experiment {{t:01:32:03}}

Returning to the manifestation theme from earlier in the class, the speaker argues that an unmanaged Moon leaves a person open to being "hypnotised" by outside influence, good or harmful. {{t:01:32:03}}

His example of harmful hypnosis

The "Blue Whale" online game, which he describes as isolating participants through a long sequence of small daily tasks before pushing some toward self-harm; he reads this as deliberately manipulating a person's inner "Moon" (mind) through repeated suggestion. {{t:01:32:35}}

His wider claim

Many everyday habits, from superstition-driven rituals to certain social media trends promising a quick fix for a relationship, are, in his view, a milder version of the same hypnosis: repeated suggestion overriding a person's own judgement. {{t:01:32:35}}

His recommendation

Notice when you are repeating an action purely out of fear or habit rather than genuine conviction, and choose consciously instead. {{t:01:33:07}}

  • He distinguishes mantra chanting from hypnosis: in his explanation, chanting done with genuine understanding works through "vibrational therapy" on a person's energetic (astral) body, not the physical body, so it will not substitute for a doctor's treatment of a physical ailment or a lawyer's handling of a court case, even though it may support a person emotionally through either. {{t:01:45:08}} He gives a specific example of a protective chant used only for emotional steadiness going into a stressful event, not as a substitute for the event's actual outcome. {{t:01:46:10}}
  • Tricking the brain. He describes a technique some athletes and dieters use: taste a sweet without swallowing it (he gives the example of chewing a sweet and spitting it out), so the mouth's receptors signal the brain that sugar has arrived and trigger an insulin response, without the calories actually being consumed. He offers this as a parallel for how much discipline it takes to engage with a craving without being controlled by it. {{t:01:46:10}}
  • The double-slit experiment, revisited. He returns to the electron experiment introduced earlier (see the manifestation section above): electrons fired at a double slit behave like a spread-out wave until a camera observes them, at which point they behave like discrete particles landing in one place. His reading stays the same: awareness or observation itself is said to change outcomes, which he uses to argue that focused attention, not wishing in the abstract, is what "manifests" anything. {{t:01:35:12}}
  • He is dismissive of excessive ritual observance as a marker of wisdom on its own, arguing that a person who argues and debates constantly to prove they are "right" (he contrasts this with wisdom, which in his view does not need to win debates) is still operating from ego, however religious their outward behaviour. {{t:01:42:30}}

The speaker's repeated use of the double-slit experiment and "observer effect" to explain manifestation and personal willpower is his own interpretation, not how physicists describe these experiments. The description of the Blue Whale game reflects his own account in the talk and is not verified reporting; these notes do not repeat further detail about self-harm beyond what is necessary to describe his argument.

10. Fixing your name: the nakshatra remedy {{t:01:39:53}}

His recommended long-term fix for a difficult Moon is to change which sign a person's "working name" activates, by choosing a first letter that falls in one of the chart's safer houses rather than a hostile one. {{t:01:39:53}}

The method

Each birth-chart house is tied to a small set of starting letters (via its nakshatra padas). Find which houses in your own chart are currently free of affliction from Saturn, Rahu, the Sun or Pluto. {{t:01:40:25}}

Why it matters

A name whose first letter falls in an afflicted house is said to keep channelling that house's trouble into daily life, regardless of what the name itself means. {{t:01:40:56}}

The fix

Use (or add, as a nickname or pen name) a first letter tied to one of the safe houses instead. {{t:01:41:27}}

  • He works through a sample chart on screen: of several candidate houses, letters tied to houses touched by Pluto, the Sun, Saturn or Rahu are ruled out one by one, leaving only a small safe set to actually name a child or oneself from. {{t:01:40:56}} He warns that only people comfortable with this level of chart detail will be able to follow the elimination step by step. {{t:01:41:27}}
  • He gives a specific example of a disruptive, defiant child whose first letter happened to fall in a hostile house; switching the effective first letter to one that sits with Ketu instead is said to calm that particular temperament, though he notes this specific fix is not always necessary, only situational. {{t:01:41:59}}
  • He references several unnamed well-known entertainers who are said to use a professional or "pen" name different from their given name, framing this as the same underlying remedy, though he is clear this alone does not guarantee success without the rest of the chart cooperating. These notes do not repeat the names he gives, since they concern real individuals' private chart claims. {{t:00:52:29}}
  • He gives a separate historical note (unconnected to any living person) that in earlier generations, a bride's effective "sign" was sometimes considered changed after marriage under the same logic, regardless of whether the two charts were formally matched beforehand. {{t:02:01:31}}

The claim that changing a name's effective first letter corrects a difficult Moon, including the examples involving real public figures' choice of professional names, is the speaker's personal framework. These notes do not repeat his claims about any named individual's real chart or reasons for using a particular name.
