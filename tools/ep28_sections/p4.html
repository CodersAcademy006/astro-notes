9. Eclipses, the Moon's shine and women's identity {{t:01:44:41}}

10. Ketu in the 1st house, and a second name {{t:01:48:22}}

Problem

Ketu in the ascendant is a dog with no identity. People say "what is he blabbering?". Useless worries, called donkey or fool. {{t:01:48:22}}

Logic

Ketu has no head, so which head will it put on? The Moon and Sun are the ones that Rahu and Ketu eat, so remedies come from these two. {{t:01:48:54}}

Remedy

Keep two names. The speaker says a second name activates a "new Moon", gives a second identity, and often works better than the first. {{t:01:48:54}}
