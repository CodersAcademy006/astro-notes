12. Questions from the class {{t:02:07:43}}

Cool Rahu with water on the head, but the Moon is also bad {{t:02:07:43}}

Question

Pouring water on the head calms Rahu, but with a bad Moon the mind turns bad. What to do? {{t:02:07:43}}

Logic

The Moon is water, so the Moon's issue needs a different water step. {{t:02:08:16}}

Remedy

  • Look at your face reflected in a bowl of water, then pour that water at the root of a tree. Best is the tree auspicious for your 9th house. Otherwise a leafy tree with no fruit. {{t:02:08:16}}
  • For Rahu: pour raw milk mixed with water on the head, the "milk bath". Do it 3, 5 or 7 days, by chart, or once if you go by faith. {{t:02:08:48}}
  • Rules of thumb: 43 days covers both Moon cycles. {{t:02:08:48}}

A deity in a dream asking for a temple {{t:02:08:48}}

The speaker says dreams come from the "munna" (the mind). The urge to build a place of dharma comes from Jupiter and Ketu. He advises paying a good astrologer to check your planets before acting, as a mistake cannot be undone. {{t:02:09:20}}

Earrings and metal for Ketu {{t:02:09:20}}

  • The ears match the 3rd and 11th houses (left and right ear). Choose the metal by the sign that sits there. {{t:02:09:51}}
  • Warning: if a bad Sun sign (sign 7) sits there and you wear gold earrings, you may get ear discharge for life, or kidney stones. Taking off the gold earrings stops the ear discharge, says the speaker. {{t:02:09:51}}

Observational claims by the speaker. See a doctor for ear discharge or kidney stones.

Burying a cooked chapati {{t:02:10:21}}

The cooked roti is Venus. If Venus is very suppressed in the chart, it is buried in the ground, with the sign checked and not the 7th house. Bury it where no building or tree shadow falls. Wedding tent grounds and big Dussehra or Ramlila grounds work, since the shade changes through the day and night is dark anyway. "You have to do this much austerity. Lives change with the right remedy." {{t:02:10:54}}

Ayurveda: no meat or liquor, sleep on time {{t:02:11:27}}

  • The speaker says Ayurveda is Mercury, so it suits a person whose issue is Mercury. {{t:02:11:57}}
  • The body heals itself. The body-brain is different from the head, and Ketu is that healing ability. When Rahu is in trouble, the body is too. {{t:02:12:29}}
  • Ayurveda's no-liquor, no-meat advice is for Mercury and Saturn faults, both neutral-gender planets. When Ketu joins Saturn it behaves like Saturn. Liquor makes bile (pitta), which hurts a pitta body. {{t:02:12:59}}
  • Those who sleep on time and never drink still fall ill, so it is not a general rule. If you have already made a mess with liquor or cigarettes, you must stop, since they raise tamas. {{t:02:14:32}}
  • Great scholars in other faiths never read scriptures. The Gita is a way of life about human consciousness, with no chanting required. A mantra is a separate science that sets a frequency with a particular deity. {{t:02:15:04}}
  • Girish, a devotee of Krishna, was a drinker who visited courtesans, as the speaker tells it. "There is no specification. It is about planets. Everyone's DNA, body and mindset differ." {{t:02:15:34}}

Commanding a medicine to work {{t:02:16:07}}

Do not meddle with it, let it do its work. "A medicine has no ears, it has no Ketu. A snake has no ears either. It will do what it does." {{t:02:16:37}}

If the chart says negative but the person has full faith {{t:02:17:08}}

The speaker says what is written, about 12 percent, will happen. He asks who has changed it: Ram and Krishna did not. Ramakrishna Paramhansa and Ramana Maharshi both died of cancer. Kakbhushundi saw the Mahabharata 14 times and the Ramayana 12 times, and it stays the same. He mentions Doctor Strange, who saw 14 million futures and found one path, and notes "everything has a payment". {{t:02:17:08}}

Silver or gold Lakshmi idol or temple at home {{t:02:18:10}}

Problem

A gold or silver idol or temple at home. The speaker says the outcome is a "great Jupiter fault", with "hell", and couples who sleep with their faces turned away, or divorce. {{t:02:18:10}}

Logic

Lakshmi is Venus. Gold is Jupiter. Jupiter and Venus are enemies, so mixing them destroys love. {{t:02:18:42}}

Use instead

Clay idols, or a wooden temple. Offerings of peace go to the temple outside, not at home. A silver one can still be managed, gold is very hard. The Golden Temple is outside, so it is different. {{t:02:19:13}}

Do not cut hair or nails on certain days? {{t:02:19:43}}

  • The speaker says that if you follow an elder's rule you have taken his head, and your Ketu does the work, not your Rahu. If you follow it, follow it. If not, then ignore it. {{t:02:19:43}}
  • Hair loss and nail rot are Ketu faults, and he says Rahu eats the hair because of Ketu. {{t:02:20:45}}
  • Check the Navamsa as well, since Ketu changes there. Vargottama is rare. A wife is the lagna chart, a girlfriend the Navamsa, so look at both. {{t:02:21:16}}

Pushya nakshatra and dreams of deities {{t:02:21:46}}

The speaker says he has not reached nakshatras yet. First the 1st, 5th and 9th signs' nakshatras need to be set. {{t:02:22:17}}

Tuesday, meat and Hanuman {{t:02:22:17}}

  • Mars is red meat and red wine, blood, surgery and cutting tools. Needles and scissors are Ketu, Mars's tail. {{t:02:22:48}}
  • There are a negative Mars and a positive Mars, like Indra and Hanuman. If you are linked to Hanuman and your mind holds that eating meat on Tuesday is sin, then do not eat it. It will hurt "100,000 percent". {{t:02:23:19}}
  • If your Mars is bad in the chart and you eat red meat or red wine, even skipping Tuesday will not save you. {{t:02:23:49}}
  • If you claim to be Hanuman's devotee, you should be celibate, and the speaker jokes that Hanuman will beat you first. Respect your friends, who are like Hanuman. Conquer ego, do not speak loudly, respect women, your mother and sisters. {{t:02:24:21}}
  • "Right and wrong, good and bad do not exist." He says he said it only as a hint, and asks nobody to start a following over it. {{t:02:24:53}}
  • Balance Rahu. Ketu has no idea what went in. Eat and drink what you like, and keep your conduct right. {{t:02:25:25}}

Dreams {{t:02:25:25}}

  • If parents or ancestors appear in a dream and ask for nothing, ignore it. Fold your hands. Even frightening dreams: "just ignore". {{t:02:25:56}}
  • Observe this: in dreams you often run around looking for a toilet. That is your body asking you to get up. This is witness mind. The child does not know this, so the body does not wake. {{t:02:25:56}}
  • A dream of a chain being pulled can be a real pull. Dreams are the mind's ("munna"), and the body has its own hidden dream. {{t:02:26:27}}
  • When you wake, observe the first thought the mind brings. Ignore all dreams, whether seen with eyes open or closed. {{t:02:26:58}}
