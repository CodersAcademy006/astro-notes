1. Rahu and Ketu are one pair: head and body {{t:00:00:33}}

This class finishes the Rahu remedies that were left over from the last week, and then moves to Ketu. The speaker's starting point: Rahu and Ketu are not two separate things. They are one position, the head and the trunk of one body, made by the Sun and the Moon.

Rahu fault

Life energy (the Sun side) goes wrong. The head jams: you are sitting helpless, the mind runs but the body does not. {{t:00:02:06}}

Ketu fault

The Moon side goes wrong. The water element and the body suffer: food and surroundings are fine but the body does not cooperate. {{t:00:02:06}}

Why a pair

The Sun is the father and the gods. The Moon is the mother and the ancestors. Water is life, and the Sun is what runs the skull. Eclipses in life come when the two fall out of step. {{t:00:01:03}}

  • The story as the speaker reads it. Vishnu cut the neck of the demon Svarbhanu. The head stayed as Rahu and the trunk became Ketu. Vishnu is Jupiter. The speaker places Jupiter on the sacral (swadhisthana) chakra, Ketu in sign 12 and Rahu in sign 6. {{t:00:02:38}}
  • Rahu sits next to the Sun and swallows it, so the skull jams. A solar eclipse means life energy stops, which is why you must not look at the Sun with the naked eye during one. {{t:00:03:10}}
  • Light and colour logic. Ultraviolet (beyond violet) is Rahu's. Infrared (beyond red) is Ketu's. Both are dangerous, so the two are called demons. Red is the longest wavelength and the "attraction" colour, so red lights, red clothes, red bindi. Red is also the base (muladhara) chakra. {{t:00:03:42}}
  • Both must be managed or they "make your life mad". {{t:00:04:13}}

Rahu: a head with no body {{t:00:04:43}}

  • People in a Rahu period feel helpless: the head spins and the body does not move. Rahu is eating the Sun, so there is no direction. {{t:00:04:43}}
  • Rahu Kaal. The speaker says this is why people avoid starting things in Rahu Kaal: the head cannot think. His example is a car accident, where the head jams and does not let the body move. {{t:00:05:14}}
  • Rahu pumps the "nectar" (amrit) upward from the chakra at the crown. The Moon (sign 4) is given the job of keeping that nectar inside the body. {{t:00:05:47}}

Ketu: a body with no head {{t:00:06:18}}

  • Ketu blocks the lower chakra so the nectar does not leak downward. If Ketu is disturbed, the lower airs fail: constipation if the apana (outgoing) air is blocked, loose motions if Ketu is gone. Upward, vomiting. "Neither food nor talk digests". {{t:00:06:18}}
  • A headless planet cannot see, speak or hear. Its picture is the dog on a leash: the body goes where the owner pulls. The speaker says we all do this in life, following a partner, friend or leader like a crowd. {{t:00:06:50}}
  • That same dog went with Yudhishthira to Dharmaraj. {{t:00:07:21}}

Husband and wife, the seesaw {{t:00:09:25}}

  • A husband and wife are Rahu and Ketu. If one is happy, the other is happy. One happy and one unhappy is impossible. If Rahu gets too happy, Ketu is ruined. When one says "to hell with you", one side is too high. {{t:00:09:25}}
  • Manifestation happens between Rahu and Ketu. Every wish, business, house, love, a child's marriage, is a manifestation. It works like a seesaw: one pan rises, the other drops. If both are level, the chart balances. {{t:00:09:55}}
  • Level means a sound mind. If it tilts, you wish for a lot and nothing happens. {{t:00:10:27}}

Why 18 and 7 years {{t:00:11:32}}

  • The Moon's cycle is about 27 to 29 days (the menstrual cycle follows it). A man has the same cycle but without blood loss, so it is not seen. {{t:00:11:32}}
  • The lunar and solar eclipse cycle repeats about every 18 years, so the Rahu dasha is 18 years. Ketu is 7: seven steps at marriage, seven days, seven sages, seven vows. The first nakshatra, Ashwini, starts with Ketu. {{t:00:12:03}}

The speaker's reading. Dasha lengths come from classical Vimshottari tables, and the eclipse-cycle link is his explanation.

2. Balancing the seesaw: witness mind and serving {{t:00:12:34}}

Problem

Everyone feels unfulfilled: no money, no fun, no bodily comfort, pain, accidents. All of that is a Ketu that is suffering. {{t:00:13:06}}

Cause and logic

If Rahu holds a witness mind (sakshi bhav), it stays level, neither up nor down. Then Ketu balances too. Once Ketu is balanced, you are both the greatest enjoyer and the greatest yogi. {{t:00:13:06}}

Remedy

  • Do something and forget it: "do good and throw it in the well" (the line painted on trucks). {{t:00:14:09}}
  • Give a share: a tenth (dashvand in Sikhism), zakat in other faiths, an eighth in the speaker's reading of Sanatan. {{t:00:14:09}}
  • Before eating, bow to the plate. Take out three portions: cow (Venus), dog (Ketu), crow (Saturn). Set aside a portion for a guest. {{t:00:14:40}}
  • The guest is Ketu. A wandering guest, a saint or dervish, pleases Ketu fully. Ketu says "give". With Ketu well placed you do not need to earn but to spend, and two extra hands come to push your car. The speaker calls it "jugaad zindabad". {{t:00:15:13}}
  • Keep the line level. Once Ketu is balanced the head goes to witness mode. You will not have ego or madness, and you "see no evil, think no evil, speak no evil". Quarrels end, since they start from "I heard this, you said that, I saw it". {{t:00:16:15}}
  • What you see can be an illusion. The eyes are the Sun, and Sun in eclipse means vision clouded. When Rahu disturbs the chart, doubt what you see. {{t:00:16:47}}
  • If witness mind does not switch on, switch on Ketu through service (seva): clean shoes at a gurdwara, tidy things at a temple. If you steal there, Ketu is destroyed, and Rahu rises: "I will kill everyone". {{t:00:17:18}}
  • Do not overdo it. If you give too much in slavery, the pan goes too low and Rahu too high. You become a servant, depressed, with blind eyes and deaf ears. You need a little Sun and a little Moon, not zero of either. {{t:00:17:50}}

Relationships as a Rahu-Ketu pair {{t:00:18:23}}

Mother and father, husband and wife, worker and owner, two brothers: if the pair is set, there is no misunderstanding and no lack of service. Ketu owns the body and bodily pleasures, including sex, earning, even theft and giving food and clothes. {{t:00:18:23}}

Doubt and suspicion are Ketu {{t:00:18:53}}

A person with no head works and serves 24 hours and never uses the brain. In the end they doubt: "is someone standing behind me? Is there a ghost?" Skin, bodily pleasure and sex are Ketu's. If the body is fine and you are not mad about a rasgulla, Rahu is balanced, and anger and greed come under control. {{t:00:19:55}}

Rahu is the snake's head, Ketu its tail, Saturn the whole snake {{t:00:20:26}}

The picture

Head with poison is Rahu. The jewel on the cobra's head is Rahu's. The tail is Ketu. A skilled worker (head) is Rahu, an unskilled one (brick, stone) is Ketu. All workers are Saturn. {{t:00:20:26}}

Cause

If you abuse a labourer or beggar on the street, Jupiter goes down, and then Ketu goes down. Saturn, the god of justice, says "I am both Rahu and Ketu" and arranges the same insult back at you. {{t:00:20:57}}

Remedy

Do not abuse workers. If you insult a woman friend, trouble comes through a woman. If a male friend, through a man. A government post or a third person can hit back. {{t:00:21:27}}

  • Rahu's dasha bites: sudden news that "someone has stung you". A trick or betrayal, or a thought so wild that your mind bursts. {{t:00:21:58}}
  • A snake can crush with its tail, but without the head and poison you can slip out of it. If the Ketu tail is down and Rahu holds you, the body decays slowly and nothing works: business, love, relations. {{t:00:22:29}}
  • The common home complaint is a daughter-in-law who feels like a maid, a husband who earns "like a servant", and a mother who says the bride has made a servant of her. "All have Rahu running, nobody has knowledge." {{t:00:23:01}}

The Jupiter fix: a guru {{t:00:23:32}}

  • Vishnu (Jupiter) cut the head from the trunk. The Sun and Moon said "these are Rahu and Ketu". So the pair are the enemies of the Sun and Moon, and when they move they eat the body's life and blur the intellect. {{t:00:23:32}}
  • Fix: find a guru. Jupiter sets them straight. When Rahu sits with Jupiter, either Rahu swallows Jupiter and fills it with dirty thoughts, or Jupiter straightens Rahu: "walk straight". {{t:00:24:05}}
  • If you give the Ketu body the head of the Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury or Jupiter, it behaves like that planet. Ketu is the "slave" that does as told. So fit a good head on it. {{t:00:24:35}}
  • When both are set: great yogi and great enjoyer, "drinking and still speaking knowledge, yet watching porn", a fearless person who still knows everything. {{t:00:25:08}}
  • The speaker's example: old leaders running wars. Whose body is used, he says, does not know what is going on. Whose head is used is a powerful planner with Rahu and witness mind. {{t:00:25:40}}

The remark on named political leaders is the speaker's opinion and is not astrological analysis.

Ketu makes things, and gives liberation {{t:00:26:12}}

  • Everything around us, sofa, cupboard, building, dog, cat, is made of atoms. Making is Ketu. Rahu should think rightly and then Ketu delivers. If Rahu thinks too much, Ketu dies. So do not make preliminary plans, do not go to the future, do not chase the result. {{t:00:26:12}}
  • Ketu gives all manifestation: a husband in your hands, a pile of notes, a good maid, a long car. {{t:00:27:15}}
  • Those who have all of it then ask who will give peace and liberation, and crowds go to a baba. The speaker says Ketu gives that too. {{t:00:27:46}}
  • He says every planet is equally important, like each room of a house, and there is no thumb rule. You must work on combinations. {{t:00:28:18}}
