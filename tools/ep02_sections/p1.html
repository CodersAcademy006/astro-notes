1. No number is good or bad: belief and body-mind sync {{t:00:00:00}}

The second class of the series opens with one rule: your belief system decides how the chart lands. The speaker says about 12 percent of life is fixed by the birth chart, and the other 72 percent is how your mind and body respond to it. When the two match, he calls it body and mind in sync. {{t:00:00:00}} {{t:00:18:51}}

Problem

People are scared of numbers: house number 4, house number 7, house number 8, a birth date of the 8th, a name that adds up wrong. Social media feeds this. {{t:00:13:01}} {{t:00:13:32}}

Cause (speaker's view)

Once you decide a number is bad, the subconscious is trained on it and every small trigger fires the old belief. Those with a troubled Rahu are the ones who believe all they hear. {{t:00:17:49}} {{t:00:18:19}} {{t:00:13:01}}

Remedy

Drop the fear. "No number is dirty or clean." Hold a clean belief in the deity or guide that suits you, and let body and mind move together. {{t:00:19:22}} {{t:00:19:54}}

  • Belief steers the help. If you believe Ram will help, Ram does, not Krishna. If you have decided a local saint will help, that one does, because your wire is connected to him. {{t:00:00:00}} {{t:00:18:51}}
  • Body-mind sync is like Bluetooth. If your chart says "work freely and you will rise like a rocket" and you insist on working harder, success drops. Sync yourself to the chart. The better your connection with God, the fewer doubts you have. {{t:00:19:22}}
  • Doubt trains the body. A person who vows "my stomach hurts because of this number" gets a stomach that hurts. Your mother-in-law, an uncle or an aunt becomes the trigger and the body fires on cue. {{t:00:18:19}}
  • The fear can vanish in a moment. A person who sees death in a flash and then gets one injection says "what death, what Yamraj" half an hour later. The speaker says this is how fast the base mind ("Munna") changes. {{t:00:14:36}}
  • Matching scores. He says a "36 of 36" marriage match was claimed for Ram and Sita, but that nobody can get 36 and above 35 is not seen. Rama and Ravana also passed through the same Saturn period (sade sati). {{t:00:00:32}} {{t:00:19:54}}
  • No one is simply good or bad. A daughter can be lovely and a daughter-in-law awful, a mother great and a wife poor. It depends on whose mind is judging. {{t:00:21:26}}

The claim about "36 gunas" and the remarks on numerology are the speaker's opinion. House numbers and name changes are treated here only as his view.
