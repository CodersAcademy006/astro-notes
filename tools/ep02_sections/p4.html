The guru comes to visit {{t:01:17:04}}

When the guru (Jupiter) comes to the house, Mercury, the daughter of the house, receives him. Mars, the commander, serves. The Moon, the mother, thinks of his feet, cooks and welcomes him with a plate of flowers. Even the Sun, the king, gets up from his seat and says "come, Guruji". The speaker's point is that Jupiter outranks the rest. {{t:01:16:32}} {{t:01:17:04}}

Women's fasts {{t:01:10:46}}

The speaker explains women's fasts (for example Karwa Chauth, kept on the fourth day of the Moon, when the Sun is in sign 7 and debilitated) as a way to protect health: he says fasting makes the body consume its own dead cells, keeps the heart clear, lowers the chances of cancer and BP and sugar trouble. {{t:01:10:46}} {{t:01:11:17}} {{t:01:11:50}}

His remarks on women, on why they are told to fast, and on health benefits are his views on religion, society and health. They are not endorsed here.

12. Rahu and Ketu in brief: the head and the follower {{t:01:20:45}}

Both get a short pass here because Ep. 26 to 30 cover them in depth. The picture: Rahu is the head, Ketu is the follower. What the head decides, the body follows. {{t:01:21:16}} {{t:01:25:28}}

Rahu

Your head. It makes astrologers, thinkers, leaders, ministers and speakers. In its dasha a person is badly helpless, which is why it is called a "bad" planet. {{t:01:21:16}} {{t:01:21:47}}

Why the beating

The thrashing during Rahu is meant to polish and make you a gnani. If you run from it, you are made helpless. A Moon with Rahu gives a sulky, brooding nature. {{t:01:21:47}} {{t:01:22:17}}

Remedy

Take grace. A strong Rahu gets ancestors' backing, so you need not go to a temple. The speaker says "I never say do work": fix the child's planet and the home's Vastu. {{t:01:22:49}} {{t:01:23:20}} {{t:01:23:53}}

  • Rahu eclipses the Sun. A person with a Rahu mind is so clever that even the Sun goes quiet, and Jupiter agrees "yes, there is something in it". {{t:01:20:45}} {{t:01:21:16}}
  • In Vastu. Rahu is the place of ancestors (pitri sthan). Get their grace and you do not need to toil. {{t:01:22:17}} {{t:01:22:49}}
  • Do not clench your fist. Fighting at home activates Ketu and kills Rahu: "no one will listen". Strong Rahu means you say "bring water" and it comes. Your sit-and-wish becomes true because ancestors are behind you. {{t:01:23:20}} {{t:01:23:53}} {{t:01:24:25}}
  • Voice power. Rahu with the blessing of Mercury, Saturn and Jupiter gives speech power (vaak siddhi). {{t:01:24:25}}

Ketu: Ganesh and the follower {{t:01:24:56}}

  • Arjuna behind Krishna. The speaker pictures Rahu as Krishna driving the chariot and Ketu as Arjuna riding behind, using no brain and following what the head says. "Whatever Munna says, Tinku follows." All manifestation is Ketu's, so the job is to follow. {{t:01:24:56}} {{t:01:25:28}} {{t:01:25:58}}
  • Choose what to follow. It is easier to be Arjuna than Krishna, and even following a wrong thing sets your life like a rocket if Ketu is active. {{t:01:25:58}}
  • Ketu is a cloud. It aspects many houses (the captions say 3rd, 4th, 5th, 7th, 9th, 10th, plus its own) and covers the planet it sits with. With the Sun there is no faith in the self. With the Moon, clouds over the mind. With Mars it is Hanuman's flaming tail. With Venus the cow's tail, with Mercury a bird's tail. {{t:01:25:58}} {{t:01:26:30}} {{t:01:27:01}}
  • If you do not follow advice (someone says something, you do not act), your Ketu is damaged. {{t:01:27:01}}
