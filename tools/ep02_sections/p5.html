14. Kaal Sarp yoga and Ekavali: the "snake" fear {{t:01:39:46}}

Question

Is Kaal Sarp yoga real? Someone asks about it and about Ekavali yoga. The speaker says Kaal Sarp is the most "defamed" yoga. {{t:01:39:46}}

What it is

It is when all the planets lie on one side of the Rahu-Ketu line. Rahu and Ketu are always opposite each other. {{t:01:40:18}}

His rule

It counts only if Rahu and Ketu sit in certain positions (the four central, angular houses) with all planets within. Otherwise it is not Kaal Sarp. {{t:01:40:18}} {{t:01:40:50}}

  • The "break" (bhang). A planet that looks inside the snake may, in the working (chalit, KP) chart, have moved into another house. Then the yoga is broken. Two signs can change at once. {{t:01:40:50}} {{t:01:41:24}}
  • Kaal Sarp has affected great sages and ministers, and the fear of it has done more harm than the yoga. {{t:01:40:50}}
  • His practical advice. Do the ritual once at the Kalahasti temple and remove the worry from your head, or your mind will stay spoilt with "alas, Kaal Sarp". He adds "nothing will happen from it, but do it". {{t:01:41:24}} {{t:01:41:54}}
  • Ekavali. When Rahu and Ketu are placed elsewhere and the other planets are on one side, he calls it Ekavali and says ignore it totally. {{t:01:41:54}}
  • Once a planet is rightly treated with proper Vastu and stone remedies, the matter is set for life. {{t:01:41:54}} {{t:01:42:26}}

The speaker's definition is partly garbled in the captions, and his view of the Kalahasti ritual is his own. Check any yoga with a trained astrologer.
