1. Neptune (Varuna): the planet of grace {{t:00:01:04}}

This class is about the planet Neptune, called Varuna in the scriptures. The speaker opens with a complaint we all share: life does not run by our wishes, and we spend it begging for grace (kripa) from God, spouse, children and business. His answer is that grace has an address in the chart, and that address is Neptune. {{t:00:02:38}}

The channel opens with a standard notice that it does not promote drugs, alcohol or intoxicants, and that the content is for information and education only. {{t:00:00:00}}

Problem

Life is not going as we want. Parents, children, the world and work do not follow our will. We beg for grace all day, even for a peaceful death and for love, and it does not come. {{t:00:01:04}}

Cause and logic

He says "God" means the five elements (earth, sky, air, fire, water). When they are balanced they give grace and life flows. Neptune is the deity who gives grace, so the work is to understand Neptune. {{t:00:03:08}}

Remedy direction

Learn Neptune's character first. Then use the chart to find where it sits and what it does there. Later parts of the class give the practical steps. {{t:00:06:45}}

  • The three far planets. Uranus (Indra), Neptune (Varuna) and Pluto (Yama) are far away. Counting from the Sun to Yamraj, Neptune comes just before Pluto. The speaker calls Neptune the "god of hell" as well as of grace. {{t:00:04:40}}
  • Mind, intellect, grace. He gives rough shares of 12 percent, 6 percent and 4 percent for the "munna" (the little mind), our own intellect and grace. The transcript is unclear on the exact numbers, so treat this as his illustration. With grace, work moves at "light speed cubed" and you do nothing: a lottery, a good partner, a job. {{t:00:03:38}}
  • Krishna gives grace when you become Arjuna, meaning when you have learned the lesson. {{t:00:04:08}}
  • If the body falls ill, the mind should not fall ill too. The body recovers sooner or later. One who weakens the mind's strength also does not receive grace. {{t:00:03:38}}

Varuna, the sea {{t:00:05:10}}

  • In the Ramayana Ram told Varuna to give way to Lanka, or he would shoot an arrow. Varuna is the god of the sea. {{t:00:05:10}}
  • Standing water rots, so the sea needs waves. The sea drinks everything the earth gives it, and a heart as big as the sea drinks everything: no cholesterol, no heart attack, in the speaker's picture. {{t:00:05:10}}
  • All treasure is in the sea: ships, gems, oil, minerals, medicinal herbs. On land you must dig. Life began in the sea, the first avatar (Matsya) came from it, and the churning of the ocean gave 14 jewels, Lakshmi and Dhanvantari. {{t:00:05:42}}
  • The sea looks endless, and it is a kind of illusion because you cannot see where it ends. {{t:00:06:45}}

The water in you {{t:00:06:45}}

  • He says the pineal gland is 100 percent water, the brain about 90 percent, the body 72 percent, falling to 60 as we age and move toward death. {{t:00:07:16}}
  • Someone whose third eye does not work, because it has calcified into stone, does not understand things. The speaker reads that as no grace of Varuna. {{t:00:07:16}}
  • Astrology is not memorising. Do not just chant "4th house this, 6th house that". Look at the sea inside you. {{t:00:06:45}}
  • Manifestation courses. Vision boards and "think it, want it" courses are, in his view, dramas to get Varuna's grace. The sea is unlimited, yet people keep building cars and houses, 4 or 500 of them, instead of building peace, love and knowledge. {{t:00:07:16}}
  • He asks us to become healers who can give grace to others through Varuna. {{t:00:08:19}}

The health figures and the remarks on the manifestation industry are the speaker's own views, not medical or scientific claims we can confirm.

Intuition: the top gift of Neptune {{t:00:08:19}}

  • Intuition (inner knowing) is the most important thing in all of astrology. You say things you were never taught and cannot say who told you. {{t:00:08:19}}
  • A poor, barely schooled person who becomes rich is "grace on this fellow". He quotes the proverb that when Allah is kind, even a donkey becomes a wrestler. {{t:00:08:51}}
  • In the chart, Neptune makes the house where it sits as vast as the sea. The weak point: it has no limit ("sky is the limit"), so you cannot see the far end. {{t:00:08:51}}
  • The Sun and the Moon rise from the sea and set into it. Stories say it gives birth to them and swallows them. It is also the beauty of the planet. {{t:00:09:24}}

2. It gives and it takes: storms, wishes and the west {{t:00:09:57}}

Problem

When Neptune is displeased it makes life hell. After grace comes a slap. Sea storms drive people mad, and what it takes it takes thoroughly. {{t:00:10:29}}

Cause

He says we also ask for ruin without noticing: "may I break a leg so I can skip that visit", "to hell with this". The sea hands over those wishes too. So if you are sad, broke or hurt today, it is partly something you once asked for. {{t:00:12:03}}

Remedy direction

Be careful what you vibrate. Ask for good things clearly and keep the heart wide. Later sections give the concrete steps. {{t:00:13:37}}

  • The planet's own weather. He says NASA studies show Neptune is the only planet where wind blows against the planet's spin, and it breaks the sound barrier at about 2,000 km/h (he quotes 2,160). He calls them storms of hell. A Mach 5 plane would burst. {{t:00:10:29}}
  • So where Neptune sits in your chart it will give or take, and it evens out. Whoever gets into friction with it, it swallows. {{t:00:11:31}}
  • The sea keeps nothing: throw something in and it lands on the shore soon. It is already full and needs nothing. You can only take from it. {{t:00:12:03}}

Water takes shape from your words {{t:00:12:34}}

  • Every ritual needs a water pot (kalash) with leaves, and the deity is invited into it. Water is held in the hand to give a curse or a boon. Water takes shape in the body, and life follows that shape. {{t:00:12:34}}
  • Saying "may my child not be hurt" while your inner water shouts "hurt, hurt, hurt" makes the hurt happen. Ten paise of peace and ninety paise of misery is the pattern. He says twenty people asking "did you get hurt?" makes the pain grow. {{t:00:13:37}}
  • He says this works especially with mothers. If you tell your mother your knee hurts, it may increase, or pass to her, because she repeats it. He says 99 percent of the problem is with the mother and 1 percent with men. A mother multiplies, makes copies. {{t:00:14:07}}
  • Saying "he does not love me" over and over will make that real. Suffering is hidden because the sea dissolves it. {{t:00:14:38}}

This is the speaker's belief about speech and water. It is not a proven mechanism and should not stop anyone from telling a family member about a real health problem.

Indra, Varuna and the west {{t:00:15:10}}

  • Indra has everything, but also fights and gets knocked about. Varuna is the one who gives. He is the god of manifestation. {{t:00:15:10}}
  • Direction. In the home the west belongs to Varuna (after Brahma, the west is Mitra's). Indra holds the east and Yama the south. Looking at India, the countries to the west are rich and risen, and he credits Varuna's grace. {{t:00:15:10}}
  • Meat and wine are also his. He is the god of demons, gods, giants and humans alike, and says "I do not care what you eat or drink. Did you come to me properly, with a pot of water or with your inner water vibrating?" He gives what is in your chart: pain if you ask for pain, happiness if you ask for happiness. {{t:00:15:40}}
  • Temples that are called wish-fulfilling are, in his view, all a west-direction game. The home's west, subtly, is Varuna's, and it gives the home peace or strife. {{t:00:16:11}}
  • If you do not know how to ask calmly, you make your own life hell. {{t:00:16:42}}

The remark on meat, wine and wealthy western countries is his opinion. Nothing here is a reason to treat any diet or country as better or worse.

Where Neptune lives in the body {{t:00:16:42}}

  • Calves. In fear your feet freeze when you see death. Varuna is the area of the calf muscles. He says the calves of jealous people go bad. {{t:00:16:42}}
  • Heart centre with Venus. Varuna is placed with Venus on the heart chakra. He explains the thigh and the Libra sign (Neptune's, as he gives it) with the Scorpio sign near the navel with Mars. {{t:00:17:13}}
  • He compares the speed of the sound sent into the cosmos: from the sacral centre at the speed of light squared, and from the heart at light cubed. All three channels (ida, pingala, sushumna) work from the heart centre, the anahata, where you "throw a bomb" and manifestation happens. {{t:00:26:07}}
  • Kundalini. It sits in the base. When you are wise it jumps up. At the navel you see a golgappa stall. At the heart you feel love. At the third eye you say "I got the idea". If it does not rise, it makes the lower region hell. {{t:00:18:17}}
  • If you sit grumbling, your calves stay still. He says doctors ask us to walk. In his numbers, sitting burns 1 calorie a minute, walking 5, then 6 after five minutes, 7 from the 11th to 25th minute, and after 45 minutes the metabolism speeds up. The bypass vein for heart surgery is taken from the leg, and he calls that "Varuna's area". {{t:00:19:20}}

The calorie figures and the claim about jealousy and the calves are his. Ask a doctor about exercise for any condition.

What Neptune governs in the mind {{t:00:20:23}}

  • How wide your heart is: narrow in love means Venus is working, wide in love means Varuna is working. Unlimited feeling and sight beyond the five senses are Neptune's. {{t:00:20:23}}
  • Who owns what. Jupiter holds the science of astrology, Mercury speech, the Sun inner knowing, Mars the weight of what you say, and Neptune intuition. {{t:00:20:54}}
  • Words that come true. If what you say tends to come true without any ritual, that is Varuna. "Saraswati sits in the mouth." You thought two minutes ago of golgappa and hear the seller call out. This is how a thought comes true. {{t:00:21:26}}
  • Neptune creates illusion. If the future is invisible and nothing makes sense, Neptune has started to work in your chart. {{t:00:22:27}}
  • What to do. Leave the boat to the oar and think of God. Take the grace of the deity at the west of your home, the one set in your mind as your chosen deity. {{t:00:22:59}}

The brain's west, the medulla {{t:00:23:30}}

  • He maps the head: north, east, south and west, with the medulla oblongata at the west, the vision centre behind it, and cerebrospinal fluid around. {{t:00:23:30}}
  • Seeing the future suddenly, or knowing where a marriage will go, is this part of the brain. He says the medulla looks like a Shivling and all sensory commands go down the spinal cord from it. {{t:00:24:03}}
  • Red light is the end of the visible band and the farthest to be seen. Beyond is infrared and microwave energy. Varuna blocks you there and protects you. {{t:00:24:34}}
  • Protection. If your brain is working you do not crash at speed, you do not hit a tree skiing. For the troubles of life, children, in-law quarrels, neighbours, police, ED cases, court, the protector is Varuna. {{t:00:25:07}}
  • Up, he gives happiness. Down, he gives hell. {{t:00:26:07}}
