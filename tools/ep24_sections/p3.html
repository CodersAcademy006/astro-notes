Problem

Cause

Hatred and grudges make the lower Neptune work, so quarrels come "for no reason". Forgiving makes the upper Neptune work. Begging like a child pulling the father's coat for ice cream gets you a slap and a dropped ice cream. {{t:00:57:24}}

Remedy

Pray once to the deity in the west direction of the temple, fold your hands and let go. Do not beg. Change your outlook, live with forgiveness. {{t:00:56:53}}

  • The chosen deity (ishta). People ask him to name their ishta. His answer: keep the ishta "in your pocket" and love it, but do not ring bells you do not know how to ring. {{t:00:56:22}}

The sacral and the heart centres vibrate into the cosmos, the vibration returns, and the work happens. That is Neptune's work, and you are doing it from below if your faith is only on the surface. {{t:00:57:55}}

7. Story: the king and Mangeram {{t:01:10:20}}

  • A king with a vast heart kept a servant named Mangeram. Mangeram loved him and said "you are my father and my god". The king even slept with him in his room, ahead of the commander. {{t:01:10:20}}
  • On a hunt they got separated from the army in a forest and the king was hungry. Mangeram picked a fruit from a tree standing on the horse. The king peeled it and cut a small piece for Mangeram first, to test for poison. {{t:01:10:50}}
  • Mangeram asked for another, and another, and wanted bigger pieces. When one slice was left, he asked for it and then snatched it. The king said he could have him killed. Mangeram said he was ready, yet he would eat it. {{t:01:11:21}}
  • The king took it and ate it. It was bitter as poison, and he spat it out. "Why did you not say?" Mangeram answered: "Your hands gave me delicious fruit all my life. When one bitter one came, should I complain?" The king embraced him and named him his heir. {{t:01:12:23}}

This is how Neptune gives grace: if we can embrace the hard things he gives, we are given more. God gave us thousands of joys, and at a small sorrow we whimper like a dog. {{t:01:12:54}}

Practice

Forgive. After 20 good phone calls and 2 bad ones, do not turn to hate. A person who learns to take grace says "a bitter fruit, so be it". {{t:01:13:26}}

Example

A young person says "I lost my job". The speaker tells him to wait two months. He could not even take two months after years of no job, and then curses. {{t:01:13:58}}
