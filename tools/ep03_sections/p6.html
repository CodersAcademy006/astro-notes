The class title promises retrograde planets. The detailed teaching on retrograde is not in this video. {{t:01:45:29}} {{t:01:46:00}}

20. Cord cutting: the exercise, step by step {{t:02:09:12}}

Problem

A mother, father, aunt, sister or other blood relative keeps hurting you, through anger, curses or worry. {{t:02:09:44}} {{t:02:10:15}}

Cause (speaker's view)

The body's cord is cut at birth but the energy cord is not. A mother's worry or abuse travels into you. DNA links you to father, sister, aunt. {{t:02:09:12}} {{t:02:09:44}} {{t:02:10:15}}

Remedy

Cut the energy cord mentally while calm, thank them, and adopt a new father or mother figure. {{t:02:14:30}} {{t:02:15:02}}

  1. Sit with no skin contact with ground or wall; no hand on hand, no foot on foot. Use a wooden chair or asana. Be calm and balanced. {{t:02:12:22}}
  2. Close your eyes. Imagine a small, dim room of your design, with golden light (best). If you cannot hold it, do not do it that day, or the mind makes a duplicate. {{t:02:12:22}} {{t:02:12:55}}
  3. Put two chairs in the room. You sit on one. Invite the person onto the other, eyes closed. {{t:02:12:55}} {{t:02:13:26}}
  4. See a cord from your navel to theirs. Only for blood (DNA) relatives; not a friend or wife. {{t:02:13:26}} {{t:02:13:58}}
  5. Take imaginary scissors and cut at your navel and at theirs. Roll the cord, put it in a blue bag, and throw it into deep, dark space. {{t:02:13:58}} {{t:02:14:30}}
  6. Bow to the person and tell them to go back to their body. Call yourself back into your own body. Thank the room and end. {{t:02:14:30}}
  • The trade-off. After a cut, the benefits that flowed through that cord stop too, "good or bad". It is not fun, and misuse brings tears. {{t:02:10:45}} {{t:02:11:18}}
  • Calm first. If you do it while upset, you sit in the lower chakra and the cord multiplies. {{t:02:11:50}} {{t:02:12:22}}
  • After. Do not attach a new cord yet. Think of an aunt as a sister, give the rakhi or tika gift, and give your wife a gift, and good changes show in work, money and health. {{t:02:15:02}} {{t:02:15:32}} {{t:02:16:03}}
  • Tantra? Asked if this is a tantra act, he says yes, he does not know much about it, and defers it to another class. {{t:02:16:35}} {{t:02:17:07}}
  • He also says Shani and Shiva worship can hurt your knees if your ninth house does not allow it. {{t:02:16:03}} {{t:02:16:35}}

Cord cutting is a visualisation exercise from the speaker's tradition, not a clinical technique. It does not replace therapy, legal protection or leaving an unsafe situation.
