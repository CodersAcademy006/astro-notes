8. Mercury: sister, aunt, daughter, and your business {{t:00:33:01}}

Problem

Moon and Mercury face each other or look at each other in the chart. Mother and daughter argue constantly. A woman with this has trouble with every woman in the family, and business suffers. {{t:00:33:01}} {{t:00:33:32}} {{t:00:34:05}}

Cause (speaker's view)

Mercury is the daughter, sister, aunt and sister-in-law. A fight with any of them damages the Mercury side of the chart, which includes trade and shopkeeping. {{t:00:34:05}} {{t:00:34:38}}

Remedy

Adopt a new sister or aunt in your mind. Treat her as your own daughter with love, without lust. If lust arises, "handle it, that is just my Mercury". {{t:00:34:38}} {{t:00:35:10}}

  • Body. Mercury governs teeth and intestines. A Mercury fault brings dental operations, gut trouble, an unclean stomach, small intestine problems, and bones and arthritis. {{t:00:34:38}} {{t:00:35:10}}
  • Chakra. Honouring sister, aunt and daughter steadies the throat chakra (Vishuddhi). He links it with "Krishna consciousness" and the power to win the world. {{t:00:35:10}}
  • Mothers and daughters. Do not argue with a daughter. Do not let her know you are angry. {{t:00:35:10}} {{t:00:35:41}}
  • Stuck in life? If you cannot see a path, the Moon (mind) is upset. Get happiness through mother-type relatives: your mother, your mother's sister, aunts, on either side. Take their blessing. Your third eye begins to work, meaning plain common sense. {{t:00:35:41}} {{t:00:36:15}}
  • If your mother is truly bad. He says the "worst mother" can be cut off by cord cutting and you adopt a new mother figure mentally. {{t:00:36:15}}

Everything on relationships and body parts here is the speaker's. It is not medical or family-counselling advice.
