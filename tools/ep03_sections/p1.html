1. Prarabdha, reaction and the "bali": why remedies cost something {{t:00:03:06}}

The class opens with two questions: why do astrologers often have messy lives themselves, and why does a remedy cost money? Problem

Things happen to you that you did not choose: death, blindness, an accident, poverty. You cannot stop them, but you react to them, and the reaction becomes new karma. {{t:00:03:38}} {{t:00:08:22}}

Cause (speaker's view)

Remedy

Do not react (that ends the 12 percent). Pay the planet its sacrifice, best in money, rather than letting it take your time, body or peace. {{t:00:08:54}} {{t:00:07:20}}

The auto-ride story {{t:00:05:44}}

Mona needs to get to a market. An auto-driver agrees at once to a low fare. She is delighted and gets there on time. On the way back she is still glowing, and the speaker says this "happiness" was taken out of her quota, so a sacrifice is now due: the next driver refuses, then asks 50 rupees extra, and she loses ten minutes. In the second version she knows the secret and pays the extra 50 straight away to save ten minutes. The point: you can pay in money or pay in time. {{t:00:05:44}} {{t:00:06:16}} {{t:00:06:48}} {{t:00:07:20}}

  • A trick (totka) for borrowed grace. A person whose chart does not allow something can take energy on loan from someone else. To renew it, give a tenth (dasvand) or similar share of your own energy to a stranger. It comes back from an unknown direction, as help from unknown forces. {{t:00:05:12}}
  • Types of sacrifice. You can give money, body or mind. For a planet's treatment, give the donation that suits the planet. Money is the easiest. {{t:00:07:20}} {{t:00:07:50}}
  • Size. If a planet's bad period will last five months, give up the equivalent of five months of earned money and the planet "becomes negligible". {{t:00:07:50}}
  • The bribe comparison. A policeman who harasses you, a doctor, a lawyer facing a murder case and the planet all work on the same "bribe" logic: hand over the offering quickly. The lawyer's fee buys months of fighting the case. {{t:00:07:50}} {{t:00:08:22}}

2. Do not fight fate: stress and the 6th, 8th, 12th and 3rd houses {{t:00:09:25}}

Problem

You nag your child to study. The child's chart shows no study yoga (a Jupiter, Mercury, Moon or Saturn fault), so the nagging turns your relationship into hell. {{t:00:09:25}} {{t:00:09:55}}

Cause

Fighting what has happened is fighting your base mind (Munna). Anger, hatred, taking offence and arguing all give you stress. Stress raises the 6th, 8th, 12th and 3rd houses, which carry disease, death and hospital expense. {{t:00:10:26}} {{t:00:12:30}}

Remedy

Do not argue over what is done. Earn your child's love, not his marks. Slow the mind down so Mercury, Jupiter, Moon and Saturn rest. {{t:00:09:55}} {{t:00:13:01}}

  • Fight and body. If you curse yourself or anyone, you wake the "bad" planets: Saturn, Indra, Varuna, Yama, Rahu and Ketu. The body then gets negative chemicals, and the liver and kidneys suffer. {{t:00:12:30}} {{t:00:13:01}}
  • Detox. The plain way is meditation, which slows the brain. When you stop reacting, Mercury, Jupiter, Moon and Saturn get rest. The Moon turns through the zodiac in 27 days, so it is fast, and the mind needs slowing down. {{t:00:13:01}} {{t:00:13:34}}
