5. Movable, fixed and dual signs: the timing rule {{t:00:25:04}}

The twelve signs repeat a three-step pattern: movable (char), fixed (sthir), dual (dwi-swabhav). He uses it for auspicious timing. {{t:00:25:04}} {{t:00:25:37}}

TypeSigns (numbers)BehaviourVideo
MovableAries 1, Cancer 4, Libra 7, Capricorn 10Things start and keep moving; no stopping.{{t:00:25:37}} {{t:00:26:11}}
FixedTaurus 2, Leo 5, Scorpio 8, Aquarius 11Things settle and stay.{{t:00:25:37}}
DualGemini 3, Virgo 6, Sagittarius 9, Pisces 12Go and stop, go and stop.{{t:00:26:11}}

Problem

Work started at a wrong moment can stall, or keep moving in the wrong way. {{t:00:26:11}}

Cause (speaker's view)

The type of the lagna at the moment of the start sets the character of what follows, with no planet needed. {{t:00:28:16}} {{t:00:28:46}}

Remedy

Match the lagna type to the task, as below. {{t:00:25:37}}

  • Wedding, Diwali puja. Done in a fixed lagna (Leo is named, which comes at midnight) so that it stays. The four fixed signs are Taurus, Leo, Scorpio and Aquarius. {{t:00:25:37}}
  • Starting construction. Strike the first hammer in a movable lagna so the work keeps moving. A fixed lagna starts then stops; a dual one goes and stops. {{t:00:25:37}} {{t:00:26:11}}
  • Bringing a bride home. The bride's car leaves in a movable lagna and will not stop (in the old days dacoits and accidents were a risk), waits below the house, and she enters in a fixed lagna after the dual one. Once in, "nobody can remove her", even if the planets do not agree. {{t:00:27:44}} {{t:00:28:16}}
  • Bringing a baby home from the nursing home. Enter in a fixed lagna and the child stays. In a dual lagna the child is in and out all life. In a movable one, he will go off to America once he is bigger. {{t:00:28:16}} {{t:00:28:46}}
  • Safe journey. The bride throws puffed rice behind her, asking for Pusha's grace (he names Pusha as the deity of the Pushya star and a kitchen-area direction). To travel safely, throw rice or puffed rice behind your head. {{t:00:26:43}}
  • Lal Kitab. A system of "fixed houses" where signs are ignored altogether, so the speaker says astrology can be done in many ways, like many healing channels. {{t:00:29:18}}

The timing rules, the claim about a bride who "cannot be removed", and the puffed-rice remedy are the speaker's own teaching. Treat them as tradition, not rules of law.
