1. Why good people are "taken early": the plate story {{t:00:00:33}}

This class is on the Moon (Chandra, "Chanda Mama"), the only moon of the Earth. The speaker opens with a question from a viewer: why does God take good people early while heavy smokers and drinkers live on? His answer is a story, and it sets up the theme of the whole class: the Moon is the water element, the mind and the life energy. {{t:00:00:33}}

  • The question. A man never drank, never lied, helped everyone, and died young. Another man smokes and drinks and lives on. A wife warned her husband that cigarettes would give him lung cancer, and she herself died of lung cancer 20 years ago while he is still alive. {{t:00:01:05}}
  • The plate. Imagine a plate with paneer, dal, sweets, kheer, halwa and bitter gourd. You pick the best item first. Why would God not pick the best first? {{t:00:01:35}}
  • If guests arrive and the sweets are short, you spoil some of them so nobody takes them. In the same way, a person with a stain on the earth is not picked up by God. A clean soul is lifted first, like clean washed clothes a housewife sets aside. A soul that is not yet clean is sent back down. {{t:00:02:37}}
  • If you want to enjoy everything on earth in maya, you need some faults. The speaker says that being too "good" is not required to enjoy life here. {{t:00:03:08}}
  • He notes that every religion promises a pleasant afterlife (houris in Islam, apsaras in Hindu talk, fairies in Christianity) and jokes that the promises are lopsided against women. {{t:00:03:39}}
  • He says he is not against pilgrimage, only that having some qualities lets you live properly on earth. {{t:00:04:09}}

The plate story, the afterlife remarks and the explanation of early death are the speaker's own opinion and religious reading. They are not an astrological rule, and nothing here replaces medical care.

2. The Moon as water: 72 percent body, 12 percent mind {{t:00:05:42}}

Problem

Pain, weakness, a failing body, a failing home or car. The speaker reads all of these as the Moon (water) going down in the chart. {{t:00:07:51}}

Cause and logic

Body is about 72 percent water. On the Moon a 60 kg person weighs 10 kg, one sixth, and one sixth of 72 is 12. So the body holds 72 and the mind holds 12. The mind rules the whole life. {{t:00:05:42}}

Remedy direction

Set the mind and its frequency. Correct the Moon in the chart. The remedies come later in the class. {{t:00:13:07}}

  • People say "change your thoughts to manifest", but nobody knows where thoughts come from. A person with insomnia cannot stop them, and a song plays in the bathroom without being chosen. The speaker says this 12 percent mind power does not change, only the body part changes. {{t:00:06:14}}
  • Pineal gland. The third eye (Shiva's eye) is the pineal gland, said to be full water at birth. It calcifies and dries with age. When it dries, common sense goes. Quick, witty people have a strong one, with an open "portal" to the cosmos that gives answers. {{t:00:06:48}}
  • Water through life. About 90 percent at birth (rubbery body), 80, then 72 in youth, then 60 in old age ("sathiya gaye"), and then brittle bones. If the water element is good, the body is fit to live. {{t:00:07:19}}
  • A sofa, house and car are also "bodies". If these keep failing, the speaker reads it as a sign of a sick Moon. If the body has no strength, the Moon is going down and a person ends up in the ICU. {{t:00:07:51}}
  • Staying only good is not enough. Positive and negative go together like proton and electron. If you never pass waste you are ill. In the same way, anger and conflict must arise, and a person who says "nothing bothers me, I am a saint" is, in his words, off. He says anger not arising at all means you will "die like that". {{t:00:08:24}}
  • He names great saints such as Ramakrishna Paramhansa, Ramana Maharshi and Vivekananda, who died at a young age or of cancer. {{t:00:08:54}}

The cancer and early death explanations are the speaker's claims. They are not medical facts. The comment on saints and on a person who feels no anger is his view alone.
