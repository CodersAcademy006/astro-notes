Problem

After a child is born the parents' romance fades and quarrels begin. Money and cars may arrive, yet love is gone. {{t:01:57:29}} {{t:01:59:36}}

Cause (his reading)

From the mother's house, the father's house sits in the 6th (enemy) and the 8th (death). The pair become rivals after a child comes. Conception triggers these two houses. It is rare for the pair to grow closer after a birth. {{t:01:57:29}} {{t:01:58:02}} {{t:01:58:32}}

Remedy

Take both grace and blessing. Grace comes unasked, blessings must be taken. Improve the 4th house, or the marriage stays an "enemy and death" relationship. He compares the back pain of not bowing to people with bowing in temples. {{t:01:58:32}} {{t:01:59:06}}

20. Question and answer: threads, karma and what remedies can change {{t:02:06:58}}

Question

Do threads on the hand or foot clear loans or fix studies? Does individual karma not matter then? {{t:02:06:58}}

Threads have not been taught yet. Set your fate so strongly that it makes you do the karma. Prarabdha is what drives action. Knowledge teaches contentment: someone can be unhappy with 5 lakh and happy with 500. {{t:02:06:58}} {{t:02:07:30}}

On not acting

You cannot avoid karma. If action is due within 15 days and you wait, the planet will "chase you" on day 15 or take a body part. Even breathing is not done by us. {{t:02:08:02}}

Cheap and costly remedies {{t:02:08:33}}

  • Two kinds: very costly remedies that are out of reach, or cheap ones like a two-rupee thread, a small tattoo, throwing something in a river, burying something, keeping something in the house. {{t:02:08:33}}
  • The cheap kind is like borrowing a neighbour's spectacles: you have to keep returning to the pandit. The costly kind is permanent, like wearing glasses or a stone, "a hundred years' holiday". {{t:02:08:33}} {{t:02:09:05}}
  • He compares this with medicine: some people need a painkiller, some need surgery, and some need neither. For a person of high awareness, a light remedy is enough. {{t:02:09:05}} {{t:02:09:36}}
  • A little unrest is useful: nobody should stay perfectly balanced, because the earth needs movement. Do something constructive. {{t:02:09:36}} {{t:02:10:07}}
  • Remedies have limits and do not work in every place. {{t:02:10:07}}

Can a remedy change fate? {{t:02:10:07}}

  • He says remedies do not change the fixed part. They twist it.   • Fate cannot be changed: Ram's and Krishna's did not change, and the great sages could not alter the exile. Astrologers' own doctors die of what they treat. {{t:02:11:41}} {{t:02:12:12}}

The tree-marriage and "remedy twists fate" claims are customs and beliefs the speaker describes. They are not verified, and they are not a substitute for legal, financial or medical steps.
