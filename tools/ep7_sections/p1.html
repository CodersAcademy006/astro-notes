1. Why 12 houses, and why one chart gives different lives {{t:00:00:30}}

This class is about the 12 houses (bhaav) of the birth chart and how planets and signs move through them. The speaker begins with the frame: the sky is a 360 degree circle, the Sun passes through it in 12 months, so the chart has 12 houses of 30 degrees each. {{t:00:00:30}}

  • Rough versus real. In practice the Earth's path makes the segments uneven: some are 30, some 32, 28 or 25 degrees, though the total stays 360. He says this is the base of the KP method and credits Krishnamurti with predicting things such as a flight landing 11 minutes late. {{t:00:01:31}}
  • He says this is why he does not take a fixed 30 degree box, and why life has so many variables. {{t:00:03:05}}

Problem

Hundreds or lakhs of people are born at the same time with the same chart, yet live very different lives. Why did only one become Ram, or only one a prime minister? {{t:00:03:05}}

Cause

Birth luck, luck from your own actions, and prarabdha (what is already written) differ. Past-life impressions decide the level you start from. {{t:00:03:36}}

The example

A farmer's child and a landlord's child lie on the same hospital bed. Both rise by the same amount, but the first ends as a landlord and the second as an MLA. What matters is the level you rise from. {{t:00:04:07}}

The names of public figures used as examples in this class are the speaker's illustrations, not astrological analysis of their charts.

4. The houses are a body, and pain points to the house {{t:00:16:39}}

The speaker draws the chart as a person, or as a car with headlights, doors and driver seat. The houses are fixed. Signs rotate through them, and planets travel with the signs. Saturn moves through and hits or helps each house in turn, but the house itself never changes. {{t:00:17:11}} {{t:00:18:14}}

  • House 1 is the head and face, and the chart starts there. It stays your face whatever happens. {{t:00:17:42}} {{t:00:18:46}}
  • The other houses map to the right and left eye, shoulders, chest, knees, private parts and feet, going down the body in order. {{t:00:19:18}}

Chest, heart and knee

If a planet is disturbed in the chest house, the speaker says the person will have a chest problem. He gives Mars in sign 4 (debilitated) for the chest and, for a girl, ovarian cysts, and a Sun sitting in sign 2 or 7 for heart trouble. {{t:00:19:48}} {{t:00:20:19}}

Cause

He says the house also holds your work. A strong Sun in the work house lifts your work and also strains the chest. So if you have risen very high and fame has come, check the chest. {{t:00:20:19}} {{t:00:20:53}}

Remedy

Know the reading yourself and do not overdo it. The remedy list comes in a later class. {{t:00:20:53}}

The links from house to disease are the speaker's astrological readings. They are not medical advice. See a doctor for any symptom.

Worked examples {{t:00:20:53}}

6. The 8th house, grace and the south-west {{t:00:29:17}}

Problem

Love, money, friendship and work "die" in a life. Work does not get done, and the mind sinks. {{t:00:30:19}}

Cause

You did not see the guru, did not bow to the guru, and did not take his grace. Grace for the guru is Jupiter, and the end is Pluto (Yamraj) sitting in the 8th. {{t:00:30:19}} {{t:00:30:52}}

Remedy

Get a guide and take grace. Study occult subjects and the 8th house. In the speaker's sky picture, Varuna (Neptune) stands just before Yamraj (Pluto) and keeps death away. {{t:00:29:48}} {{t:00:34:59}}

  • Death itself backs off when you turn to wisdom (the "cult" or occult study). His example is the sage Markandeya, a devotee of Shiva, whom Yamraj could not take. Shiva is the Moon. The Moon is also Saturn's seat, so "Munna" (his word for the mind) is also Saturn. {{t:00:29:17}} {{t:00:29:48}} {{t:00:36:31}} {{t:00:37:03}}
