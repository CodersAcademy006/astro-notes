11. Ganesha, the water centre and the elephant on the mouse {{t:00:55:42}}

  • Breath is really taken by the water centre, below the navel. Birth, the union of egg and sperm, and all reproductive fluids are made from this chakra. If you learn to breathe so that you feel the heartbeat at the navel, he says you stay sexually active, with no urinary or sexual trouble, ever young. {{t:00:56:12}}
  • Ganesha is first among gods, worshipped before a wedding, house work or a child's rites. Lakshmi sits there too. Ketu is there. Nothing is thought higher than Ganesha and Lakshmi. {{t:00:56:44}}
  • Diwali. On the dark new-moon night Saturn is strongest. Saturn holds all tantra, meaning what is beyond the body (tan). Saints set the body so it runs by their will. Hypnotherapy gives a body part the order "heal" and it can. He says operations and diseases have reversed and cancers vanished, all by Ketu's grace. {{t:00:57:15}}

The claim that cancers vanish and operations reverse through bodily commands is his. It is not medical evidence. Do not delay cancer treatment.

  • Ganesha satisfies your Ketu, and from there he fulfils wishes. His head is an elephant's, his belly is big, and a mouse sits below. {{t:00:57:46}}

Eclipses and the child {{t:00:58:17}}

The eclipse

The Moon between Sun and Earth is the solar eclipse (Rahu's position). Earth between the Sun and Moon is the lunar eclipse (Ketu's). The Moon goes dark and ancestors' grace goes out. {{t:00:58:17}}

His claim

If the DNA is weak, the body's repair systems break. He says Ketu attacks children: no child is born, or one is killed. If conception falls during an eclipse and the mother sits with limbs bent, cuts with scissors or lights a flame, the child is born crooked. {{t:00:58:47}}

What he says

Check this timing before conceiving. Otherwise life goes with an ill child. {{t:00:59:18}}

This is a strong claim about eclipses, pregnancy and birth defects. There is no medical evidence for it, and it should not alarm or blame any parent. Birth defects have many causes. Speak to a doctor.

  • He says people who end up in old-age homes have a bad Ketu because their children do not want to serve them. Fix Ketu by right conduct: pleasure and sex with love and not by compulsion or force, exchange of food and things, and service to a stranger whose "head" you do not know: a sadhu, a saint, a dog. {{t:00:59:51}}

The remark that people in old-age homes have a bad Ketu is his. Many people in care homes are there for health or other reasons, and it says nothing about their character.

Why an elephant sits on a mouse {{t:01:00:22}}

  • It is symbolic: an elephant cannot ride a mouse. The idea is a portal in our body, called Ketu, that gives worldly enjoyment and liberation (moksha). Heaven and hell are duality, a drama. Ida and pingala are plus and minus: "got a job, lost a job". The third is "the job is in my pocket, it will come looking for me". {{t:01:00:22}}
  • Some people have offers of marriage at home and jobs and money coming to them. We chase. The reverse flow comes when Rahu and Ketu are on a level platform. {{t:01:01:25}}
  • Social media and Instagram pump Rahu up and bring Ketu down. Wisdom comes when the elephant rides the mouse. {{t:01:01:57}}
  • Technical point. The elephant (gaj) is the nakshatra Revati, the last nakshatra, in Pisces (sign 12), meaning the end and liberation. The mouse is the shape of stars at Magha in Leo, the Sun's sign, where the Sun gives the best result. The first nakshatra Ashwini begins with Ketu. Mercury owns both Ashlesha-type ends, so Ketu and Mercury connect through Revati. {{t:01:02:30}}
  • Life starts at the navel and ends at the 12th. "That is the beginning and that is the end." Some people die at one chakra and some at another. He thinks a class on which chakra one dies at would be lovely. {{t:01:04:01}}
  • Ganesha is the one asked to protect us from Rahu and Ketu, since they will not manage us and we lack the sense. Seeing the world, the body goes mad: it kills itself over what is bad or runs after what is good. We need a god with the wisdom to balance, and a big belly (Jupiter's grace) as the support. {{t:01:05:04}}
  • One Ganesha at home. People who put two or three have placed deities to balance Rahu and Ketu in four places, which he says are "demons of blood". Others make Ganesha the gatekeeper. Ganesha gives riddhi and siddhi, growth by two, four and ten times. {{t:01:05:35}}

His rules on one Ganesha at home and the mouse and elephant meaning are the speaker's reading.

Fate and a plane crash {{t:01:06:36}}

  • We need the intelligence of an elephant. Keep one Ganesha, one elephant. The mouse digs into the ground and is a son of the earth, so also Mars and Ketu. This is why the god was made with an elephant's head and a mouse for a ride. {{t:01:09:14}}
  • Serving Ganesha, the elephant and mouse also strengthens Ketu. A head alone, he says, is Rahu's correction and then it makes Ketu. {{t:01:10:17}}

Statues, doors and pundits {{t:01:10:48}}

Poverty, illness and foolishness come in. Put it inside the wall's last point and in a temple, not at home. {{t:01:10:48}}

His views on governments, education, the treatment of pundits and a "conspiracy" against Sanatan, and his remarks on communities are his opinions on politics and religion. We list them as his and do not endorse them.

  • Do not believe that a Haridwar dip washes sins. He says that is the third eye and pineal decalcification, which nobody wants to learn. Look at the priest or astrologer's own record before you trust them. {{t:01:15:30}}

Broken idols and gold plating {{t:01:17:02}}

  • Without Ganesha, Lakshmi is not accepted. Diwali's worship of both is needed. On that dark night, many notes can be printed by tantra, he says. {{t:01:17:02}}

  • Idols should be of clay, not brass. A brass or multi-metal Ganesha is not for him, though eight-metal ones with gold are fine. {{t:01:18:04}}

A fake Jupiter has been put on the jewellery. Artificial jewellery in general is fine, only gold plating is the problem. {{t:01:18:34}}

The claims on gold plating, idols and ill effects on the body and marriage are his. We cannot confirm them.
