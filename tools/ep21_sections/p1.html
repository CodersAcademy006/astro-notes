1. Pluto, the god of death, and the story of the two unborn twins {{t:00:00:33}}

This class is on the remedies for Pluto, whom the speaker calls Yamraj, the lord of death and of Dharma. The speaker says the earlier Pluto class left remedies untouched, and so this one covers them. He starts with the point that this god causes the death of things, money, love, relationships and finally the body, and that we spend a whole life fighting him. {{t:00:00:33}}

The two babies in the womb {{t:00:01:36}}

  • Two small babies in the womb talk to each other. One says life here is good: food comes, no need to breathe, no crying, sleep and dreams. The other says they must leave the womb since there is not enough room. {{t:00:01:36}}
  • The first fears that outside there is no food. The second says they will eat with the mouth and see with the eyes, and that outside they will get a mother's love and meet the one who gave birth to them. {{t:00:02:40}}
  • They are born. They live their life, and as adults they wonder whether there is another world beyond this one, with a creator they must meet. They say life after birth began with breath, heartbeat, waste and desires, so the next must also start. {{t:00:03:12}}
  • He says we spend our whole life on this one riddle, "what is the purpose of life?" {{t:00:04:16}}

The story and its message are the speaker's illustration for the idea of death and rebirth. They are a belief and an analogy, not something astrology can show.

2. Yamraj, the percentages, and a new house {{t:00:04:16}}

Problem

You cannot get a new house, car, job or body, or something keeps dying that you hold on to. {{t:00:07:23}}

Cause as he reads it

Pluto (Yamraj) is working, and an old form must go first. A person stuck in this is like a soul without a body (pret yoni), seeking a new home. {{t:00:07:23}}

Remedy direction

Set Pluto first in front of a guru. Under a guru's direction Pluto and death are controlled. Do not panic in Pluto's time. {{t:00:07:55}}

  • Praarabdh and the body. The speaker says about 12 percent of our karma is "written" at birth (praarabdh). He quotes the Gita: as one changes worn clothes, the soul changes the body. The soul (Self) is about 4 percent of our energy and leaves a body that is no longer needed. {{t:00:04:16}}
  • He compares it to a house camera you switch off once it is not needed. Sometimes Yamraj does not take the person even when the body is rotting, and that too is a torment. {{t:00:05:20}}
  • Yamraj does not kill. He takes the soul from one robe to put it in another. The 12 percent fixed karma means his timeline is fixed. Death arrives suddenly and unannounced. {{t:00:05:52}}
  • He says the 6 percent is knowledge energy ("Krishna consciousness"), and the 4 percent Self has no give and take. The soul moves from body to body, even into a parrot or stone, and Yamraj works there too. He takes even Indra and Brahma. {{t:00:06:22}}
  • New house and car. If you have looked for a new house for two years, or cannot change the TV or car, he says Pluto is working and keeping you as in a pret yoni. All the pret courts are with Yamraj. Set Pluto and it will come. {{t:00:07:23}}
  • He says a doctor stands in the place of the guru and gets the dying body going again. A relative who arranges a match is also in that role, so follow someone. If you have such a person, Pluto calms by itself. {{t:00:07:55}}
  • New body means new things. Pluto also gives the new body. Preparation for a change of body shows as new businesses. So in Pluto's time, do not panic. {{t:00:08:28}}

The claims about pret yoni, souls and the percentages (12, 6, 4) are the speaker's own belief system. They are not astrological rules in the classical texts and cannot be checked. The advice to keep a guru or doctor is his.

3. Pluto is cold, trapped water, and the body darkens {{t:00:08:59}}

  • From the Sun outward: the Sun gives life and Pluto takes life, drawing the soul out. Jupiter sits in the middle, and we stand close to life. First we abuse the guru, then servants, then life and grace, and then death embraces us. We go cold as we move that way. The temperature at Pluto is minus 400, he says. {{t:00:08:59}}
  • As the body and the mind's energy go cold, we deteriorate. If your enthusiasm dies, Pluto has turned on, and it brings aimlessness. {{t:00:09:31}}
  • Trapped water. Scientists have found water and a trapped ocean inside Pluto. So Pluto traps the Moon, the mind. The Moon should keep moving (it changes sign in two and a half days), while Pluto takes about 20 to 21 years in one sign. {{t:00:09:31}}
  • All water is controlled by the Moon, and all ice is Venus, but ice frozen hard is Pluto. A cold hand means the body is dead. Pluto means your business, relationship, love and body go cold. {{t:00:10:02}}
  • Growth needs open water. The Moon is Shiva, who gives life, and if you put Shiva down, you are lost. The mind should keep moving. Many people have a mind that is stuck: money withheld by a brother, papers that will not finish, a child's insult, a husband's betrayal. {{t:00:10:34}}
  • Wherever the Moon is trapped in your chart, Yamraj takes hold and that thing dies: work, job, money, love for children, peace, health. A body part that goes bad slowly turns black. {{t:00:11:06}}
  • Dark planet. Pluto is dark and far from the Sun. In the story the Sun's wife was Chhaya (shadow), who is always dark. Saturn is dark and Pluto is his brother. So the body darkens. {{t:00:11:37}}
  • Cortisol. He calls cortisol the stress hormone. When the mind is in distress, blackness begins to come on the skin, and inside it rots bones and harms organs. {{t:00:11:37}}

The statements about cortisol turning the body black and about a body part going dark are the speaker's own. They are not medical statements. Skin or bone changes should be seen by a doctor.

4. Death in instalments and the "let go with a jerk" rule {{t:00:12:08}}

Two worries

When will I become great, and why does this part hurt. He says both belong to Pluto, who scratches both away and gives "suicide in instalments". {{t:00:12:08}}

The picture

A man scratches until the skin tears and bleeds. Whatever comes suddenly is Pluto's: death, a revival by a doctor's shock, a newborn's first breath. Saturn too has this nature. {{t:00:12:39}}

Remedy

Leave a habit all at once. Stop the cigarette, the drink or the weeping in one jerk, and it ends. If you cut it slowly it does not. {{t:00:13:10}}

  • People say "my job is gone". It was a garment, and you just got a new one. "Have patience." Weeping for years while Pluto grinds on gives it your whole hold, the Moon is pressed, and that thing will die, "guaranteed". {{t:00:13:10}}
  • He says we beg for love, house, health, but should let go in one jerk, and that he has said in earlier classes to drop it or Pluto grabs it. Then give Pluto strength in the right sign, so that it gives a good result. {{t:00:13:40}}
  • Some people have cried over one thing for 20 years. Pluto's cycle is 20 to 21 years, Indra's (Uranus) 14 and 28, Varuna's longer. If you do not let go in 20 years, you will in 40, then 60. {{t:00:14:12}}
  • Order in which things die. Mercury (speech) and Venus (pleasure) stay on till death. Then Mars (effort) is killed first. Then Jupiter, you stop listening to any guru. Then Saturn, you find no servants or people to love you. {{t:00:14:44}}
  • You will not die until your time. Astrology does not change death, it improves the body's state so that you arrive well at the end. {{t:00:15:46}}
  • Share the pain. If you get a kilo of sweets you share it. If you get a kilo of grief, share a little and it is lighter. He says people hold it in and fume instead. {{t:00:16:19}}

The order in which planets "die" and the claim that astrology does not change the time of death are the speaker's reading. Medical or mental health trouble from grief should be shared with a doctor or counsellor, not only a friend.

5. Body signs of Pluto with Uranus, Venus and the legs {{t:00:16:49}}

  • Uranus (Indra). He places Uranus in the thigh area, with growth hormones there. That is why a doctor asks a weak person to do squats. When Pluto joins Indra, the growth hormones fall and the cells deplete. {{t:00:16:49}}
  • Venus. Venus is the seventh dhatu, made last from food. Pluto ends enjoyment: sexual life, the bond with a partner. Tasting good food and seeing a lovely sight are also forms of pleasure that end here. {{t:00:17:20}}
  • Calf pain. Uranus is the god of grace, with the underworld below. The calves have a pump that sends blood back to the heart, and calf pain means you are going near Pluto. {{t:00:17:52}}
  • Death arrives only when written, but you will live in pain if you do not let go. Do it in one jerk. It was in the 12 percent, and was bound to happen. {{t:00:17:52}}
  • He says predictions that work are the proof that astrology is real, and that people who do not give the 72 percent body fail to match the 12. {{t:00:18:24}}

The body part mapping (thigh to Uranus, calf to Pluto) and the sexual health remarks are the speaker's reading and not medical fact. See a doctor for leg pain, since calf pain can be a circulation problem.

6. Cycles, the loop and dharma {{t:00:18:56}}

  • Pluto's place. Pluto sits about 20 years in a sign and its circle is over 250 years. Whatever other planets cross the place where Pluto sits gives results in months, days or years. {{t:00:18:56}}
  • The biggest problem lasts 20 years, a small one 14 (Uranus) or 7 (Saturn's sade sati) years, or two and a half years (Saturn). You must hold patience and let go. {{t:00:19:27}}
  • Planet time scales. Moon about 72 hours, since a doctor asks you to wait 72 hours. Sun one month. Mercury about 22 days. Venus and Mars about one and a half months. Jupiter one year, so take a guru's grace for your largest issue. Mars retrograde and direct is three to four months. Saturn two and a half years. {{t:00:19:59}}
  • If you do not drop the problem, the next cycle restarts. {{t:00:21:00}}
  • Pluto is the god of the loop. The same thing happens every May, in the family for generations. A loop is created by our own thoughts. What a father did to a son, the son does to his own son. A mother's way passes to a daughter. So drop it, or Pluto will not drop you. {{t:00:21:00}}
  • Death does not leave Krishna or Ram. The scriptures give ten days of mourning, and after that you must stop, or you will die in instalments. People who say "take me too" at a funeral get no death, since Pluto follows dharma. {{t:00:21:33}}

Dharma

Yamraj works by dharma and not by sin and merit. The Buddha's path made meat acceptable and weapons forbidden. A person's own rule becomes his dharma. {{t:00:22:34}}

Conflict at home

As many heads, as many dharmas. A wife, husband and brother press each other's dharma on each other. That is the war at home, he says. {{t:00:23:38}}

Example

He holds up Switzerland as a country that takes no sides, with no aid given or taken, and so is the happiest. {{t:00:24:09}}

His remarks on Buddhism, China, Tibet, Bhutan, Islam and the claim that Switzerland has tunnels and blasting set at its borders are the speaker's opinions on religion and politics. They are not facts checked here and are not astrology.
