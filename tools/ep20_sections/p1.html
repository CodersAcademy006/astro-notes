1. Pluto is Yama: death in instalments {{t:00:00:33}}

This class is on Pluto, which the speaker calls Yamraj or Dharmraj. He plays on the name: "Plu-to, the one who loots (lootna)". In the Hindu texts the planet is compared to Yama, the one who takes the soul from the world of the dead toward hell or the world of the gods. {{t:00:00:33}}

  • Death comes in instalments. First a little of the mind dies, then a little of the body. Then the whole mind or the whole body dies. The master of that is Yamraj. {{t:00:01:05}}
  • In the home he sits in the south. Sanatan dharma gives death a deity, which is Yama. {{t:00:01:05}}
  • Yama versus Shiva. Shiva is called the god of death, but the speaker says Shiva is the god of reincarnation. Vishnu means the atom of the universe. Brahma is Brahm, consciousness. Shiva is the divine in everyone, life. If the divine is in a dead body, it is Shiva. A dead body is "shav", and Shiva gives life again. Generate, operate, destroy: the link just before Shiva is Yamraj. {{t:00:01:36}}
  • Yamraj ends everything slowly: work, money, wealth. Sometimes we are looted and broken by illness and come back. Some people have come back from the mouth of death: after total ruin a dead love came alive, body parts regenerated, a new rise began. The speaker says that happens when Yamraj's grace comes. {{t:00:02:08}}
  • That grace comes through the "Mrityunjaya" god, Shiva, who tells Yama "step aside, this is my child, I will give him life again". {{t:00:02:39}}

The speaker's reading of Shiva, Vishnu and Brahma as consciousness, atom and ether is his own gloss, not a standard teaching stated in the class.

2. The Kashi story: Kaal Bhairav stops Yama {{t:00:02:39}}

The setup

A messenger tells Yama that a soul in Kashi cannot be taken: some invisible power blocks it. Yama says he alone owns the soul and rides his buffalo to Kashi. {{t:00:02:39}}

The guardian

He finds an invisible power protecting all of Kashi. It is Kaal Bhairav (kaal is death, Mahakal is Shiva). Kaal Bhairav says the land belongs to Shiva and this soul will get moksha: Yama would only put it back in the cycle of birth and death. {{t:00:03:43}}

The result

A fierce battle. Yama is beaten and prays to Shiva, who says "it is my responsibility". The speaker reads this as Shiva, the Moon, being able to give life again. {{t:00:04:15}}

  • When a person is near death the doctor says "the blood has clotted, the water of the body has frozen". That means the Moon has lost its light. A Moon that goes weaker and weaker means Pluto is gaining power and death will swallow you. {{t:00:04:46}}
  • A dying body goes cold. What do we do for a dying person? Rub the soles of the feet. {{t:00:04:46}}

3. The feet belong to Pluto {{t:00:05:16}}

The speaker's map: the feet are Pluto's place, with Neptune above, then Saturn and Jupiter. He compares the body to a child squatting: knees come up, thighs stay, and the toes land by the hip bone. Death starts in the area of Pluto. {{t:00:05:16}}

Sign

If the doctor says you are ill (blood pressure, sugar), the feet go cold. The speaker says life is leaving from there first. In fear the feet freeze: "my feet will not move". {{t:00:05:48}}

Why we touch feet

We touch the feet of a god, a big man, an angry elder or mother-in-law and ask forgiveness. "Hold the feet, hold Pluto, or death will come." {{t:00:06:21}}

Also

Mosquitoes bite the feet first. Daughters-in-law press the mother-in-law's feet, which is the Neptune side, hell, saying "do not make my life hell, mother-in-law". {{t:00:06:21}}

  • Ageing comes from the feet. Some people's tongue runs without a stop but the feet do not. If your feet stop moving, Pluto is on, as if Yamraj says "come this way". So the doctor says: start walking. Walking sets your Pluto. {{t:00:07:24}}
  • The speaker calls this a claim about consciousness, and says that if it is awake, 6 percent of the alpha-brain element switches on and the akashic channels open. {{t:00:06:21}}

  • If you find a ten-rupee note or gold on the ground, he says Pluto gave a gift, together with Saturn. Land comes to you or does not: quarrels in relationships, no love, then Pluto and Yamraj are angry. {{t:00:08:26}}
  • Home. The south zone of the house, the area he labels S5, is Pluto's. If it is in a bad state, trouble comes. Vastu can set it, remedies will come later. {{t:00:08:26}}
  • Protect your feet first: running away on your feet can save your life. Pressing, massaging and serving the feet is service of Pluto. {{t:00:08:57}}

The speaker's claims that cold feet signal Pluto, that "walking sets Pluto" and that touching feet serves Pluto are his own readings. The alpha-brain remark is not a verified statement. Cold feet or mobility problems need a doctor.

Cremation, burial and the soul {{t:00:16:13}}

Burning the body

Other traditions

He says in Abrahamic religions there is one birth and bones are kept, so the soul circles in a ghost state (pret yoni). He says that has no link to any religion in particular, and some Hindus too stay in that state. {{t:00:16:44}}

Ghosts

Pret souls are ones Yamraj did not even accept. He ties this to a diary you never close: "my brother borrowed 10 lakh ten years ago and I am still crying". {{t:00:16:44}}

  • Yamraj also gives liberation. A leg that hurts is cut off by the doctor. A kidney is removed and you are fine. Forty percent heart damage, medicine, and the man is fit. Yama released the pain. That is a "small death". {{t:00:17:17}}
  • When a person dies, the soul stays near the body. It waits until the body is burnt, and then it is sure "this property is lost". Then it goes by its record of actions to heaven or hell. At Yamlok it presents itself, and Chitragupta sets out its record and says what happens now. {{t:00:17:47}}
  • A small child is often buried and not burnt: the soul came for six months to two years, and the burial stops it taking another body, since that would end early too. Someone wrote a hundred years in the contract, someone two. {{t:00:18:20}}
  • In the ghost state the account of actions is settled, and then the soul takes the next birth. It may take six days or six yugas. The speaker says only Yamraj and Chitragupta know. If your trouble does not end, Pluto has not released you, and the soul wanders. {{t:00:18:52}}
  • Examples: a grandmother crying "my nine-lakh necklace", a father in the ICU crying "what do I do". All ICU patients, the speaker says, are with Pluto: "I will kill in instalments." A sudden death in sleep is a fine death, and Pluto has been kind. {{t:00:19:23}}

All of this on souls, ghosts, cremation versus burial, infant burial and other faiths is the speaker's belief and retelling. It is not endorsed or verified, and he describes other traditions loosely. These notes do not rank any funeral practice. Grief and end-of-life care need real support from family, clergy and doctors.

8. The story of Mona: letting go of the Yama in the mind {{t:00:19:23}}

The case

Mona was lying in a big hospital with all reports bad and doctors baffled by her blood reports. Some said she would die in their hospital and give them a bad name. {{t:00:19:54}}

The visit

She asked to see a baba on a hill before being moved. In the ambulance and wheelchair she bowed. He asked why she came: "if you do not plan to come back, why leave? Stay and die here." {{t:00:20:26}}

His words

"Pain in the body means a worm in the mind. Something inside you is weighing so much that doctors cannot treat the body. You must come back, so leave that garbage here." {{t:00:21:31}}

  • She meditated for five minutes and left everything: revenge, quarrels, anger, objections, deals, fights. She forgave all and said "free me". {{t:00:23:06}}
  • Back in hospital, retests began. By the seventh day the reports slowly came right, and in ten days she was well. Doctors from both hospitals asked what she did. She had done nothing but bow and leave the inner garbage at the baba's feet. {{t:00:22:02}}
  • The speaker's lesson: if you want freedom from dead wealth, a dead body and troubled relations, take the Yamraj out of your mind. Remove the strife and bitterness and the body heals, since the body follows the mind. {{t:00:23:06}}
  • Doctors give the best treatment and watch for 72 hours, which is the time of "Chhenu" (the destroyer god). Then they say "leave Pluto to the Sun". {{t:00:23:37}}

The Mona story is a retold anecdote and cannot be checked. It must not be read as a claim that forgiveness cures illness or that medical treatment can be skipped. The doctors in the story kept treating her. Anyone who is seriously ill needs medical care.
