10. Sun, Venus and Neptune: perfume, salt and the 14-year storm {{t:00:52:19}}

Sun and Venus

Perfume left in open air vanishes and alcohol evaporates. Heat finishes both, so the Sun destroys Venus's comforts. {{t:00:52:19}}

Sun and Neptune (Varuna)

When the Sun goes away from the sea god, envy grows, calves are hurt, and there is poor self-confidence and "I am not beautiful". {{t:00:52:51}}

Why

The Sun's rays are Mars at the navel. When the navel is weak, the speaker says, caesarean cuts follow: Mars cuts, and the Sun does not hold him back. {{t:00:53:22}}

  • Dignity (maryada). A child who has finished a paper and waits for the 20th for results is calm. One who starts crying on the 10th "travels in time" and weakens the Sun. Keep to decorum and "no one can uproot you". {{t:00:53:54}} {{t:00:54:26}}
  • Hanuman and the sea. Mars crossed Neptune's sea in one leap. The Sun is the male principle: he does not act. Doing is the work of the female principle: Vishnu does nothing, Lakshmi does. {{t:00:54:26}} {{t:00:54:58}}
  • Salt. The sea's salt is held by the Sun. If the sea throws up water, tides and tsunamis come: the Sun has left. In the body the sea is the stomach: lose salt (electrolytes) and you get vomiting and bad digestion. {{t:00:55:28}} {{t:00:55:59}} {{t:00:56:32}}
  • 14-year cycle. Neptune takes about 14 years per sign. The Pandavas had 12 years of exile and a 13th hidden, and Rama had 14. The speaker links these. {{t:00:55:28}}
  • Japan. "The land of the rising sun" gets the most tsunamis, he says, because the Sun comes first and then leaves. {{t:00:56:32}}

Electrolyte loss and vomiting are medical issues and should be seen by a doctor. The link to the Sun's sign is the speaker's reading.

Chest and heart trouble {{t:00:57:35}}

Lung and chest problems, chest-bone pain and heart trouble are, in his reading, Neptune damaged by a broken heart. He adds that someone who has not learned to forgive and clean the slate gets no benefit from any remedy. {{t:00:57:35}}
