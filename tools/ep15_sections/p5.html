18. Water to the Sun, the courtyard and ultraviolet {{t:01:59:43}}

Why dawn

The dawn light before sunrise carries ultraviolet, the colour just past violet and next to the crown chakra. He says an eagle that spots a tiny insect from the sky sees by it. {{t:01:59:43}} {{t:02:00:45}}

How

Offer water in the Brahma muhurta, the red glow before the Sun appears. At 9, 10 or 11 am it is just a formality. {{t:02:01:17}} {{t:02:02:53}}

What it means

The water goes to Shiva within the Sun, who has the power to change fate. The brain is mostly water, so it cools. {{t:02:01:50}} {{t:02:02:21}}

  • Open centre of the home. Keep the courtyard open. He says houses with walls in the central space (Brahmasthan) make heart patients, eye trouble and friction in the couple, and the wife usually dies first. {{t:02:00:15}}
  • Past dawn. Offering water late brings kidney and sugar trouble, he says, unless done at dawn. Beyond red is infrared, which he calls "death energy". {{t:02:02:21}} {{t:02:03:23}}
  • Only if the Sun is spoiled. A person with a good Sun should not cool it for free. If a good Sun is giving away wheat and flour, "you are done for". He says moon-strong people who build rest-houses end up on the road. {{t:02:03:56}}

The speaker's links from water offerings to kidney and sugar disease, and from home layout to heart attacks, are his claims, not medical findings.
