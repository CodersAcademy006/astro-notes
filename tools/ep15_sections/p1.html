1. Why the Sun first, and the rule of no free gifts {{t:00:03:07}}

The speaker opens with housekeeping, then names the Sun as the planet that can bring you quickly to a good guru, a court win, government favour and stalled work moving. The Sun is "the god of gods" in his words, because without it there is no life on Earth.

  • Housekeeping. He clarifies an earlier remark about giving money at a gurdwara: "throw" meant to leave the money and forget it, like a used tissue, so no ego of "I gave so much" grows. He says that if the meaning is not clear he apologises. He also says the classes are for knowledge, not a chart reading, and that consultation slots fill up within a day. {{t:00:00:33}} {{t:00:02:05}}
  • The Sun gives without being asked. To ruin your Sun, keep taking free gifts. The speaker says nothing is free and your Sun falls with every free gift. {{t:00:03:39}} {{t:00:04:10}}
  • The Brahmin's case. A Brahmin accepts gifts in exchange for blessing, with the right to do so. Fire takes whatever is put in and does not die: the thing is changed. He says a Brahmin has the strength to "drink" the fault in your gift, and someone has to carry the fault. {{t:00:04:41}}
  • Many Brahmins live poor, he says, because they do not know how to discharge it. Jupiter's class will explain. {{t:00:05:13}}

The speaker's claims about who may accept gifts, and what happens to the giver and receiver, are his belief. They are not an astrological rule that others share.

2. Sun as soul and aura: the third eye, vitamin D and the smile {{t:00:06:16}}

The Sun is "the supreme father" (Parampita). The speaker places it at the third eye, with the Moon on its left, and says aura is nothing but the Sun.

Third eye

The kundalini runs from the base chakra to the third eye, above the five elements. Sun and Moon sit here, just below the crown (sahasrara). Consciousness enters here and is filtered. {{t:00:06:48}}

Sun and Moon

The Sun is the energy body. The Moon is the causal body, the answer to "why was I born". Without a Sun there is no birth. {{t:00:07:21}}

One thought

A strong Sun gives one single thought: you do it whether right or wrong. A split Sun gives two minds, and you wander down into other planets' areas. {{t:00:10:01}}

Signs of a weak Sun {{t:00:07:54}}

  • Aura. A low Sun means a dull, broken, flat person, low morale, low life energy, and illness closing in. {{t:00:07:54}}
  • Bones, glow, joints. Weak Sun shows as weak bones, loss of glow on the face, joint pain (the Sun is the "grease" of the body), and loss of will to do anything. {{t:00:12:37}} {{t:00:13:08}}
  • Vitamin D. The speaker calls a low vitamin D reading "just a reading that says the Sun is down". Plants eat sunlight, and we eat plants, so we eat sunlight. A person with a strong Sun even meets a good surgeon who saves them. {{t:00:08:57}} {{t:00:09:30}}
  • Old Jain rule. Eat while the Sun is up, because microbes come after sunset. Put old quilts and clothes in the sun. {{t:00:07:54}} {{t:00:08:25}}

Vitamin D and bone health are real medical topics, but treating them is for a doctor. The link from a lab value to "the Sun is down" is the speaker's reading. Check levels with a test.

Fix the Sun at home, per the speaker {{t:00:13:08}}

  • Smile. When you smile you rise out of the throat and the lower chakras for that moment. A hanging face means a Sun that is down, "wow, joy" means the Sun is set. {{t:00:13:40}}
  • Unfazed Sun. The Sun never moves, never goes and never comes, but it pulls the whole solar system. It only throws light. {{t:00:14:12}}

3. When nobody listens: below Jupiter, Ketu and Saturn {{t:00:10:33}}

Problem

"Not even a dog asks about me." Children, siblings, parents ignore you. You feel you do not exist. {{t:00:10:33}} {{t:00:11:04}}

Why (speaker)

You have slipped below Jupiter. Jupiter is the largest planet, so the sunlight reaches well up to it. Under it lie Ketu (sign 12) and Saturn (10 and 11): the dark area. Lust, anger, greed, attachment, pride and envy rule there. {{t:00:10:33}}

Result

The space element goes first: throat and thyroid, and the thought "no one hears me". The Sun's strength is what clears this. {{t:00:11:35}}

  • Makar Sankranti. When the Sun enters Saturn's sign Capricorn, that lower dark region becomes worthy of worship: the days of light return. Bhishma waited for the northward run (uttarayan) to die. The Sun then goes through Aquarius and Pisces, and "God also asks about you". {{t:00:11:35}} {{t:00:12:06}}

4. Dharma comes from the Sun, and the witness mind {{t:00:14:12}}

The speaker derives dharma (your rule, your constant way of living) from the Sun. His point: dharma is not a religion; it is the thing you stick to without bending.

  • Everyone has their own. A thief's rule is theft. Duryodhana's was "by any means". Whatever you hold constant is your dharma. Sanatan, he says, is the one dharma that accepts all of them. {{t:00:14:12}} {{t:00:14:42}}
  • Do not bend your character. The Sun does not say "it is hot there, shall I cool down". A person who stays flat and steady keeps bones, teeth, gut and eyesight. Dramatics weaken the body. The body shows the state of your Sun. {{t:00:14:42}} {{t:00:15:13}}
  • Witness view. If not a leaf moves without God's wish, then who are you to sin, to do good or to give? Seeing this makes you a witness, and the Sun gives witnessing. A witness turns into a giver. {{t:00:15:45}}
  • Sign of a strong Sun. You do not beg for a promotion. Things reach you before you ask. {{t:00:16:16}}
