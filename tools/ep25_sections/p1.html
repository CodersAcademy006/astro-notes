1. Neptune (Varuna), the sea of "never enough" {{t:00:00:33}}

This class finishes Neptune, the sea god Varuna. Last week covered his attributes. This week covers what happens when other planets enter Neptune's sign, what Neptune does in other planets' signs, his conjunctions, and the west direction of the home, the place of wish fulfilment. {{t:00:00:33}}

Recap {{t:00:01:05}}

  • Varuna is the ocean, unlimited and everywhere. It has two main parts: water (the Moon) and salt (the Sun, which binds the water). The Sun also binds the Earth and the Moon in orbit, and drags our whole system through the galaxy. {{t:00:01:05}}
  • When Sun and Moon mix, you get a god of grace. Neptune is the sea, with no end, like the universe. {{t:00:02:09}}
  • The endless wish. Everyone wants wealth, joy, pleasure and life with no end. Neptune keeps the head saying "more", never "enough". Of a lakh of breaths, a person curses the one that brought a sneeze, and one mistake of a lifelong friend starts a fight. {{t:00:02:40}}
  • With realisation, Neptune also gives "a hundred crimes forgiven", a huge heart. He is both the madness of endlessness and the realisation of limits. A spoiled Neptune in a house keeps saying "more, and all of it is fake": in the money house, "money is short"; in the home house, "only four or five homes, I need more"; in love, a Casanova; in drink, endless drink; in knowledge, endless. {{t:00:03:11}}
  • Enlightenment is "enough". How big a house do you need? Not a 4 km house. {{t:00:03:41}}
  • His sign is number 7 (Libra, the scale) and he gives the best result in sign 12. The sea keeps nothing: whatever is thrown in comes out or sinks. He is a giver. {{t:00:04:44}}

2. Sun in Neptune's sign (sign 7) {{t:00:05:15}}

Problem

Heart attack, kidney stones, drying of body water, bone and joint weakness. By house: 1st head injury, 5th kidney stone then children and study, 7th bladder stone, other houses leg, shoulder or eye trouble. {{t:00:05:15}}

Cause as he reads it

The Sun comes to the sea and sucks the water, like grace drying up. Body water falls from 90 percent (infant) to 72 (young) to 60 (old), bringing wrinkles and weak joints. He says every heart and kidney patient has an angry Neptune sign attacked by the Sun. {{t:00:05:45}}

Remedy

Boil a fistful of wheat, cool it, and feed it to a cow every Sunday for life. Never add white salt on top (black salt is fine). Do yoga. {{t:00:06:48}}

  • Cow is Venus. Venus is about 471 degrees and the sea is cold. {{t:00:07:18}}
  • Heart picture. The heart has right and left bundle branch arteries, working in pairs like ida and pingala. If Neptune is spoiled, the right or left bundle branch trouble appears and blood supply slows. A right bundle branch person can live normally, though doctors give statins (blood thinners). If you please the sea god with a big heart, he says repair systems in the body turn on. {{t:00:07:18}}
  • The Sun in sign 6, 8, 9, 12, 1 or 2 may still shift to sign 7 in the chalit or nirayana chart, so check the combined chart. {{t:00:08:21}}
  • Neptune gives, so he does not give death here. {{t:00:08:51}}
  • Salt in the home is kept to kill negative energy. The Sun is your father, so tension with the father or his absence may follow. You may have to become the father yourself, and need inner strength. The Sun in sign 7 is "highly negative" and gives only faults. {{t:00:09:52}}

The heart, kidney and bone statements and the claim that every patient has this placement are the speaker's own. They are not medical facts. The mention of statins and blood thinners is his aside. See a doctor, and do not stop any medicine because of a chart.
