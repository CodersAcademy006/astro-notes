5. His universal law: a retrograde planet needs its stone {{t:00:37:18}}

Having set up what retrograde means, the speaker states what he calls a single rule that covers every case: whichever planet sits retrograde in your birth chart, you fit that planet's gemstone, no exceptions. {{t:00:19:36}} {{t:00:37:18}}

Why not skip it

He compares a long-standing problem to a cough that has lasted fifteen years: short remedies (a prayer, a small donation) are like a cheap cough syrup that never reaches the real damage underneath. {{t:00:39:27}} {{t:00:40:32}}

His reasoning

A retrograde planet is "too powerful" to respond at the surface level. Only when the sahasrara's grace is reached, in his model, does the planet give results in ordinary consciousness, and a well-fitted stone is how that channel is opened. {{t:00:21:07}} {{t:00:41:03}}

Remedy

Fit the correct stone once, properly. He says a well-fitted stone can carry a person for a hundred years, unlike repeated small remedies that have to be redone again and again. {{t:00:40:32}} {{t:00:56:39}}

A caution for the 3rd, 6th, 8th and 12th houses {{t:00:20:06}}

If the retrograde planet owns one of these four houses (loosely linked to endings and loss in his scheme), wearing its stone can carry a real side effect, which he compares to a steroid: it may be necessary to survive a crisis, but it is not free of cost to the body. He says this is why case-by-case study matters rather than a blanket rule. {{t:00:20:06}} {{t:00:21:07}}

  • He is blunt that gemstones are expensive and that cost is the main reason people avoid them, but adds that expensive "surgery" style remedies work faster than a lifetime of small, repeated fixes. {{t:00:40:32}} {{t:00:41:03}}
  • On the mindset behind giving: if you resent paying for a remedy, calling people "thieves" while paying a doctor but not a priest, he says that resentment itself blocks the benefit. Giving without that resentment, he argues, is what actually "cuts the planet's account" in your favour. {{t:00:41:03}} {{t:00:42:59}}
  • He tells a story of three robbers in Delhi who, after threatening a man with a knife, found almost nothing in his wallet, felt pity, and left money behind instead of taking it, as his example that unexpected kindness can come from anywhere once grace is in play. This is an unverified anecdote, not a general claim about crime. {{t:00:41:34}} {{t:00:42:05}}

The specific rupee figures in the priest-versus-doctor story, and the claim that paying a priest "cuts" a planetary debt, are the speaker's own belief about giving and should not be read as financial or religious advice.

When the "right" stone does not suit {{t:01:44:35}}

  • Pitambari (a yellow-and-blue stone) can stand in when neither a yellow topaz for Jupiter nor a blue sapphire for Saturn suits the wearer. {{t:01:44:35}} {{t:01:45:06}}
  • A green sapphire can stand in for an emerald (Mercury) that does not suit, and a red sapphire can stand in for a ruby (Sun) that does not suit. {{t:01:45:06}}
  • Matching a stone to a person is "hit and trial" that a jeweller or astrologer has to work out; it is not a fixed lookup table. {{t:01:45:38}}
  • The stone is a filter, not a booster. He is explicit: a gemstone never raises or lowers a planet's underlying power. It only filters how much of that planet's rays reach you, the way a strainer holds back tea leaves. {{t:01:48:16}} {{t:01:48:47}}
  • Clarity matters. A stone with visible lines or cracks scatters the incoming rays instead of focusing them cleanly, so the effect is only partial, for life. A cheap, flawed stone is why people say "the remedy did not work" when the real issue is the stone's own quality. {{t:01:46:10}} {{t:01:46:42}}
  • Buy the best you can. His advice is to treat the stone as a one-time gift to yourself, since a well-made one can last a hundred years, far outlasting the few years many people expect a remedy to run. {{t:01:47:14}} {{t:01:56:39}}
  • Children can wear a light version early, mainly for concentration (Mercury, Jupiter), since a child has no wealth, family or power concerns yet for the stone to carry. If a baby is diagnosed with a serious condition early, he says, start then rather than waiting, even using a small ring or a cord for a very young child. {{t:01:48:47}} {{t:01:49:49}}
  • A stone is not swapped for a new one once properly set, in his telling, the way a car or a marriage is not casually replaced either. {{t:01:56:39}} {{t:01:57:10}}
