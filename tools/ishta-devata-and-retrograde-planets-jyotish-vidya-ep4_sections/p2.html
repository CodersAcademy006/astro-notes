6. Before you worship anything: when the "fix" backfires {{t:00:24:38}}

A deity tied to a weak or debilitated (nich) planet is not automatically safe to invoke, and praying at a temple is not automatically good. The speaker spends real time warning against guessing. {{t:00:24:38}}

Debilitated Mars

Mars debilitated in sign 4 still "burns like fire," even to the point of making someone rich, but it eats the kidneys. Worshipping a Mars-linked deity (Hanuman) without checking this can make things worse, not better. {{t:00:25:40}}

Debilitated Mercury

If a 12th-house Mercury is debilitated and money has become that person's de facto Ishta, temple visits can activate the debilitation rather than calm it; the speaker's blunt advice to such a chart is "never go to temple." {{t:00:31:27}}

Why

Puja works by activating the matching planet's energy. Activating a planet that is already weak or poorly placed intensifies the weakness instead of fixing it. {{t:00:31:58}}

  • Hanuman puja is powerful, and risky for some. An exalted Mars making Hanuman a natural Ishta is a gift, but Hanuman is a celibate (brahmachari) deity: a married woman who worships him intensely can find her physical married life fades even while the marriage itself stays prosperous and respected. {{t:00:26:42}}
  • Where Hanuman himself is awkward to invoke directly, his own object of devotion, Rama, works as a substitute: praying to Rama still reaches Hanuman's grace indirectly, because Hanuman exists to serve Rama. {{t:00:27:46}}
  • An alternative: formless (nirakar) worship. Instead of picking a deity by chart alone, the speaker offers Kabir's answer when asked when he prays: he never stops, because the inner thread of awareness never breaks no matter what the hands are doing. {{t:00:29:20}}
  • His parable: a girl walks a stony path balancing a water pot on her head and a baby on her hip, singing as she goes. The pot, the baby and the song are all being watched by the same unbroken awareness. That unbroken awareness is the real Ishta, whether it is pointed at Rama, Krishna or anything else. {{t:00:30:21}}
  • Your Ishta might just be whatever occupies your mind most. If the mind is full of money-worry, money is functioning as your Ishta whether you admit it or not. {{t:00:30:54}}
  • Worked example: a 6th-house exalted Mercury sitting in Rahu's sign can make someone wealthy through sheer focus on money, with no temple-going at all, "because that is where his consciousness lives." {{t:00:31:27}}
  • Temple-going is not inherently good or bad. It depends on the chart. For someone whose consciousness has not yet "woken up," a temple at least gives a place to cry and be comforted, which the speaker calls valuable in itself. {{t:00:32:28}}
  • Vastu example. A couple with constant household tension changed nothing except their bedsheets and pillow covers, which were red, a colour the speaker calls highly negative in the west direction; they reported feeling calmer within an hour, because, in his telling, the subconscious mind is constantly reading the environment. {{t:00:32:58}}

The anecdotes about Hanuman puja affecting a married woman's physical relationship, and about red bedsheets in the west, are the speaker's own framework and personal case stories. They are not medical or relationship advice.

7. Mars (Mangal): marriage, fire and the manglik question {{t:00:36:52}}

Mars gets the longest single treatment in this class: as the Sun's commander, the planet of agni (fire), blood and weapons, and the planet most tangled up with Hindu marriage ritual. {{t:00:36:52}}

  • Mars rules red, blood, accidents, surgery, and the south-east direction of a home. {{t:00:37:47}}{{t:01:04:57}}
  • Mars answers to nobody except Jupiter, which the speaker says anchors Mars at the navel from the sacral chakra just above it; disturbed digestion (vomiting, loose motion) is read as the navel's fire being pulled around. {{t:01:00:45}}
  • Mars's transit speed is irregular, unlike other planets; a stretch where it sat in sign 4 (a debilitated Moon's sign) is linked by the speaker to a period of civil unrest, "because it throws fire into the world when it cannot be contained." {{t:01:02:21}}

Manglik placements and the agni-phera remedy {{t:01:11:16}}

The placement

Mars sitting in house 1, 4, 7, 8 or 12 of a chart is called "manglik." {{t:01:11:16}}

What it threatens

The speaker links it to marriage breakdown, because the Hindu wedding fire ritual (the pheras round the fire) is specifically what "ignites" Mars; skip the fire and the dosha has nothing to activate. {{t:01:06:33}}

The remedies offered

A court marriage, a Sikh Anand Karaj, or any contract-style registration (which he calls "Mercury's marriage," since it runs on paper and signatures) carries no fire, so it carries no Mars dosha either. Red bindi and sindoor for the bride are the companion remedy where fire pheras are performed. {{t:01:06:33}}{{t:01:09:42}}

  • Shani cancels Mars's dosha. If Saturn also sits in house 1, 4, 7, 8 or 12 of the same chart, the Mars dosha is considered cancelled: the person can marry a manglik or a non-manglik partner with no extra risk, "like a universal blood donor." {{t:01:11:46}}
  • Where the fire ritual is performed and Mars is active, the speaker says it also protects the couple's sex life and shuts down the extramarital troubles he links to an afflicted south-east direction. {{t:01:08:37}}
  • Remedy if divorce looks destined. Court-marry first, so there is no fire for the real marriage to burn; if a couple already married with fire and the chart still points to separation, he describes practices like a symbolic prior marriage to a peepal tree or a clay pot to "pre-empt" Mars. {{t:01:07:33}}
  • A brother's anger around the time of your wedding is treated as a bad omen for the marriage itself, because it unsettles Mars (karaka for brothers); patching that relationship, or symbolically "adopting" another brother figure, is offered as a fix. {{t:01:08:05}}
  • Universal Mars remedies: feed jaggery to a cow; on a Tuesday exam, give something sweet and red (laddoo, barfi, rasgulla) instead of the usual yellow Jupiter sweet; regular blood donation or blood tests "pre-pays" the planet's demand for blood, which he connects to avoiding accidents. {{t:01:05:30}}{{t:01:12:16}}

The claims connecting a punctured south-east door to sons dying, and the Atlantis and Mahabharata-brahmastra story about Mars being scorched barren, are the speaker's own mythic framework and should be read as that, not as fact. {{t:00:59:11}}{{t:01:03:23}}

8. Sun (Surya): kidney stones and the missing blessing {{t:01:13:49}}

A side-discussion on gallbladder and kidney stones, which the speaker ties to an afflicted Sun and a missing elder's blessing rather than to diet alone. {{t:01:13:49}}

The problem

Gallbladder or kidney stones, along with ongoing cash-flow trouble. {{t:01:13:49}}

The speaker's cause

An afflicted Sun, read as a missing blessing: from a father, a husband, a guru, or a family elder. The matching home zone (south, south-south-east, south-east) carrying blue, black, grey or pure milky white is named as a contributing vastu fault. {{t:01:14:22}}{{t:01:14:55}}

What is offered

Correct the colour in that zone and repair the relationship that is short on blessing. No medicine or dosage is suggested in the class; this is a blessing-and-vastu remedy only. {{t:01:14:55}}

  • Sun in house 2 or house 7, or in a marriage-linked house generally, is specifically called out for kidney stones and, separately, for raising divorce risk: "it burns and finishes things." {{t:01:15:26}}
  • A placement's strength depends on its exact degree; a Sun sitting at only two or three degrees into a house, for instance, "has not caught power yet" and will not show the full effect, which is why the speaker recommends checking the chalit and KP charts for precision rather than reading the headline sign alone. {{t:01:15:58}}
  • Your Ishta is not always one of the nine grahas. It can be an ancestor (pitra) or a family deity (kuldevi or kuldevta) instead. {{t:01:16:30}}

The specific claim that a 2nd or 7th house Sun guarantees kidney stones, and that this is caused by a missing blessing, is the speaker's own diagnostic framework. It is not medical advice and these notes do not endorse it as a cause of any illness.
