29. Venus, the south-east and shared food {{t:01:39:45}}

Venus is the planet of romance, food, adornment and beauty, and he calls it the woman's indicator in a man's chart. {{t:01:39:45}} In the house it holds the south-east zone. {{t:01:40:15}}

  • South-east kitchen. He says a woman who wants a loving husband should have the kitchen in the south-east, which he calls ideal. Failing that, the south or south-south-east. If that is not possible, keep food items, make-up, expensive clothes and beauty items in that corner, which he claims strengthens the woman's hold on the home. {{t:01:40:15}} {{t:01:40:45}}
  • With a kitchen there he says the woman "slims down by herself", food is made at home and children enjoy eating. {{t:01:41:15}}
  • If the house does not allow it, the couple eat each other's leftover (jootha) food, if the 7th house permits it. He says that keeps love and romance. {{t:01:41:15}} {{t:01:41:46}}

He describes the old custom of a wife eating the husband's leftover as a form of binding (vashikaran), and the custom of keeping a woman out of the kitchen during her period as a way to protect her health. These are his readings of old practice. This note does not endorse any practice that treats a woman as impure or as a servant, and he himself calls it oppression if it is forced. {{t:01:41:46}} {{t:01:42:17}}

  • He says he has a story about a woman that he will not tell, since it would make listeners cry. {{t:01:42:48}}
