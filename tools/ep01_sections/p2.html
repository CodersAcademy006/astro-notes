3. Why name tricks and colour tricks fail {{t:00:15:40}}

Problem

Popular fixes sold today: add a letter to your name, change your lucky number, wear a colour, face south, feed a cow jaggery. People try everything and nothing holds. {{t:00:15:40}} {{t:00:16:12}}

Cause (speaker's view)

These work only on a "superficial layer". The real driver is the planetary calculation: the planet period (dasha) says how long your good time lasts and when the bad time follows, whatever the name or colour. {{t:00:17:15}} {{t:00:17:46}}

What he points to instead

Astrological calculation tells you dates: "after the 18th this happens, after the 25th that, your house will not sell before October". He calls this the top layer over Vastu, Tarot and Reiki. {{t:00:19:20}}

  • His example. A well-known cricketer's side won a world T20 in 2024 and then lost a series in the same year with the same name and the same colours. If a name drove luck, he asks, what changed? Each person's time cycle changed. {{t:00:16:12}} {{t:00:16:42}}
  • Remedies that work "give a while of relief and then it falls again", e.g. he mocks the advice to give liquor to others to help yourself. {{t:00:17:15}}
  • Why these sell. We fight fate, and life beats us. The pain comes because God wants one thing and we want another. {{t:00:17:46}}
  • Pay the debt early. Many pay a cheap doctor fifty rupees again and again and a big doctor once. In the same way, prarabdha is cleared faster by one big, deliberate payment (daan or remedy) than by small repeated ones. {{t:00:18:19}} {{t:00:18:49}}

The remarks on money and on the cricketer are the speaker's examples. He is not a statistician. Treat them as illustrations, not proof.

Two problems with astrology {{t:00:19:20}}

  • He says astrology runs into two problems and names this one: the subject is true but astrologers are false, so people lose faith. {{t:00:19:20}} {{t:00:19:52}}
  • The main trouble in life, he says, is that we steer our consciousness in the negative direction, attract bad company, get cheated and cry. If the consciousness is good, you will not be pulled around by the quota of Mona and Minal in the earlier story, and you can lengthen the good part of the quota. {{t:00:20:22}}
  • The chart is a snapshot of the sky at your birth. The same rays that touch your body then keep working on you. He compares it to a bulb that lights your room from afar, or an X-ray. {{t:00:20:52}} {{t:00:21:23}}

4. Human consciousness: ask big, command clean {{t:00:24:30}}

The speaker keeps returning to one rule: what you ask for is what the planets deliver. If your first command to the subconscious is small, the chart gives something small, even if it had a bigger yoga. {{t:00:28:39}} {{t:00:30:48}}

The CAT student {{t:00:24:30}}

A student was fixed on one exam and one kind of college. A chance came elsewhere and he said "I will decide in two days". It was gone. The speaker's view: the boy and his parents wanted him to be an employee. The owner of the company that would hire him probably never cleared the exam, but that owner asked big. {{t:00:25:00}} {{t:00:25:32}} {{t:00:27:05}} {{t:00:27:35}}

  • Some remedies do not remove an event but convert it. Instead of a wasted year, a bad marriage can be converted (the speaker mentions marrying a tree as one such device). {{t:00:26:02}} {{t:00:26:34}}
  • "Think big." Someone who aims at being president of a country, however loudly they speak, shows what it means to ask large. {{t:00:27:35}}

The remarks about a named head of state and about the student's parents are the speaker's opinion and are not astrological analysis.

The vehicle yoga {{t:00:28:39}}

  • A chart can carry a "vehicle yoga" (vahan yog). A horse or elephant in the word means a ride. It may be a bike or a car. {{t:00:28:39}}
  • If you ask "whatever comes is fine", the planets give you a cycle. If you say "I want that 18 lakh bike", they work toward it. {{t:00:28:39}} {{t:00:29:13}}
  • Some rich people live in a flat for 20 years with money in hand. Nobody blocks them. It is their own thinking that does. {{t:00:29:13}} {{t:00:29:43}}
  • If an ordinary person asks only for a job or "just one son", that is what they get. The son then gives them nothing later and they cry that he does not care. {{t:00:30:16}} {{t:00:32:20}}

Seed and tree {{t:00:32:52}}

  • You planted a mango seed. When the mango starts to come you shout "I wanted bananas" and run to a mantra, a temple or a tantrik. It will still be a mango. The first command is fixed. You can graft another type of mango, but not a banana. {{t:00:32:52}} {{t:00:33:24}}
  • Childhood commands. A boy who says "I want a wife like my mother" gets what he said, then finds her washing clothes by hand and refusing the air conditioner, and it suffocates him. That is why the old system sent children to a celibate student life (brahmacharya), to learn before issuing commands. {{t:00:33:24}} {{t:00:33:54}}
  • The same with "party, party, party" in youth: later you want a club-going wife, and if you marry one by mistake it ends in divorce, because the early command went in uneven. {{t:00:33:54}}

Why the rich "sinner" prospers {{t:00:29:43}}

The speaker says a bad man with good planets and 5 lakh in the bank will enjoy and gamble. When the money and the good period run out, he falls. He says "there is no punishment in this world" in the sense that the result simply plays out by the clock. If you learn the balancing, you can cut your own quota. {{t:00:29:43}} {{t:00:30:16}}

The umbrella story {{t:00:38:04}}

A drought village hires a priest from another village to pray for rain. A farmer takes his family along and says "God gives everything". After the prayer his daughter hands him an umbrella. He asks why. She says "you just asked for rain". He breaks down: the prayer had no faith behind it. The speaker's moral: if you do not believe, praying is wasted. Madmen get results because they ask for the whole aeroplane and believe it. {{t:00:38:04}} {{t:00:38:36}} {{t:00:39:09}}

  • If you fight a lion, you may be eaten, but at least you lost to a lion. Fighting a donkey and breaking your leg is worse. So aim at the lion. {{t:00:39:09}}
  • If you cannot ignore small things, two planets in the chart are making you dwell on them. Treat those planets by daan, stones and remedies. The same for disbelief and for a child who refuses to study: fix the planet and the mind follows. {{t:00:39:42}} {{t:00:40:13}}

The speaker also warns that you should not expect an astrologer to make you superhuman. A doctor removes your problem, then you are an ordinary healthy person. {{t:00:41:47}}

  • The banyan tree. A very tall tree has roots as deep underground as the branches are high. A saint is the one who stopped looking at the roots. Big happiness comes with matching sorrow. {{t:00:35:58}} {{t:00:36:29}}
