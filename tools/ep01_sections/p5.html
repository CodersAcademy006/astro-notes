13. Q&A: how to give a command, and how to keep the mind busy {{t:01:52:44}}

Question

"How should I give a command for manifestation? Which words should I use or avoid?" {{t:01:52:44}}

Why a "no" fails

Your words go to space exactly as spoken. The cosmos is above the navel. A "no" in the command takes you below the navel to the base (muladhara), and that part says "so be it". {{t:01:53:15}} {{t:01:53:47}}

Remedy

Say the wish with no negatives. Say it so you feel it and believe it, like going to ask for rain with an umbrella in hand. {{t:01:53:47}} {{t:01:54:18}}

  • "I want to become an IAS officer" is a good command if you also feel it. If it were easy, everyone would be a champion sprinter. It takes the knack of feeling it. {{t:01:53:47}} {{t:01:54:18}}
  • A badminton player who hits the shuttle so hard it lands outside still loses. You must know how to feel it right. {{t:01:54:18}} {{t:01:54:49}}

Positive thinking and negative thinking {{t:01:54:49}}

  • The question: should we only think positive, or is negative thinking sometimes needed? He answers with examples: the positive thinker made the pencil, the negative thinker made the razor. One made the boat, the other the life jacket. {{t:01:55:19}}
  • Then his twist: you are not the one thinking. Thinking is done by "Munna" (the base mind, muladhara). If you are busy choosing what to think, he can wash you out. The thinking department belongs only to Munna. {{t:01:55:19}} {{t:01:55:50}}
  • The scale of these planets' cycles: Jupiter takes about 12 years round the Sun, Saturn about 30, Uranus 84, Neptune 164, and Pluto still more. He says (his figures) Saturn has over 100 moons and Jupiter 84 found so far. {{t:01:55:50}} {{t:01:56:22}}

The genie story: keep the mind occupied {{t:01:56:54}}

A wanderer sells a genie (bhoot) to a merchant for 500 rupees. It does every task at once: shift goods, build a godown, a factory, plant a garden. But if it has no work, it will eat its master. The merchant, now a billionaire in two days, cannot sleep and flees to a saint. The saint says: give it a bamboo pole to climb up and down all day, and call it back when you need it. The merchant asks what this means. {{t:01:56:54}} {{t:01:57:25}} {{t:01:57:57}} {{t:01:58:28}} {{t:01:58:59}} {{t:01:59:32}}

  • Meaning, as the speaker tells it. The genie is the mind. The pole is the breath. Tell the mind to go along with every breath and call it when you need it. The mind obeys. {{t:02:00:03}}
  • Give your mind work and the mind will do all other work as asked. {{t:02:00:36}}
  • The speaker says the exact word for the command is not clear from the captions, so only the idea is noted here. {{t:02:00:03}}

Smaller questions {{t:02:01:07}}

  • Can I use an iron bed? Yes, place it on the west side of the room. {{t:02:01:07}}
  • Should I wish before doing something, or act with no wish? Make the wish first. Without it, the cosmos will not run the exam for you. {{t:02:01:07}} {{t:02:01:38}}
  • In the Vastu deity map, the centre is Brahma. Four deities come first (Bhu, Bhudhara, Aryama, Vivasvan, with Mitra) and decide the reason for acting, the means, the wealth and the result. All four should be present. {{t:02:01:38}} {{t:02:02:09}} {{t:02:02:39}}

The names and roles of these deities are taken as they are heard in the captions and are not checked against a Vastu text.

14. Omkar or ahankar: who is making the command {{t:02:03:10}}

  • People ask if you should do meditation for an hour. The speaker says the real aim is to be a witness all 24 hours. But if you will not sit for even an hour, how will you practise? Do both. {{t:02:03:10}} {{t:02:03:40}}
  • "Ek Onkar" is read as: kar means the hand that does. The hand sits at the heart, where Venus and Varuna are. "Kar seva" is not about cars but about the doing that comes from Omkar. {{t:02:04:10}} {{t:02:04:42}}
  • Two doers: Omkar (the witness) and ahankar ("I did, my child, my house"). If there is no ego, only Om remains, and it runs the universe. Yet we do not trust it. {{t:02:05:13}} {{t:02:05:45}}
  • The "better" treadmill. At 20: a car, 3 lakh in the bank. Then "something better": a bigger house, a wife, children, and again "better". He says the whole life becomes this. The ask that comes from ego never ends. {{t:02:06:17}} {{t:02:06:51}} {{t:02:07:21}}
  • He gives a case of women who asked for a handsome, rich husband. They got him, then the husband did nothing else and gave no love. The result matched the command exactly. The fault was the command, not the astrologer or God. {{t:02:07:21}} {{t:02:07:52}}
  • Whoever asked for nothing is given what is right. {{t:02:08:24}}

The example about a husband and wife, and the claim that "God and the astrologer are blamed unfairly", are the speaker's views on relationships.

15. A child's chart, and why remedies cost money {{t:02:08:24}}

Question

When should a parent show a child's kundli to an astrologer? {{t:02:08:24}}

Answer

Whenever the child is in trouble, and in every case with the father's and mother's charts too. A problem with the child means the parents' fortune carries sorrow from the child, often a pitri dosha. {{t:02:08:24}} {{t:02:08:56}}

Remedy

Expect to spend. He says "no free results": a miracle in the occult costs money, as a hospital does. {{t:02:09:27}}

  • After age 15. The speaker says Jupiter shifts and a first-rank child may fail. He says Venus faults can lose biology, Mercury faults can lose maths, Mars faults can mean the child cannot become a doctor even if admitted. {{t:02:10:28}} {{t:02:10:58}}
  • Precaution is like vaccination. You gave polio drops and a diphtheria shot when small. The child now says "it never would have come". Remedies before the problem get the same response. {{t:02:10:58}} {{t:02:11:29}} {{t:02:12:00}}
  • He says a stone costing a lakh or two will serve a hundred years while a phone is obsolete in two. {{t:02:12:00}}
  • You pay in body, mind and wealth. About eight parts in all. {{t:02:12:31}}
  • Rich people spend ten or twenty lakh on surgery without thought. A poor one borrows and still gets cured. The same is true in the occult. {{t:02:09:58}} {{t:02:10:28}}

His claims that "wealthy people get wealthier after paying a Vastu consultant" and about consultants' fees are the speaker's. They are not an endorsement of any fee, and I have not checked them. {{t:02:09:27}} {{t:02:09:58}}

Lal Kitab {{t:02:13:04}}

  • Do Lal Kitab remedies work? He says yes, "100 percent". He does not know the book but does the remedy. {{t:02:13:04}}
  • One he mentions: where the trouble sits in the 4th house, the place of water, put that planet's thing into water. {{t:02:13:04}}
