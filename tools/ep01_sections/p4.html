10. The Bhrigu story: Jupiter, Venus and the written charts {{t:01:07:06}}

The speaker gives the origin story of Jyotish through the sage Bhrigu. He treats the characters as planets: Vishnu is Jupiter, Lakshmi is Venus, and the two are not friends. He tells it as a story (puran means "old tellings"), and says it is not in the Vedas but in the Parashari tradition. {{t:01:07:06}} {{t:01:08:10}}

Bhrigu goes to Vishnu's abode while Vishnu rests and Lakshmi massages his feet. Offended that he was not welcomed, he kicks Vishnu's chest. Vishnu rises, takes Bhrigu's foot and asks if the sage's foot is hurt. Lakshmi, hurt in the heart, curses that she will never go to a Brahmin's house. Bhrigu, angry in turn, says both must be born on earth many times (the captions are unclear on the count). Vishnu pacifies the sage, who then promises to write a text so that Lakshmi will come to the feet of those who know it. The footprint on Krishna's chest in statues is said to be Bhrigu's. {{t:01:08:42}} {{t:01:10:46}} {{t:01:11:17}} {{t:01:12:21}} {{t:01:12:52}} {{t:01:13:22}}

  • Planet reading. Jupiter (knowledge, Vishnu) and Venus (comfort and the heart, Lakshmi) are enemies in this teaching. One is the guru of the gods, the other of the demons. {{t:01:08:42}}
  • When Venus is exalted it can become indulgent. The speaker claims an exalted Venus in the 7th house, the wife's house, can bring divorce, "the best position, yet it divorces". {{t:01:11:17}} {{t:01:11:50}}
  • Bhrigu's text is the Bhrigu Samhita. The speaker says it records three births for a person: how many children, brothers, what problems, what remedies. {{t:01:13:53}} {{t:01:14:25}}
  • Other samhitas exist (he names Arun, Ravan, Lal Kitab) and he calls the whole field "stronger than opium". {{t:01:13:53}}
  • Charts change. Someone may have changed your time line by becoming your guru, so a new reading may differ from an old one. Predictions can shift. If you made no change, the old reading holds ten or twenty years later. {{t:01:14:25}} {{t:01:14:58}}
  • So: astrology is not false. Astrologers change things, and predictions change as consciousness changes. {{t:01:15:30}}
  • Keep a family astrologer like a family doctor, because a remedy that suits you is personal. Also keep a doctor and a friend among astrologers. But beware of a showman who misguides you. {{t:01:15:30}} {{t:01:16:00}} {{t:01:17:02}}
  • Bhrigu's son is Venus (pleasure and joy). Jupiter's grace is what lets you take knowledge. {{t:01:16:31}}

The speaker's reading of Hindu stories as planetary events, his claim about exalted Venus and divorce, and his remarks on communities are his own. They are not verifiable from the video.

12. Which deity to keep at home: set the ishta from the chart {{t:01:43:47}}

The speaker advises not building a temple inside the home. Keep a deity only if the chart supports it, because the wrong one can hurt. {{t:01:43:47}} {{t:01:44:19}}

Problem

Constant quarrels in the home even after keeping many pictures and statues. {{t:01:47:58}} {{t:01:49:33}}

Cause

The deity kept is the "enemy" of a family member's ishta. For example, an enemy-position Jupiter makes Vishnu a bad fit. {{t:01:49:33}} {{t:01:50:05}}

Remedy

Find the ishta in the chart, check the common best deity among the four to five family members, and place the ninth-house deity at home. {{t:01:44:19}} {{t:01:49:33}} {{t:01:51:09}}

  • A kundali shows who suits you. A temple is forbidden in some charts, and the chart can even say "do not do puja", so put the deity as an object and let it work by itself. {{t:01:44:19}}
  • Example, Saturn on a person's 10th sign. The speaker shows how that chart wants no puja. He suggests a Hanuman statue resting while carrying a mountain (Mars is exalted in the 10th sign), and a Buddha statue to quiet the home, which he equates with Mercury, a close friend of Saturn. {{t:01:46:24}} {{t:01:47:28}} {{t:01:47:58}} {{t:01:49:01}}
  • Sun and Saturn together or aspecting each other makes father and son enemies. If Saturn aspects the Sun, "the son beats the father and the father cannot respond". {{t:01:48:30}}
  • God does neither good nor bad. Vishnu hands out fruit. If you cannot take sugar and eat it, it harms you. If your bones are brittle, almonds will not help. Water kills if you put it in someone's nose. The deity is neutral, your karma is yours. {{t:01:50:05}} {{t:01:50:38}} {{t:01:51:09}}
  • For a cupboard or almirah. Put the picture on an iron plate and stick it with a magnet, because iron is the metal of that place. {{t:01:51:09}} {{t:01:51:42}}
  • Foods connected to a deity can be placed in a special place at home as a remedy: a turmeric knot, a yellow or red thread. This is the kind of remedy he says the class will explain. {{t:01:44:19}}

Which deity suits which chart is his teaching. Take it as his view, and check the chart with a trained astrologer before changing the home.
