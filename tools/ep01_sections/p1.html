1. Why this series: grace first, astrology second {{t:00:00:31}}

This is the first class of the Jyotish Vidya series. The speaker's opening claim: astrology (jyotish) is a true science of the unseen, but astrologers can be false. So he says to doubt a particular astrologer, not the subject. The aim of the series is not to make you an astrologer but to teach you how to receive grace (kripa).

Problem

People argue, doubt and suspect. They haggle with a cheap doctor and call an expensive one a thief, even when the expensive one cures them. {{t:00:00:00}}

Cause (speaker's view)

Human consciousness is the first thing to set. Without grace you cannot understand astrology, and your birth chart may even carry a planet that makes you disbelieve it. {{t:00:01:03}} {{t:00:42:49}}

Approach

Learn enough to be like a basic compounder: know which remedy fits which problem. Then set the human mind (Ajna chakra) before the planets. {{t:00:22:25}} {{t:00:02:06}}

  • You are not here to become an astrologer. If your chart does not carry the yoga to be one, no teacher can make you one. You are here to learn to take grace and to do small remedies yourself, like first aid. {{t:00:22:25}}
  • The doctor comparison. The speaker says an astrologer is a doctor of the stars. A doctor fixes calcium and magnesium in the body. An astrologer fixes Jupiter, Mars, Mercury and Rahu in the body. If Saturn has become "too much", remedies stop it from growing further. {{t:00:40:13}} {{t:00:40:43}}
  • Believing in the subject versus the person. You may say "I do not believe in this astrologer". He says it is like not trusting one doctor, which does not mean medical science is false. {{t:00:00:31}} {{t:00:19:52}}
  • The core problem the series sets out to solve: why do people born in the same hour (even a hundred in the same lagna) live different lives? Why did Ram become Ram when other children shared his chart? His answer: human consciousness. {{t:00:01:34}} {{t:00:34:27}}

The speaker states strongly that astrology is "100 percent true". This is his belief and teaching position, not something shown in the video.

The Alexa picture of prayer {{t:00:02:38}}

  • Early in the class a smart speaker interrupts. The speaker uses it as a teaching example: a device obeys the voice it was tuned to, and ignores strangers. {{t:00:03:10}}
  • In the same way, the speaker says money, children, a spouse, even God "listen" once your mental order, wish, firmness and faith are tuned to the cosmic vibration (a cosmic Wi-Fi). {{t:00:03:41}} {{t:00:04:13}}
  • Most people do not know how to connect, so they rub their forehead at temples, mosques and shrines and complain that nothing listens. {{t:00:04:13}}
  • One who is tuned can send a call to the cosmos, and it recognises their voice. A call from just anyone is not heard. {{t:00:04:45}} {{t:00:05:16}}

Para-vigyan: knowledge beyond science {{t:00:05:49}}

  • Knowledge (gyan) comes before science (vigyan). Knowledge belongs to the energy body: it gives taste, sound and sight. Science is what the physical, matter body builds with it, such as a mobile phone. {{t:00:05:49}}
  • Astrology belongs to the "occult" side (para-vigyan, beyond science). So does Tarot, Reiki, pendulum and past-life work. Love is also beyond explanation. {{t:00:06:20}}
  • Whether astrology is true can only be found by trying it. Life, he says, beats people into wisdom, and he has seen it in many lives. {{t:00:06:52}} {{t:00:07:24}}
  • It is right-brain, not left-brain. Logic will not carry it. Only grace does. {{t:00:23:57}}
  • Knowledge is useless without wisdom, and wisdom comes by grace. {{t:00:21:55}}

2. Prarabdha: the fixed part and the part you add {{t:00:07:55}}

Before any planet, the speaker explains prarabdha (what you carry in from past lives). He says about 12 percent of life is already fixed, and the rest is where your consciousness and effort act. {{t:00:07:55}}

The bank balance example {{t:00:08:28}}

  • Say that over the last five years you saved 5 lakh, bought a bike and a one-room flat. That is your balance when you wake. It is also your starting point tomorrow. {{t:00:08:28}}
  • That same balance includes loans and a borrowed suit you must return. Debts come due on their day. {{t:00:08:58}}
  • A newborn is the same. The family you are born into, rich or poor, and the debts you carry, were set by past-life karma. {{t:00:08:58}} {{t:00:09:29}}
  • New actions (gambling, a fixed deposit, indulgence) are added on top, but the old balance does not leave you. The bank still wants its money four years later. {{t:00:09:29}}

The tunnel {{t:00:10:01}}

  • Prarabdha is a tunnel running from birth to death. It has turns and loops, and at each loop you may go to a different place. But you still go through the same fixed stops. {{t:00:10:01}}
  • If you insist on going one way, the tunnel throws you back until you take its path. Free will is only as much as a horse's rope allows: left or right. {{t:00:15:07}} {{t:00:15:40}}
  • Even Ram, he says, only did what was written. {{t:00:15:07}}

A story of three people and a quota {{t:00:10:01}}

The speaker tells of a man who is destined to meet two particular people in this life and to spend a fixed number of years with each, like a quota. He loves the first but circumstances start his life with the second. Years later the old love returns and the marriage cracks. He then spends the leftover years with the first through meetings and quarrels. The new fights he creates along the way become fresh karma for the next birth, where the two may meet only to fight. {{t:00:10:32}} {{t:00:12:34}} {{t:00:13:04}}

  • The point he draws: a debt owed has to be met. A person who owes you may borrow from you or may come back to help you. You cannot decide this. {{t:00:10:32}} {{t:00:11:02}}
  • Reacting in anger adds new karma. To finish old karma without adding, do not react. {{t:00:25:24}}

Why love dies after marriage: the speaker says marriage exists for sex (a licence for it), that it is a hunger of the sacral (swadhisthana) chakra, and that what many call love is lust, which dies. This is his personal view, not an astrological rule. {{t:00:13:34}} {{t:00:14:06}} {{t:00:14:37}}
