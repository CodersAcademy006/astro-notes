8. Why one chart alone can mislead near a sign's edge {{t:01:19:39}}

The class closes its main teaching with a worked demonstration of why a reading needs more than the plain birth (rashi) chart: the cuspal (chalit), sidereal (nirayana) and KP charts can each shift a planet into a different house, especially when the ascendant sits close to the edge of a sign. {{t:01:19:39}} {{t:01:20:41}}

  • With an ascendant only a few degrees into a sign, the neighbouring sign is often still partly "active" on the same house, so two or three signs can genuinely share influence over one placement at once. {{t:01:21:44}} {{t:01:22:45}}
  • In his demonstration chart, a debt-linked Mars placement that looked ordinary in the plain chart became far stronger once the chalit chart was added, changing the practical advice from caution to "this person should take on debt deliberately." {{t:01:23:17}} {{t:01:23:48}}
  • The same cross-check could reduce a seemingly clear indicator elsewhere (he gives a case where a plain chart suggested divorce, but the fuller picture instead pointed to an outside relationship without a legal separation). This is his own reading method for one example chart, not a general claim about anyone's marriage. {{t:01:25:52}} {{t:01:26:24}}
  • His takeaway: predictions from a single chart, without checking the chalit, nirayana, KP and navamsa charts together, are only a first, rough pass, the way a doctor starts with a basic test before ordering something more specific. {{t:01:35:18}} {{t:01:35:50}}
  • He adds that the navamsa chart becomes fully active only after marriage, so a person's placements can genuinely add new effects at that point in life that were not visible before. {{t:01:34:47}} {{t:01:35:18}}
