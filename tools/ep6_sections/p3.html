5. Mercury: speech, business, and why it ruins things in the Moon's house {{t:00:53:50}}

Mercury, the speaker says, is a "daughter" planet: it governs speech and trade, but only works safely under the Sun's protection, so how you speak matters as much as what you say. {{t:00:53:50}} {{t:00:56:28}}

When Mercury is afflicted

Stinginess if the fault is one kind, or non-stop, pointless chatter if it is another. A retrograde Mercury can bring a stammer, broken confidence and a feeling of words catching in the throat. {{t:00:55:25}} {{t:00:57:00}}

His reasoning

Mercury depends entirely on the Sun for protection, the way a small child holds a parent's finger. Placed in Mercury's own sign or the Sun's, it is fine; placed in the Moon's sign (the 4th), he says it ruins whatever house it occupies, failing exams, breaking marriages, even impotence if it lands on the children's house. {{t:00:58:36}} {{t:00:59:09}} {{t:00:59:40}}

Remedy

Speak with measure, build a small community of five to seven people you look after (Mercury is said to strengthen through group care), and seek a guru's guidance to learn to speak without anger. {{t:00:56:28}} {{t:01:01:11}} {{t:01:02:16}}

  • Mercury's colour is green, tied to vegetation and the business community; he calls shopkeepers and traders especially blessed by it. {{t:01:00:10}}
  • He extends the stammer-and-silence idea to a claim that women who are kept from speaking freely at home can develop thyroid trouble from a combined Rahu-Mercury fault. This is the speaker's own belief, not a medical finding, and is not repeated here as support for restricting anyone's speech. {{t:00:57:00}} {{t:00:57:33}}
  • A retrograde Mercury needs an emerald (panna); he repeats the "universal law" that cost is not a reason to skip it, comparing it to the arrival of eyeglasses once ending a lifetime of slow blindness from cataracts. {{t:01:02:47}} {{t:01:03:57}}
  • Mercury behaves with a soft, feminine temperament whatever the wearer's own sex; speaking sweetly and staying clean and tidy is said to keep it active and "polished". {{t:01:05:32}} {{t:01:06:03}}

The claim connecting restricted speech in women to thyroid disease is the speaker's own view and is not supported medically; thyroid disorders have medical causes and need a doctor, not a gemstone.

6. Rahu: the space element, carefree speech, and why rank is earned, not born {{t:01:11:32}}

Rahu gets its own detailed turn: the speaker ties it to the space element, to a near-magical confidence in speech he calls "vaak siddhi", and to a long explanation of caste (varna) as something earned through role rather than birth. {{t:01:11:32}} {{t:01:12:22}}

  • Rahu is pure space. Nothing burns in space, he says, which is why the south-east zone (Rahu's area in his vastu scheme) should stay closed off from fire and kept undisturbed for ancestors' grace. {{t:01:12:22}} {{t:01:12:54}}
  • Vaak siddhi. A person who stops reacting, who "sees, hears, speaks and does not act" like a renunciate, can reach a point where whatever they say carelessly tends to come true, the way people wait for a line from a famous saint. {{t:01:13:26}} {{t:01:17:35}}

His reading of varna (caste) through a mother's roles {{t:01:21:45}}

He argues a mother has no fixed caste: she is "shudra" when cleaning a baby, "kshatriya" when protecting a growing child, "brahmin" when teaching right from wrong, and "vaishya" when managing the family's money as the child grows up, so a woman's role shifts with the task rather than birth. This is the speaker's own reading of the varna system, not a settled or uncontested one, and is presented here only as his argument, not as fact. {{t:01:21:45}} {{t:01:22:46}}

  • Remedy. Respect anyone doing low-status or unskilled work (sweepers, unskilled labourers) as Saturn's and Rahu's agents; a blue thread, cheap and widely used because Rahu is hard for other planets to override, is his most-repeated Rahu fix. {{t:01:20:10}} {{t:01:21:14}} {{t:01:21:45}}
  • Mother Saraswati (knowledge and speech) is paired with Rahu here; being genuinely carefree, not performing carelessness, is what he says actually opens this placement. {{t:01:16:02}} {{t:01:18:37}}
