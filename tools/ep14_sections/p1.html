2. Hair, Rahu and Ketu: why Mercury needs order first {{t:00:11:02}}

The speaker ties Mercury (the thinking mind) to Rahu and Ketu through the head. His practical rule: tie up the hair and you tie up Rahu.

Problem

Loose, messy hair. In the speaker's frame it is Rahu unset: lust, restlessness and an unsteady mind. {{t:00:11:35}}

Why

Rahu is the unruly head energy. A bun, band or comb (Ketu) binds it. Matted locks of ascetics are the same idea. {{t:00:11:02}}

Fix

Comb and tie the hair, men and women. Curly hair too. Cover the head with a scarf, turban or dupatta. {{t:00:14:10}} {{t:00:15:44}}

  • The speaker says a topknot (shikha) lets heat leave the head, where the two brain halves meet and cerebrospinal fluid flows. The explanation is his own, not an established medical claim. {{t:00:14:41}}
  • He says head covering across religions is the same Saturn-Rahu setting. When hair turns white, he says, the need to cover eases. {{t:00:15:44}}
  • Hair, the band and the comb map to Rahu, Ketu (the band and comb) and Mercury (the order that results). {{t:00:12:06}}
  • Mercury and money: Mercury is paper and green notes. Donations at a place of worship are Mercury offered at the feet of Jupiter, his father figure in the story, so the mind does not wander. {{t:00:12:36}}
