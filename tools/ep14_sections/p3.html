7. Where Mercury hurts: houses 6, 8 and 12 {{t:00:55:35}}

The speaker reads remedies from the main (lagna) chart first, whatever the exact degree of the ascendant. Mercury owns signs 3 and 6, so those two signs and the houses they land on decide the result.

Mercury in 6, 8 or 12

The speaker says a person with Mercury here is expert at making enemies: always arguing, scared, creating strife. {{t:00:55:35}}

Mercury in the 6th, for marriage

He checks whether the boy or the girl has Mercury in the 6th. If so, his advice is not to marry. Expect a sharp tongue and anger. {{t:00:56:06}}

Mercury in the 12th

Highly negative in any sign. Said to block children, disturb the sex life, scatter the mind outward, give irritation and poor sleep. {{t:00:57:09}}

  • The exception. A 12th-house Mercury that sits in the signs of Jupiter and Ketu (9 and 12) drops its restlessness and is not bad. In its own sign 3 it is very good: it brings money, spends it and controls speech. {{t:00:57:42}}
  • Sign matters. If the sign changes to 4, 5 or any other, the whole result changes. The speaker is not using transits here. {{t:00:58:12}}
  • Worst case. A 12th-house Mercury sitting in the 6th looks at the 12th and becomes "highly destructive". {{t:01:04:28}}
  • Best case. Mercury in the 4th is called the best. Mercury in signs 3 and 6 is free from most faults. {{t:01:03:56}}

Nose-piercing remedy for 12th-house Mercury {{t:00:58:12}}

  • Rule. If Mercury is in the 12th, in any sign, or in the 9th, pierce the centre of the nose for 100 hours. {{t:00:58:44}}
  • How. Silver stud, then remove it after four days and keep the hole closed with surgical thread. Go to a doctor for the piercing. Many tribes keep nose and ear piercing for life. {{t:00:59:16}}
  • Who. Girls can pierce the side if the centre is hard to afford socially. The speaker says men used to do it too. {{t:00:58:44}}
  • Why. The tip of the nose is a Mercury point. 100 hours is his round number; 96 (four days) is also quoted. {{t:00:59:48}}

The speaker also says a nose ring can be used to "rein in" a woman and that a girl with Moon and Mercury together will not get along with other women. These are his views and not astrology rules. Piercing should be done hygienically by a professional.

10. Emerald, dashas and one-time fixes {{t:01:07:36}}

The speaker's logic: a stone does not add or remove power. It wakes the owner of the house where the planet sits. This is the speaker's philosophy and is not accepted by all astrologers, and gemstones are costly. Take your own chart read by someone you trust before buying anything.

  • When. If Mercury is retrograde and the sign is not at fault, wear an emerald. If the sign is 3, he says wear it only with guidance. {{t:01:07:36}}
  • Quality. 4 to 6 ratti. Cheap stones will not work. No black line, no crack. Zambian and Colombian are best. Prices run from about 5,000 up to 5 lakh. {{t:01:08:06}}
  • The reason. A stone is a once-for-all fix, like a vaccine, glasses or surgery, while small remedies are like a painkiller: they work but come back. {{t:01:08:38}}
  • Wake the owner. If Mercury lies in a house whose owner sleeps drunk elsewhere, wake the owner. Even if Mercury is debilitated in 12, you set signs 3 and 6 with an emerald. {{t:01:09:40}}
  • Dasha vs chart. Dashas keep changing and life looks the same for years. Set the main chart first, then look at dashas. Transits are the same for everyone. {{t:01:12:47}}
  • Combust or retrograde signs. Weak maths, endless arguing, counting notes twice, a child saying "Mama, I missed 20 marks" after a paper that was full. {{t:01:10:11}}

Example: sign 3 in the 12th

Mercury in the 12th from the home, in sign 3: endless fights with spouse and mother, ill health and no property solution. Needs an emerald. {{t:01:11:45}}

Why

The house owner has gone to the 8th or the 6th and "lies dead". The house is not being served. {{t:01:12:16}}

Fix

Set the sign owner first. Then add the stone for any planet Mercury sits with, such as Saturn, Mars or a retrograde one. {{t:01:13:18}}

  • Seriousness. The speaker says do not kill the child in you. Stay playful; seriousness belongs on a hospital bed. Jupiter's rule keeps Mercury obedient. {{t:01:10:43}} {{t:01:11:14}}
  • A strong Mercury makes a politician. A damaged, combust or retrograde one weakens a political career. {{t:01:11:14}}
  • Pay once. The speaker's analogy to repeated small payments to an astrologer, or to a doctor, is that cheap remedies bring you back again and again. A good fix once costs less in the long run. {{t:01:13:50}}

Remarks on doctors, surgeons and astrologers' fees are the speaker's opinion. Health decisions should follow your doctor's advice.
