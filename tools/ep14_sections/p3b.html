Mercury in the 12th

Do not pass cash or paper money hand to hand to your husband or wife. The speaker says Mercury is touched and the trouble starts. {{t:01:16:26}}

Why

Cash is Mercury. A debilitated Mercury handed through the partner's hand carries its fault into the home. Put money on a table, or give goods instead of cash. {{t:01:17:27}}

Fix

Keep it case-specific. It is not a rule about all money dealings, only for this placement. {{t:01:17:59}}

  • Children. The speaker says 12th-house Mercury in the 7th house makes natural conception hard and that even IVF or IUI may fail. He suggests a Pitru Shanti at Gaya. These are his claims, not medical advice. {{t:01:17:27}}
  • Cash at a place of worship. With debilitated Mercury in the 9th or in a dharma position, giving cash there sets life on a hard path. Give goods instead. {{t:01:18:29}}
  • Body parts. He ties Mercury to knee joints, bones below the waist, the intestines (constipation then loose motions) and, with a Jupiter fault, the liver, jaundice and hepatitis. A statement from the speaker, not a medical one. {{t:01:18:29}} {{t:01:19:01}} {{t:01:19:32}}
  • Receiving is fine. You can take cash from your spouse. The restriction is only on giving it hand to hand. {{t:01:19:32}}

  • Mercury in sign 9 or 12. Treat both alike, as the fault. It is a flaw in both signs. But the owners of those signs (Jupiter and Ketu) do not take offence at it. {{t:01:21:37}}
  • Friend of both sides. Like Durga, Mercury has devotees among gods and demons. It adjusts to anyone: jokes, wisdom, abuse. Its one real harm is to the Moon. {{t:01:22:11}}
  • Quicksilver. The metal is liquid and will not mix with sulphuric acid (Mars). Swallowed, it damages the kidney (water) and brain (water). The speaker draws from this that foul words and quarrels shrink the kidney and heat the head. {{t:01:22:44}}
  • Talkers live long. An active Mercury keeps you from dying early, because it is Saturn's and Pluto's friend. The speaker points to old people who keep talking. {{t:01:23:17}}
  • Silence of the mind. Silence in speech wakes Saturn, who is Lakshmi. But if the mind keeps going while the mouth is shut, you cannot become rich. Wealth needs Mercury and Saturn together. {{t:01:23:48}}
  • "I cannot hear it, do not tell me." The speaker says repeating this harms eyes and ears and leaves one lonely and low on energy. His claim. {{t:01:24:18}}
