QuestionThe speaker's answer
Is kaal sarp dosh (a Rahu-Ketu affliction) real, and does everyone need it treated?He says it is tied to Rahu and Ketu's placement but claims the overwhelming majority of people who are told they have it do not; he calls the widespread paid rituals for it mostly unnecessary. {{t:01:58:42}}
What actually happens when Ketu is badly placed?Unlike most planets, which he says mainly affect the house they sit in, he claims Ketu's effect spreads very broadly across many houses at once, which is why its damage is so hard to isolate. {{t:01:59:43}}
What is the one daily remedy for keeping Ketu balanced?Stop doubting, stop assuming the worst about people and outcomes, and stop letting suspicion colour a situation before it has actually happened. {{t:02:02:48}}
Should alternate-nostril breathing always start on the left?He says not to overthink left versus right and instead let the sushumna (the central channel in this system) regulate the two automatically, calling conscious left/right switching unnecessary effort. {{t:02:14:24}}
Is reading scripture necessary if you already have a teacher's guidance?He says it is optional, not mandatory, since in his own case he considers himself largely self-taught rather than scripture-trained, and recommends taking whatever blessing or guidance is genuinely available instead of treating textual study as compulsory. {{t:02:05:25}}
Why explain all of this through science rather than simply stating the religious teaching?He says tying it to science quiets the analytical ("left-brain") objections that would otherwise block someone from following the teaching at all. {{t:02:06:59}}
Should a stated intention or command be repeated daily, or said once and left alone?He recommends repeating it daily until it resolves, sometimes over a year or more, rather than treating one statement as sufficient. {{t:02:10:42}}
Buried in debt with no clear way out, what should be done?He says to accept the situation fully, act with total confidence anyway, and stop trying to calculate exactly where the money will come from; he is explicit that this will not work for most people who merely imitate the attitude without genuine conviction. {{t:02:08:40}}
What keeps a household's northeast energy, and its peace, intact?He directs this specifically at how spouses treat each other day to day, saying a wife who is made to grieve or feel unheard damages the home's energy, and a husband's foolishness does the same; both notes are his own, and these notes record them as his claims about marital conduct rather than settled fact. {{t:02:13:22}}
