7. General remedies for Mercury {{t:00:52:40}}

Remedies the speaker gives as broadly useful whenever Mercury is troubled, before he turns to house-specific fixes.

  • Alum (fitkari), for the evil eye. Hold a piece of alum, circle it anticlockwise three times above the head in an open space (not indoors, where salt is used instead), then throw it behind you. {{t:00:53:12}}
  • Green cloth, given to a hijra or a similar neuter-planet representative, ideally something they ask for directly (clothing, a kitchen item) rather than only cash. The speaker's reasoning: let them earn and prosper like anyone else rather than resenting their income. The speaker's own social and religious framing; presented as his personal view of a tradition. {{t:01:40:43}}
  • Whole green moong dal (mung beans), given as charity, placed in a temple, or given to children at a school, is called the easiest Mercury donation because it is simple food that gets eaten. {{t:01:08:55}}
  • Keep a pet goat, or feed one; a goat is also linked to Mercury in his scheme. {{t:00:54:16}}
  • Keep a pet parrot or otherwise care for one: parrots are called "intelligent" in speech (they can be taught words) yet eat almost anything, which the speaker reads as a living picture of Mercury. {{t:01:14:38}}
  • Plant a tree is named as another simple, everyday Mercury remedy. {{t:01:04:46}}
  • Keep accounts, write by hand on plain paper (not glossy or decorated paper) when your Mercury is in good shape; if it is afflicted, the advice is the opposite, keep rougher, informal notes rather than careful ledgers, since the speaker claims writing itself can make money "disappear" for a badly placed Mercury. {{t:01:05:17}}
  • Befriend a trader or shopkeeper (bania) as a way of keeping company with Mercury's natural energy. {{t:01:02:09}}
  • Keep currency organised, not crumpled, notes facing the same way, without counting it obsessively; an approximate sense of how much you have is enough, exact counting is said to stunt its growth. {{t:01:03:11}}
  • An emerald (panna), Zambian or Colombian preferred, worn in the smallest finger, is the speaker's main gemstone remedy for an afflicted or retrograde Mercury. He stresses it is the most fragile major gemstone: a cheap stone can crack within months, so a stone should be checked for quality rather than assumed to be faulty when it chips. Gemstone advice is the speaker's own practice; it is not medical, legal or financial advice, and any stone should only be worn after a qualified astrologer has read the full chart. {{t:01:51:06}}
  • Donate stationery items at a school, including one set up for blind students, as another easy Mercury donation alongside whole moong dal. {{t:01:09:55}}
  • For a Mercury placed in the 4th house specifically, the speaker gives a small ritual: wash four leaves from a palash or peepal tree in raw milk and release them into flowing water. {{t:01:16:12}}
  • Smaller everyday items he also assigns to Mercury: a cap, a belt, and sand. {{t:01:16:43}}
  • A bat (the flying mammal, a creature of the night air) is another animal he files under Mercury, alongside the goat and parrot. {{t:01:09:25}}

The speaker works around the chart from the 1st house. Mercury's own strong houses are the 3rd (Gemini) and 6th (Virgo); he treats the 4th and 12th as unusually protective; 6th and 12th together, or 7th and 12th together, as the ones to be most careful about.

Problem

Business, money lending, stock market or crypto investment losses that the person "never recovers from," plus the risk of court cases and, in bad cases, jail. {{t:01:22:58}}

Why: the 6th/12th rule

Mercury in the 6th or the 12th house (any sign) is treated as the single clearest "never go into business" signal in the chart, because decisions taken under this placement are said to work against the person like an axe against their own foot, sooner or later. A strong financial claim tied to a chart placement; the speaker's own predictive framework, not financial advice. {{t:01:22:58}}

What he advises

Avoid business, lending and speculative investing outright if Mercury sits in the 6th or 12th house of either the main chart or the navamsha (a divisional chart), unless a qualified astrologer has reviewed the full chart and specifically says otherwise. If the 3rd or 6th sign itself (Gemini or Virgo) is well placed there, the damage is said to be contained to "not ruining your own house," even though losses still happen. {{t:01:35:02}}

He adds one further escalation: on its own, this placement mainly limits itself to financial loss, but if a poorly placed Mars is also involved, he says it can turn into debt serious enough to end in a police station or a courtroom. A strong financial and legal claim; the speaker's own predictive framework, not legal advice. {{t:01:35:32}}
