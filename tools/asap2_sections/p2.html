3. Mars: the Earth's bodyguard {{t:00:14:08}}

The picture

Mars sits at the navel, pictured as Hanuman: the army, the commander-in-chief who takes the hits so the kingdom doesn't have to. {{t:00:14:40}}

His asteroid-belt story

Space debris ("cosmic junk") drifts toward Earth. Jupiter's gravity catches most of it and flings the rest onward; whatever still gets through is absorbed by Mars, which sits just in front of Earth and "takes the beating" so Earth stays safe. {{t:00:15:12}}

Why it matters

This is his reasoning for calling Mars Earth's dearest friend (mitra): Hanuman silently absorbed every hardship in Ram's story without complaint, and that silent absorption is what friendship means in his scheme. {{t:00:15:43}}

  • Jupiter sits at the sacral chakra (swadhishthana) and governs reproduction and manufacturing broadly: everything built, a house, a car, a body in the womb, draws on this energy, radiating out from the body into the cosmos as the thing takes shape. {{t:00:16:46}}
  • He separates this from Saturn's job at the base of the spine: Jupiter builds new things in this life, while Saturn's zone stores the backlog of karma carried over from past lives, which decides what you are actually allowed to build and when. {{t:00:17:16}}
  • Varuna (grouped with the outer planets in his scheme) is linked to an extra, otherwise unnecessary vein near the heart that doctors use for cardiac procedures; he reads this as the body being pre-built with a safety margin for exactly the kind of damage a heart attack causes. {{t:00:17:47}}
  • Indra, also placed near the navel in his map, is linked to growth hormones generally. {{t:00:17:47}}
  • Ketu sits with Jupiter in his map and governs the sexual organs and the lower, headless parts of the body, continuing the "headless deity" theme covered in other classes. {{t:00:18:19}}
  • Pluto (Yamraj, the lord of death and of dharma) is linked to secrecy: death, in his telling, never announces itself, and a "good death" is one that arrives quietly rather than being dragged out. {{t:00:18:50}}

4. The Sun through the signs, part one: houses 1 to 6 {{t:00:22:58}}

The core of the class: what the Sun does when it sits in each of the twelve signs (he counts by sign position, which he also calls "house" in this shorthand), read as the Sun as king visiting each other deity's home.

SignWhose houseWhat the Sun does there
1stMars (commander)Extremely strong and wilful; "I won't listen to anyone." People born on the 1st of a month show this pattern too, in his numerology aside. {{t:00:23:29}}
2ndVenus (pleasure)Weak and drained; the king's energy, wealth and authority get "used up" by Venus's household comforts. He adds a gendered stereotype here, that this placement gives a man a tendency toward indirect, "feminine" conflict-handling; a disputed, unverified claim. {{t:00:25:35}}
3rdMercury (open sky)Blunt, plain-spoken, very good with money and conversation. In a woman's chart he says this directness is treated as unusual, while the same bluntness in a man is considered normal, a double standard he notes without resolving. {{t:00:28:13}}
4thMoon (mother)Frequent foreign travel, property built away from home, a well-known name; some chance of the mother's (or in a few cases the father's) health suffering. {{t:00:28:44}}
5thSun's own sign"Royal": generous without being asked twice, lucky and devoted children, buys only branded or quality goods. {{t:00:30:17}}
6thRahu, in his schemeDebt, disease and enemies all get destroyed; distance from mother, siblings and family; behaves like a tyrant toward opponents but cannot actually be defeated by them. {{t:00:31:21}}
  • "Commander yoga". He describes a combination where the Sun sits in Mars's sign as a "commander" pattern: such a person manages a household or team exceptionally well, but should let someone else make the final call rather than deciding alone, the way a real army commander executes a king's decision rather than making it. {{t:00:24:33}}
  • His household skit for the 2nd-house placement: an exaggerated scene where a mother always smooths over conflict diplomatically rather than scolding directly, leaving the father to play the "bad cop"; he uses it to contrast the Moon's diplomatic style with the Sun's blunt one. {{t:00:25:03}}
  • A 2nd-house Sun also marks the eyes. One eye gets a defect, there is head heaviness, and he claims brain tissue can "dry out" under this placement; a direct hit (not just an aspect) on this sign, he adds, guarantees a scar or injury to the head or face at some point. {{t:00:27:11}}
  • He repeats throughout this section that the sign-by-sign reading is only the first, simplified layer: the actual planet sitting in that house, not just the sign label, decides the real outcome, and he warns against treating this list as a complete reading on its own. {{t:00:29:16}}
  • His concrete picture of 5th-house generosity: such a person gives more than asked without being prompted twice, for example handing over 500 when only a small favour was requested, and does it so warmly that the receiver doesn't feel they have to ask again. {{t:00:30:48}}
  • His description of the 6th-house Sun's attitude: "I alone exist" (main hi main hoon), a near-tyrannical self-focus toward rivals, even while the person stays personally undefeated by them. {{t:00:31:53}}
  • A Sun below 6 degrees in any sign is "faint" (nistej) in his reading, as if newly risen and not yet carrying its full force. {{t:00:27:11}}
  • The Sun destroys whatever house it occupies to some degree, and also damages the house directly opposite it (its 7th from itself); he flags stomach trouble as the generic cost of a Sun in the 7th sign from the lagna. {{t:00:27:42}}
  • A 2nd or 7th Sun, he adds, tends to bring kidney stones, gallbladder stones or heart trouble, varying with exact degree; surgery is not guaranteed, only a real possibility. {{t:00:33:56}}

The remark comparing a blunt-speaking woman unfavourably to a blunt-speaking man, and the claim linking a sign placement to "feminine" conflict style, are the speaker's own gendered generalisations. These notes do not endorse them.
