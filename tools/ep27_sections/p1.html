1. What Rahu is and how it behaves {{t:00:00:33}}

Rahu is a shadow planet (chhaya graha). It has a head and no body. Everything it does follows from that one fact: it can see, hear and think, but it cannot act, enjoy or feel. Ketu is the body (the headless torso), so action belongs to Ketu.

PointWhat the speaker says
Origin storyAt the churning of the ocean, Vishnu in Mohini form cut the demon in two. The head is Rahu, the torso is Ketu. The speaker reads the story as a way to explain a real astronomical event: the Moon or Earth blocking the Sun's light. {{t:00:00:33}}
What Rahu stands forIllusion, confusion, the single thought that blocks vision. When you stare at one thing out of 400 and the mind gets stuck there, that is Rahu. {{t:00:03:05}}
Two faces of RahuAfflicted: the ordinary person caught in illusion, asking questions with no answer. Awakened (sakshi bhav, witness mind): the sage who sees that the blockage is only a block. Both are Rahu. {{t:00:02:35}}
What it damagesMind (Moon), intellect (Mercury) and discrimination (Jupiter). Rahu can destroy all three or wake all three. {{t:00:04:10}}
Why it is a magnetIt pulls the power of whatever it sits with and turns into it. So it works both ways, good or bad, depending on what it is attached to. {{t:00:09:20}}

The eclipse lesson: the 7.5 minute window {{t:00:04:10}}

Light takes about 8 minutes to reach Earth, and the speaker says an eclipse is a window of at most 7 to 7.5 minutes. He uses this as a rule for daily life:

  • When someone says something and your mind goes blank with anger, that is your own small eclipse.
  • Hold yourself for those few minutes and Rahu is set. It then makes you wise.
  • If you start a fight inside that window, Ketu (the body) carries out what the corrupted mind decided, and the damage is done.
  • He says a further effect of about three days will be covered in the Ketu class.

Speaker's own framework, not a classical rule.

Rahu dasha: the 18 year loop {{t:00:05:43}}

Problem

Rahu grabs one thought and hammers it daily. Old grudges are dragged along for years: what someone said 4 years ago, what a relative did 40 years ago. No forgiveness, either of others or of yourself. Life turns into a loop.

Why

Rahu has no body, so it cannot enjoy food, comfort or physical pleasure. What is left is hearing, seeing and speaking without doing. {{t:00:06:44}}

Solution

Practise kshama (forgiveness). Spot the one repeating thought and deliberately drop it. Keep the body active and enjoy food, which feeds Ketu. {{t:00:06:44}}

The speaker's modern example of this loop: a train coach where all 40 people are on their phones, scrolling reel after reel. That is Rahu holding the whole family, and it eats time. {{t:00:07:15}}

Strong Rahu: stambhan, and the in-law connection {{t:00:07:48}}

  • Stambhan means silencing others. A strong Rahu means people cannot speak in front of you. The speaker notes wives with strong Rahu often silence their husbands.
  • Sasural (in-laws) and nanihal (maternal side) are Rahu. Warm relations with them mean a healthy Rahu. {{t:00:08:18}}
  • Warning sign of afflicted Rahu: no love link with maternal uncle or maternal grandparents, nobody living on the maternal side, or constant friction with in-laws. Then the mind stays blocked and cannot learn anything new.
  • If Rahu is awakened into witness mind, you can pick up new knowledge without a guru.
  • Rahu with Mercury. The speaker calls Mercury the sky (Durga) and Rahu the blue of the sky and the sea (Saraswati). Rahu is friendly to Mercury. {{t:00:08:50}}

2. Rahu in the 7th house {{t:00:09:53}}

Covers partnership: marriage, business, friendship, sex and children through love. The speaker notes houses 1 to 6 were covered in the previous class.

AreaWhat happens
MarriageCouples only talk and listen. There is no body to act, so no duty, service, love or physical closeness. The relationship goes stale. {{t:00:10:26}}
MindRestless. It wants intimacy and partnership with everyone but cannot carry any of it out. {{t:00:10:56}}
BusinessPartnership gets swallowed. Friend becomes partner, partner betrays. The speaker links the 7th sign to Neptune (Varuna, the ocean): vast, showy, deceptive, intoxicating. {{t:00:11:27}}
If Rahu is strongBrilliant, magnetic, people become infatuated. A "captain of the ship" and a leader. He becomes the boss who does not work, with the best car and foreign trips, and the staff resent it. Many relationships, mostly through conversation. But family life does not sit well with it. {{t:00:12:30}}

Problem

Betrayal in partnership, loss of love, loss of the spouse's physical presence, stale relationship.

Cause

7th house is where Rahu has no body to act. The ocean-like showiness hides the truth. {{t:00:11:58}}

Remedies and rules

  • Avoid business partnerships. If you must, expect betrayal at any time and stay in witness mind. {{t:00:13:31}}
  • Do not mix friendship with love.
  • Never put land or property in a friend's name, even a best friend's. {{t:00:14:02}}
  • Do not over-sacrifice for a spouse, child or friend. It will not be returned with love. Treat it as duty, like a nurse, not as love. Even with God, relate by rule and duty. {{t:00:14:32}}
  • Shiva worship. The speaker says only Shiva can set Rahu, because Rahu can strike any planet including the Moon. {{t:00:15:34}}
  • Silver brick at marriage. The bride should bring a small silver brick (eent, thicker than a biscuit) to her husband's house at the wedding. It stands for the Moon. {{t:00:15:02}}

The reasoning given: Shiva wears the Moon on the head to hold the upper portal shut so the drop of energy (amrit chakra) does not escape upward. The silver brick brings that Moon into the marriage so the mind bond between husband and wife stays strong. This is the speaker's own interpretation.

3. Rahu in the 8th house {{t:00:15:34}}

The house of death, the occult, mystery and Indra's place.

  • Elephant rule. Treat Rahu here as an elephant. A grey elephant means it has merged with Ketu. A white elephant is Airavat, Indra's elephant, and is supremely powerful. {{t:00:16:04}}
  • Gifts when well placed: knowledge of the secret of death, tantra power, the stambhan skill. A single look can silence someone. The speaker says such power needs the Moon's grace, and in the natural zodiac (Kalpurush) chart the Moon is debilitated in the 8th. {{t:00:16:36}}
  • When afflicted: a mind full of illness and death thoughts, constant talk of dying "in instalments", deep fear. A person who lies, steals and cheats. Indra gets his heaven stolen, so enjoyment is lost. {{t:00:17:07}}
  • Life is a ping-pong: king one day, beggar the next, power lost and regained. {{t:00:18:39}}
  • Either a super common-sense tantric, or a fool who is never able to understand anything however much it is explained. {{t:00:18:08}}

Problem

Fear of death, deceit, mental blockage, heaven repeatedly snatched away.

Cause

Rahu swallows Indra, the lord of heaven. The speaker adds that the pineal gland (the third eye) is 100% water and calcifies, and that Ganga water works on that principle (his own view). {{t:00:18:08}}

Remedies

  • Stop white things after sunset. {{t:00:16:36}}
  • Add a little Gangajal to your drinking water. Any clean, holy water works, as the effect comes through the faith in it. {{t:00:17:07}}
  • Carry a solid silver ball (thos goli), in pocket, purse or locker. It acts as a solid Moon and works for any sign when Rahu is in the 8th. {{t:00:18:39}}
  • Do not spend your life bathing in the Ganga every week while the problem is in the mind.

4. Rahu in the 9th house {{t:00:19:09}}

The house of dharma, guru, father and luck.

  • Rahu here is nearly debilitated. The person is a bedharmi, running their own religion, with heavy struggle. {{t:00:19:09}}
  • The core problem is decisions. The head has no body, so every decision you make cannot be carried out properly. Anyone who takes decisions with Rahu in the 9th lives a hard life. {{t:00:21:45}}
  • Seesaw rule: give power to Ketu and Rahu goes down; give power to Rahu and Ketu goes down. They sit opposite each other. If someone has climbed too high, bringing them down is Rahu's job. {{t:00:19:41}}
  • Rahu suppresses the Sun. If prana is so strong the Sun would kill, Rahu holds it down. If the Sun is badly placed it can show as blindness, stomach trouble, osteoporosis or heart attack. The speaker says the so-called sinful planets keep us alive. {{t:00:20:13}}
  • Too much of anything is poison. Excess puja, excess intoxication, excess charity (giving away the whole house) all make you a bedharmi. {{t:00:20:44}}
  • Lineage warning. Houses 1, 5 and 9 represent family, children and grandchildren. Rahu here can block children, or children born and lost. Being a parent is very hard. {{t:00:21:15}}
  • Beatings in childhood (teacher at school, parents at home) and risk of accidental death. {{t:00:23:18}}

Problem

Wrong decisions, defiance of dharma, trouble with children, family lineage disrupted, childhood punishment, accident risk.

Cause

Rahu's head decides but has no body. The mind refuses a guru and will not accept another's advice. {{t:00:21:45}}

Remedies

  • Install Ketu: follow a guru. Let someone else guide the body and stop taking big decisions yourself. {{t:00:19:41}}
  • Strengthen Jupiter. {{t:00:21:15}}
  • If the person will not accept a guru, find one with an even stronger Rahu, someone who can overpower them. That person will listen. {{t:00:21:45}}
  • Check Jupiter and Mercury in the chart: combust, retrograde, in Saturn's sign, or afflicted by sign. {{t:00:21:45}}
  • Do gurudwara, temple and dharma sthan upay, giving the items of the house the planet is in, depending on the sign. {{t:00:22:16}}
  • If Mercury is involved, worship Durga or honour a young girl who has not yet reached menarche (kanjak) with respect and love. This restarts the thinking mind. {{t:00:22:46}}

6. The 10th house opening: Saturn link and the scale remedy {{t:00:28:34}}

  • The 10th is Capricorn, Saturn's sign. Rahu is Saturn's shadow agent, which gives a very dark combination: Saturn is darkness and the Sun's son, and Rahu is the one with the strength to blacken the Sun. {{t:00:28:34}}
  • The cobra image. The snake's mouth is Rahu. The jewel on the hood is the witness-mind Rahu, the giver, who keeps giving until money piles up. The speaker links this to Venus, guru of the demons, "the blind generous one". {{t:00:29:06}}
  • Boss effect. A strong 10th house Rahu makes you act like the boss even inside a job, but you must still keep a boss above you. {{t:00:30:39}}

The scale (tarazu) remedy: balancing Mercury and Venus {{t:00:29:06}}

A balance scale has two pans, Mercury (intellect) and Venus (enjoyment). Keep a scale at home and watch which pan hangs lower.

  • Pan with Venus lower: more enjoyment, less intellect.
  • Pan with Mercury lower: more intellect, less enjoyment.
  • The speaker's reading of the world: the very intelligent end up making others rich, and heavy enjoyers end up hiring an intelligent person to serve them. An owner needs to be the enjoyer, a worker the intelligent one.
  • Rahu's job is to keep both pans level. When they are level, both Rahu and Ketu are set. When Rahu goes up, Ketu goes down, and the other way round.
  • The method for setting Rahu is to use Ketu, and for setting Ketu to use Rahu. Both are demons, so they are balanced against each other. {{t:00:30:08}}
