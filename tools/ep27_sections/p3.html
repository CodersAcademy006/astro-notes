11. More Rahu combinations {{t:01:02:08}}

Rahu with several planets {{t:01:02:39}}

  • If Rahu sits with 2 or 3 planets it takes the aspects and power of all of them and drains them. {{t:01:02:39}}
  • Jupiter and Mars are "luck-type" planets, and Rahu cannot kill them. It beats the Sun, the Moon and, in part, Rahu itself. Saturn can fight it, but Rahu eats the Sun outright. {{t:01:03:11}}
  • Why this helps. Where the Sun is damaging your life (no fame, no bond with the father), Rahu is needed. {{t:01:03:43}}
  • Remedy the speaker gives for a bad Sun-Rahu situation: gift beer. {{t:01:03:43}}

The speaker states this directly. Treat it as his teaching, not a general recommendation.

Rahu + Mercury {{t:01:03:43}}

Both belong to the throat portal. Described as "an excellent combination", whether Mercury is debilitated or exalted.

Problem (if Mercury is afflicted)

A superb conspirator. Can plan a coup, train terrorists, work in an agency such as the CBI, or influence anyone by speech and corrupt their mind. "Can even cut a diamond." {{t:01:04:14}}

How it works

Mercury is intellect and sky, Rahu draws its power. With Mercury set well, the speaker says a daughter-in-law becomes her mother-in-law's favourite, because she speaks with a magical sweetness. {{t:01:04:45}}

Remedies

  • Keep Mercury strong and not negative. {{t:01:04:45}}
  • Nose piercing for girls. The speaker links the nose to Mercury, and says the saying "put a rein on the nose" means the girl should not get angry. {{t:01:05:16}}
  • Stay quiet and respectful before the four key people in the house (mother-in-law, father-in-law, husband, one more elder). They will then back her against the world. {{t:01:05:46}}

The speaker says the same applies to men: keep the mouth shut, be calm, and you become dear to all. Rahu + Mercury + Sun together is a "very good yoga" where the Sun enjoys itself with Mercury. {{t:01:06:19}}

Rahu in the 6th house: the enemy-killer {{t:01:06:19}}

  • The speaker calls Rahu here a killer. It pulls enemies in and destroys them, even when it is not exalted. Do not take such a person on. {{t:01:06:19}}
  • Forms the shatru-hanta yoga. New enemies keep arriving and keep getting finished. {{t:01:06:52}}
  • It wins by cleverness, not fighting. His examples are Chanakya and Krishna. Today's old world leaders do not fight themselves either. {{t:01:06:52}}
  • Advice to women: be a copy of the sweet face, keep the beak shut, and you will be everyone's favourite. {{t:01:07:22}}

Rahu + Jupiter ("guru chandal") {{t:01:07:56}}

The speaker rejects the fear around guru chandal yoga. He says it is used to defame people, citing how Osho got a bad name from one title ("Sambhog se Samadhi"). {{t:01:07:56}}

  • Rahu simply drains Jupiter's power. If Jupiter is bad, that helps. If it is good, the person loses some grace. {{t:01:08:28}}
  • Speaker's controversial point. A man with exalted Jupiter, or a girl with exalted Venus, can see the household destroyed. These planets are so strong they need a little pressing down. {{t:01:08:58}}
  • Jupiter is a "vacuum cleaner in space". It never lets negative things in and is the source of gravity. Even debilitated, it does not kill its own house. {{t:01:08:58}}
  • In the 10th, Jupiter can cause quarrels between parents and a long gap between the deaths of grandparents. If Rahu joins, it pulls Jupiter's knowledge, making a better speaker. {{t:01:09:30}}
  • The one problem is that Rahu breaks the "gravity" of a good Jupiter. {{t:01:10:32}}

The gold example: how Rahu panic works {{t:01:10:32}}

Fifteen people tell you gold is rising. You call your uncle, sell property, borrow from a friend and buy a pile of gold. Then the price falls 100. You freeze. Four days later your head is still stuck on the numbers: down 1 lakh, 2 lakh, the house will be sold. Then you sell in a rush, miss the next rise and cry again. Every decision is taken out of panic and not out of knowledge. That, says the speaker, is the Rahu loop. {{t:01:11:04}}

Problem

Panic decisions, repeated thought loops, quarrels over small things.

Cause

Rahu with no body, running on fear and borrowed opinion.

Remedy

Take a guru, someone who gives advice without fear. Your father (Sun) holds you back to protect you, and your mother (Moon) holds you back from fear, but a guru holds you back without fear. Listen to the guru. {{t:01:12:38}}

In small disputes (a maid asking for a saree, a child talking back) pay the small price, thank them and move on. Let the other person weep about their own Rahu. {{t:01:12:06}}

A note for astrologers: where is the planet actually working? {{t:01:13:39}}

  • Two planets that seem together may do their work in different houses. Look at where each planet really acts before predicting. That is why predictions fail when the chart is unclear. {{t:01:13:39}}
  • His comparison: like a doctor with a larger hospital, who needs more tests. Depth in reading placement makes predictions "go like a rocket". {{t:01:14:11}}
  • A six-month course and a certificate do not make an astrologer. Judge by knowledge. {{t:01:15:43}}
  • Miserliness: a bad 11th house gives a miser, a good one gives a generous person. {{t:01:16:14}}

12. The main remedy method: daan (donation) {{t:01:17:16}}

This is the speaker's central practical method for Rahu. It is the most useful part of the video.

The rule

  • Each type of giving "places" the planet in a house of your chart. Giving in a temple, gurudwara or religious place sends the planet to the 9th house. Giving to unknown people goes to other houses. Giving in hospitals, to enemies or in court goes to yet other houses. {{t:01:17:16}}
  • To settle a planet, give things belonging to that same planet. Quantity can vary; matching your body weight is the usual measure. {{t:01:17:47}}
  • Give with your own hand the item of the planet that is troubling you. Never donate the item of a good planet (for example, almonds). Even if you must not give, convert it. {{t:01:19:51}}
  • Anonymous giving works best. The person who gives without a name and without a receipt gets it back. Everything else is "a Crocin tablet", a temporary pain relief. {{t:01:20:22}}

Barley (jau) in water: the universal Rahu remedy {{t:01:17:47}}

StepDetail
Why barleyBarley is the husk covering the wheat, which is the Sun. So barley covering the Sun is Rahu. {{t:01:17:47}}
WhenSerious illness, mental illness, a body or bone problem, helplessness in life, or a feverish patient. The speaker says to give the medicine as usual, as this is support and not a replacement. {{t:01:18:18}}
How muchThe patient's own body weight in barley. If that is not possible, 4 kg up to 12 kg. These figures are indicative only. {{t:01:18:49}}
HowThe patient touches the barley, then it is poured into flowing water. Moving water stands for the 4th house, the Moon. Rahu folds its hands before the Moon, so it cools by itself. {{t:01:18:18}}
AlternativeSoak 12 kg of barley in water and feed it to birds and animals, or to strangers. Humans do not eat barley, so birds and animals take it. If given as flour, it becomes the Sun again. {{t:01:18:49}}

Beer (alcohol) donation {{t:01:19:19}}

  • Give beer to unknown people when Rahu is torturing you with disease of the body or mind. The speaker places this in the 12th house. {{t:01:19:19}}
  • Throwing beer into a river or open place is a 7th house placement. {{t:01:19:19}}
  • Never in a religious place. Rahu entering Jupiter's place (temple) or the 5th house of learning along with Sun and Jupiter gives ruin. Dharma needs its dignity. {{t:01:19:19}}

The speaker is aware this sounds tamasic and says so. Whether to follow it is a personal and legal decision. Many followers use other Rahu items instead (see the remedy table).

The speaker says Ketu is the one who brings back the blessings of this giving, and promises to cover it in the Ketu class. {{t:01:20:22}}

13. Rahu with Venus, Saturn and the outer planets {{t:01:20:52}}

Rahu + Venus {{t:01:20:52}}

Problem

Excess in everything. Not satisfied with one car, one house, one perfume, wants 40. Collecting 150 bottles of different brands, 135 shades of pink sarees. Becomes rich but multiplies every desire. Mentally characterless, very flirtatious, big on talk. {{t:01:21:23}}

Cause

Venus (enjoyment) is given Rahu's head, with no body to actually enjoy, so the mind runs wild and multiplies. {{t:01:20:52}}

Remedy

Do more Venus remedies (see the cow, curd and milk remedies above) and practise limits. Do not let the mind multiply desires. {{t:01:21:55}}

Rahu + Saturn {{t:01:22:27}}

  • The speaker calls this the "perfect snake" and says there is nothing better. It can make iron never rust, or destroy iron entirely. {{t:01:22:27}}
  • Saturn, the giver, makes you earn through the brain and stop physical labour. The lazier, easier, sharper-tongued and ruder the person, the faster the rise. Such people sit in high posts and subordinates call them awful. {{t:01:22:57}}
  • Helplessness: you can only hear, see and speak, you cannot do. So turn it into strength. Do not rush to "do". {{t:01:23:27}}
  • Remedy: read the Gita, or keep Krishna as your charioteer. Say "I will only shoot the arrows, Krishna, drive the chariot." Work by intelligence, not by labour. {{t:01:23:27}}

Rahu + Uranus (Indra) {{t:01:23:59}}

  • Uranus stays 7 years in a sign. The person is fussy, high-flying, talks big, but does outstanding work. Gives lavish gifts. {{t:01:23:59}}
  • If Uranus is in sign 6 (Rahu's house), gifts arrive at home without asking. "You think of two boxes of sweets and three arrive by evening." {{t:01:24:30}}
  • This needs Jupiter's grace, the Sun giving light and a calm Moon. Then Rahu does the work for you, just from your thinking. {{t:01:25:01}}
  • Weakness: selfish, will do anything for personal gain, a gift from such a person means they will want something back. Typical brokers, mediators and big communicators. {{t:01:25:31}}
  • Timing tip. When transit Rahu passes the sign where your natal Uranus sits (about 1.5 years), you meet high-level people and your life takes off. {{t:01:25:31}}

Rahu + Neptune (Varuna) {{t:01:26:03}}

  • Neptune stays about 14 years in a sign. With Rahu it is either a great giver or a great curser, either hell or boon, without limit. {{t:01:26:03}}
  • Extremes in everything: may talk about the Gita and adult films, drink and dharma together. {{t:01:26:34}}
  • Imagination plus Rahu's precision can produce a writer, for example a crime novel, or a book on terrorists or knowledge, depending on the sign. When transit Rahu crosses its sign, life expands greatly. {{t:01:27:06}}

Rahu + Pluto (Yamraj) {{t:01:27:06}}

  • Pluto stays 21 years in a sign. Rahu with Pluto wipes out enemies, ignores right and wrong and takes the shortest path to success. {{t:01:27:06}}
  • People like him: even when his words are twisted, "the man is right". He can rouse a crowd to war, to die, to commit suicide, or to follow a leader. Whether it goes to dharma or destruction depends on the placement. {{t:01:27:39}}
  • With Uranus and Pluto, Rahu can turn poisonous: kill by poison or cure by poison. With Neptune, cure through addictive medicines, painkillers, vitamins, even drugs. Holds an "anti-therapy" for each situation. {{t:01:28:09}}

14. Kaal Sarp yoga {{t:01:29:13}}

  • Condition. Rahu and Ketu sit opposite each other in the chart, and all the other planets lie on one side of that line. {{t:01:29:13}}
  • Check. Rahu and Ketu must be alone. If Rahu has Mars or any planet with it, it is not Kaal Sarp. {{t:01:29:43}}
  • Ekavali yoga. If a planet or two have slipped to the other side, that is called ekavali yoga, and not Kaal Sarp. {{t:01:30:13}}
