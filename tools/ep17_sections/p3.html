Problem

No tears even when you want to cry. You feel the water inside has dried up. {{t:00:51:24}}

White blood cell problems, allergies, lactose intolerance, food allergies and other water-related trouble all trace to a bad Uranus. {{t:00:51:24}}

Linking allergies, white blood cell problems, lactose intolerance, skin and sexual function to a planet is the speaker's claim, not medical advice. Stones are his suggestion and are not endorsed here. Get medical care for any of these.
