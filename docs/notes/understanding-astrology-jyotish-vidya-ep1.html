The talk in brief

  1. Astrology is true, astrologers can be false. Doubt one doctor, not medicine. The aim is grace, not becoming an astrologer.
  2. About 12 percent of life is fixed (prarabdha). The rest follows your commands and reactions. To end old karma, do not react.
  3. Ask big and ask clean. No negatives in a wish. The planets deliver the size of your first ask.
  4. The body is a set of planet portals. Mars at the navel, Jupiter at the liver, Saturn at the bones. Pain marks the portal that is off.
  5. Twelve signs, twelve rulers. Rahu, Ketu, Uranus, Neptune and Pluto each take a sign.
  6. Neptune (Varuna) grace comes first. Set the west of the home. Without grace every other tool misses.
  7. Do not build a temple inside the home. Keep the deity your chart supports. God is neutral, karma is yours.
