The talk in brief

  1. Marks can fall after age 10 for planetary reasons. A combust or retrograde Mercury or Jupiter is his usual explanation, not laziness.
  2. Do not punish a struggling child. A rude tone, refusal to listen, mood swings or superstition are read as planetary faults, not character.
  3. A failed exam can be the cheaper outcome. A lost fee or a repeated year is treated as a cheaper payment than a health problem later.
  4. Match the deity to the chart. The commonly named deity is not always the right one for a specific child's placement.
  5. Exam-day colours and threads are specific, not universal. What helps one child's chart can hurt another's; this page keeps his examples as illustrations only.
  6. No single chart is read alone. Near a sign's edge, the chalit, nirayana and KP charts can each shift a planet into a different house.
  7. Gemstones are a one-time investment. A well-fitted stone is treated as good for about a hundred years, not replaced every few.
