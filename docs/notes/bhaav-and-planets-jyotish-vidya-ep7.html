  2. You rise to a written level. Luck lifts you about as far as you were set to go. Awareness decides how well you use it.
  3. The 12 houses are fixed. Signs and planets move through them, but the first house is always you and the seventh always the partner.

  6. Ancestors before God. If the fifth house is disturbed, settle the ancestors first, with love for children and rites.
  7. Give by the planet. Cash can backfire if the sign is Mercury. Service and items fit more charts.
