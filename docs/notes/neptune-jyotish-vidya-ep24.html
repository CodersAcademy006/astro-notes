The talk in brief

  1. Neptune is the planet of grace. The sea gives intuition, treasure and manifestation, and it also takes. Which one you get depends on your inner state.
  2. Where it sits, it is limitless and false. That house is vast but unreal: the result is hard to see, so do not chase understanding.
  3. Do not beg, forgive. Asking once and letting go works. Grudges bring the lower sea, and forgiving brings the upper.
  4. Sign type matters. Movable, fixed and dual signs set how Neptune acts. It stays about 14 years in one sign.
  5. Each house has its own pattern. From low self-worth in the 1st to money coming through expenses in the 12th.
  6. Do not pre-live results. Rehearsing a wish or a fear cancels it. Think once and drop it.
  7. Remedies come next class. Only the aquamarine and a few habits are given here. Most remedies are deferred.
