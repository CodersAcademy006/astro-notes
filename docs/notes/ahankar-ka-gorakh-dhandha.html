One-line summary

Ego (ahankar) is a fast-brain state (beta and gamma) triggered by a fixed-fate programme the speaker calls Munna. It produces anger, comparison, desire and relationship fights, which together make the gorakh dhandha, a tangle that no longer makes sense. The way out: repay the five debts (rin), stay content (tript), do your karma and hand the result to the Divine, command old memories to stay in the past, and switch on love first instead of fighting the ego.
