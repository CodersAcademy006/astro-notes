The talk in brief

  1. Ketu is a body with no head. It feels and acts but cannot think. Fish, sword, flag and shield show up wherever it sits.
  2. Ketu cuts, then frees. Its "moksha" arrives after real fulfilment, and often after being beaten into wisdom.
  3. Instant karma. Ketu looks at Saturn about 70 to 80 percent of the time. Harm wished on others returns fast, so forgive.
  4. The 12th house multiplies what you give. Give to strangers, respect women, your guru and grandparents.
  5. Serve the dog, cow and crow. A toy dog works if you cannot keep one. Pierce the ears for lower-body trouble.
  6. Cat's eye is a hard stone. Finger, metal and chart checks matter. Gomed is easier. Many cases need no stone.
  7. The west-south-west of the home is Ketu's zone. Keep it clean, with no cutting tools or violent images.
